# Academic Practice Nuggets: Videos for Social Sciences

These videos were created as part of the **„Innovative Digital Teaching at University of Mannheim (InnoMA)“** project, funded by the Stiftung Innovation in der Hochschullehre.

**Goal:** The primary objective was to address heterogeneity among students, especially exchange students, regarding advanced academic practices in the social sciences.

**Usage:** These videos are available for use in your classes. They can be used either selected or over the course of ten seminar weeks. Students are advised to watch the video as part of seminar preparation and apply the content to the weekly readings.

### Video Topics

The resource comprises 10 distinct modules covering essential academic skills:

1. Time efficient and effective reading
1. Reflect critically
1. Scientific Critique
1. Causality
1. Theory
1. Ethical Considerations
1. Theory Argumentation
1. Design Thinking
1. Open Science
1. How to critique
