# Data and Web Science Group

Welcome to the webpages of the **Data and Web Science Group**. The group conducts research and offers teaching in the areas of data analytics, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and data integration. The group comprises 7 professors and approximately 35 researchers and supporting staff members.

## 🔬 Research Focus

The Data and Web Science Group focuses on developing methods for managing, integrating, and mining large amounts of heterogeneous information within both enterprise and open Web contexts.

**Research Activities:**

- The group is involved in several [national and international projects](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/) and actively cooperates with partners from industry, research institutes, and other universities.
- Details on research outcomes are published in conference and journal papers.
- Resources, including open-license datasets, open-source software packages, and benchmarks, are available on the [resources page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/resources/).

**Research Focus Groups:**
The group is structured around several specialized focus areas:

- [Web-based Systems](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/web-based-systems-prof-bizer/) (Prof. Bizer)
- [Data Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/) (Prof. Gemulla)
- [Responsible AI and Digital Health](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-philipp-kellmeyer/) (Prof. Kellmeyer)
- [Machine Learning](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/) (Prof. Margret Keuper)
- [Web Data Mining](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/web-data-mining-prof-paulheim/) (Prof. Paulheim)
- [Natural Language Processing](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-prof-ponzetto/) (Prof. Ponzetto)
- [Process Analysis & Management](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-daniel-schuster/) (Prof. Schuster)

For the latest updates from individual group members, please check the [X twitter feed](https://twitter.com/dwsunima).

## 🎓 Teaching and Education

The group offers comprehensive educational opportunities for bachelor and master students.

- **Courses:** Regular courses are available covering topics such as data mining, data integration, information retrieval, natural language processing, knowledge engineering, and knowledge management.
- **Theses & PhD:** Students have the opportunity to write bachelor or master theses related to the group's research topics, and excellent alumni can pursue a PhD within the group.
- **Research Assistants:** The group is currently seeking motivated students to work as research assistants.

## 🚀 Applied Projects and Industry Cooperation

Beyond fundamental research, the group emphasizes applied research and technology transfer, maintaining close cooperation with industry partners. Key collaborations include:

- The [Mannheim Center for Data Science](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/datascience/) (MCDS)
- The [Institute for Enterprise Systems](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/ines/) (InES)

For details on course offerings, please visit the [course details page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/teaching/course-details/).
