# Data and Web Science Group

The Data and Web Science Group conducts research and offers teaching in advanced areas including data analytics, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and data integration. The group comprises 7 professors and approximately 35 researchers and supporting staff members.

## 🔬 Forschungsschwerpunkte (Research Focus)

The core research activity involves developing methods for managing, integrating, and mining large volumes of heterogeneous information within both enterprise and open Web contexts.

### Forschungsbereiche (Focus Groups)

The group is structured around several specialized focus areas, each led by an expert professor:

- **Web-based Systems:** [Prof. Bizer](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/web-based-systems-prof-bizer/)
- **Data Analytics:** [Prof. Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/)
- **Responsible AI and Digital Health:** [Prof. Kellmeyer](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-philipp-kellmeyer/)

### Forschungsergebnisse und Ressourcen

The group is actively involved in [national and international projects](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/) and collaborates with industry partners, research institutes, and other universities.

- **Publikationen:** Research results are published in conference and journal papers.
- **Ressourcen:** Open-license datasets, open-source software packages, and benchmarks are available on the [resources page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/resources/).

## 🎓 Lehre und Studium (Teaching)

We offer regular courses for bachelor and master students covering topics such as data mining, data integration, information retrieval, natural language processing, knowledge engineering, and knowledge management.

- **Thesis:** Students have the opportunity to write bachelor or master theses related to the group's research topics.
- **Promotion:** Excellent alumni can continue their studies at our group to achieve a PhD.
- **Assistenz:** We are currently seeking motivated students to work as research assistants.
- [Details on courses](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/teaching/course-details/)
