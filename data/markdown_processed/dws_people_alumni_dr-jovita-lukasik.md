# Jovita Lukasik

**Researcher**
University of Mannheim
B6, 26, Room B 003D-68159 Mannheim
**Email:** [jovita](mailto:jovita@informatik.uni-mannheim.de)

Jovita Lukasik is a Ph.D. candidate focusing on Computer Vision within the [Data and Web Science Group](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/home/). Her research is also connected to the [Computer Vision and Machine Learning Group at the Max-Planck-Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/computer-vision-and-machine-learning/people/jovita-lukasik). She is supervised by [Prof. Dr.-Ing. Margret Keuper](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/).

**Activities & Interests:**

- Co-organizing a series of [virtual seminars on AutoML](https://automl-seminars.github.io/).
- Was part of the organization team for the second NAS workshop at ICLR 2021.
- Portfolio: [https://jovitalukasik.github.io/](https://jovitalukasik.github.io/)
