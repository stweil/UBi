## Academic Profile

Fabian David Schmidt is a Ph.D. candidate within the **Chair of Text Analytics for Interdisciplinary Research** ([https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-prof-ponzetto/](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-prof-ponzetto/)). This chair is part of the **Data and Web Science Group** ([https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/home/](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/home/)) at the [University of Mannheim](http://www.uni-mannheim.de/1/english/). He is supervised by [Prof. Dr. Goran Glavaš](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-goran-glavas/).

## Projects

**EU In Action:**
This project applies state-of-the-art natural language processing methods to political texts. The goal is to determine the drivers and explain the mechanics of legislative changes at the European level, categorized by policy area. This is conducted in cooperation with the University of Leiden and the University of Strathclyde.
*Source: [EU In Action:](https://www.wim.uni-mannheim.de/news/prof-goran-glavas-erhaelt-eu-foerdermittel-fuer-interdisziplinaeres-forschungsprojekt/)*
