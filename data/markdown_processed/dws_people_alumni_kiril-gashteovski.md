# Kiril Gashteovski

**Researcher**
[kiril.gashteovski@gmail.com](mailto:kiril.gashteovski@gmail.com)

## Academic Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [Chair of Practical Computer Science I: Data Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/). This chair is part of the [Data and Web Science Group](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/home/) at the [University of Mannheim](http://www.uni-mannheim.de/1/english/). My research is supervised by [Prof. Dr. Rainer Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-rainer-gemulla/).

## Professional Experience

- **2020 – Present:** Research Scientist at [NEC Laboratories Europe GmbH](https://www.nec.com/en/global/rd/labs/europe/index.html) in Heidelberg, Germany.
- **2015 – 2020:** PhD Researcher / Teaching Assistant at the [University of Mannheim](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/) in Mannheim, Germany.
- **2015:** Data Scientist at [CupoNation GmbH](https://www.cuponation.com/) in Munich, Germany.
- **2012 – 2015:** Founder / Data Scientist at Cyclope Inc in Skopje, North Macedonia.
- **2014:** R&D Consultant in Data Mining at [OmegaCube](https://www.linkedin.com/company/omega-cube/about/) in Marseille, France.
- **2014:** R&D Developer in Data Mining at [INRIA](https://www.inria.fr/en/) / [EDF](https://www.edf.fr/) in Rennes, France / Paris, France.
- **2012 – 2013:** Data Scientist at [OmegaCube](https://www.linkedin.com/company/omega-cube/about/) in Marseille, France.
- **2010 – 2011:** Research Assistant / Software Developer at [South – West University “Neofit Rilski”](https://www.swu.bg/?lang=en) in Blagoevgrad, Bulgaria.
