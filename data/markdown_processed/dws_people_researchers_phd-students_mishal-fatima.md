## Research Profile

Mishal Fatima is a Ph.D. candidate in the Machine Learning group, supervised by [Prof. Margret Keuper](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/).

**Research Focus:**
The primary research interest lies in deep learning for practically efficient computer vision tasks. The work explores novel model architectures and data-driven approaches aimed at improving the robustness and efficiency of vision systems, specifically focusing on co-designing parameters between sensor systems and neural networks.

**Current Project:**
The research is conducted within the inter-disciplinary project group, [Learning to Sense](https://www.learning2sense.de/), which investigates how sensor systems can be co-designed with neural networks.
