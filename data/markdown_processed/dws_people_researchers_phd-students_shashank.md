### 🔬 Research Focus

Shashank's research centers on analyzing and improving the **adversarial and Out-of-Distribution (OOD) robustness** of deep learning methods, particularly in computer vision.

**Key Research Interests:**

- Architectural Design Choices for OOD Robustness and Adversarial Robustness of Vision Models.
- Pixel-wise prediction tasks.
- Neural Architecture Search (NAS).
- Sensor and sensor-related parameters and signal processing impact on model reliability.

### 📝 Profile Summary

Shashank Agnihotri is a Ph.D. student in the Machine Learning Group at the University of Mannheim, supervised by Prof. Dr.-Ing Margret Keuper.

**Experience Highlights:**

- Previously served as a Research Assistant in the Machine Learning Group at Albert-Ludwigs Universität Freiburg, focusing on Neural Architecture Search (NAS) and the robustness of one-shot NAS methods, working with Prof. Dr. Frank Hutter and Dr. Mahmoud Safari.
- He has a strong publication record at top-tier venues including **ICML, ECCV, ICCV, NeurIPS, TMLR**, and ICCP.
- He has contributed to the organization of the 45th DAGM German Conference on Pattern Recognition (GCPR) 2023 and served as the Publicity Chair for GCPR 2025.
