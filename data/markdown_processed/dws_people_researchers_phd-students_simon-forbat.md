## Academic Context

I am a researcher affiliated with the [Chair of Data Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/), which is part of the [Data and Web Science Group](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/home/) at the [University of Mannheim](http://www.uni-mannheim.de/1/english/). I am supervised by [Prof. Dr. Rainer Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-rainer-gemulla/).

## Research Interests

My research focuses on advanced topics in machine learning and data analysis, including:

- Data augmentation for graph classification & applications of graph edit distance for graph learning.
- Tabular foundation models and their applications to relational deep learning.

**Research Assistants:**
The chair actively seeks highly motivated research assistants. Candidates with a strong background in deep learning and interest in the above domains are encouraged to submit their CV and a brief statement of interest.
