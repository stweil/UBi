# Research Overview: Data and Web Science Group

The central challenge of the modern information society is leveraging the massive volume of data available across the Web and enterprise contexts. The **Data and Web Science Group** conducts research on advanced methods for managing, integrating, and mining large amounts of heterogeneous information to support companies, public institutions, and individuals.

Our research portfolio is structured around several specialized, yet interconnected, focus areas:

## Core Research Pillars

### Web-based Systems (Prof. Bizer)

This area explores technical and empirical questions concerning the development of global, decentralized information environments. Our focus is on the evolution of the World Wide Web from a document publication medium into a global dataspace. We monitor the adoption of Semantic Markup and Linked Data technologies, focusing technically on integrating data from numerous Web sources. Key topics include information extraction, identity resolution, schema matching, data fusion, and building large-scale knowledge bases (e.g., DBpedia).
[Learn more about Web-based Systems](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/web-based-systems-prof-bizer/)

### Data Analytics (Prof. Gemulla)

Our research centers on developing systems and methods for analyzing and learning from large datasets, with practical applications. Key research areas include:

- Machine learning applied to semi-structured and structured data.
- Combining unstructured and structured knowledge sources.
- Representation learning for multi-relational graphs.
- Developing efficient and scalable systems for data-intensive processing.
  [Learn more about Data Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/)

### Web Data Mining (Prof. Paulheim)

This group researches methods for mining knowledge from vast amounts of structured and unstructured data found on the Web. To handle the scale, heterogeneity, and dynamics of web data, our focus is on supervised or unsupervised methods that combine logical and statistical inference. We utilize existing and automatically acquired knowledge to facilitate data integration, enrichment, cleansing, and to bootstrap the overall data mining process.
[Learn more about Web Data Mining](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/web-data-mining-prof-paulheim/)

### Natural Language Processing (Prof. Ponzetto)

The NLP and IR group researches knowledge acquisition and Natural Language Processing (NLP) methods, applying them to empirical research in Computational Social Sciences and Digital Humanities. We investigate a wide range of text understanding techniques—from representation learning and distributional semantics to symbolic, entity-based approaches leveraging wide-coverage knowledge graphs. Applications include computational semantics, multilinguality, information retrieval, and multimodal NLP.
[Learn more about NLP and IR](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/natural-language-processing-and-information-retrieval-prof-ponzetto/)

### Artificial Intelligence (Prof. Stuckenschmidt)

An internationally visible group with approximately 25 researchers and PhD students, we develop and improve AI methods in:

- Machine Learning from Structured Data.
- Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning and Learning.
- Fairness and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI).

We actively cooperate with industry partners (like SAP) and regional organizations, applying our expertise to real-world problems such as Business Process Analysis, Data-Driven Demand Forecasting, Human Activity Recognition, and Smart City solutions.
[Learn more about AI](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/artificial-intelligence-prof-stuckenschmidt/)

### Process Analytics (Prof. Van der Aa)

This group researches the interplay between business processes and information systems, focusing on automated methods for data-driven process analysis. Our research encompasses process analysis, process mining, and robotic process automation (RPA). Primary interests include conformance checking, natural language processing, event abstraction, data privacy, and data uncertainty.
[Learn more about Process Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/process-analytics-prof-van-der-aa/)

### Responsible AI & Digital Health (Jun.-Prof. Dr. Kellmeyer)

Addressing the accelerating innovation of AI, this research program focuses on balancing normative, technical, and societal requirements for effective regulation. It combines conceptual foundations with empirical mixed-methods research, with a specific focus on AI systems within the context of digital health and human-technology interaction.
[Learn more about Responsible AI & Digital Health](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-philipp-kellmeyer/)

### Computer Vision & Machine Learning (Prof. Keuper)

Our research interests lie in Computer Vision and Machine Learning, applied across diverse fields. Key technical areas include:

- Improving Model Robustness and Generalization Ability.
- Neural Architecture Search.
- Grouping Problems (e.g., Image and Motion Segmentation, Multiple Object Tracking).
- Motion Estimation and Segmentation.
- Image Analysis and Image Generation (including Deep Fake Detection).
  [Learn more about CV & ML](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/computer-vision-machine-learning-prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/)
