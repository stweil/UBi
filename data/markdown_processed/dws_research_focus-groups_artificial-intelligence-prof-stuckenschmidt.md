### Course Competencies (Summary)

The group covers advanced topics in modeling, decision support, and AI theory:

- **Modeling & Process:** Description of domains and processes of simple to medium complexity using standard languages (UML, BPMN) and tools.
- **Advanced Theory:** Decision Theory, Decision- and Business Rules, Planning Methods and Algorithms, Probabilistic Graphical Models, and Game Theory.
- **AI Core:** Search and Problem Solving (Block I-IV), Constraints, Propositional Logic, and Planning as a Search/Satisfiability Problem.
- **Skills:** Teamwork, presentation skills, and understanding complex interdisciplinary relationships.
