# Process Analytics Group Research Focus

The Process Analytics group at DWS conducts research on the interplay of business processes and information systems. The core focus is the development of automated methods for data-driven process analysis.

**Key Research Areas:**
This research encompasses several specialized fields, including:

- Process analysis
- Process mining
- Robotic process automation (RPA)

**Specific Interests:**
Primary research interests include, but are not limited to:

- Conformance checking
- Natural language processing
- Event abstraction
- Data privacy
- Data uncertainty

**Group Background:**
The Process Analytics group was founded in April 2020. It is based on the Junior Professorship in Artificial Intelligence Methods held by Prof. Van der Aa, supported by the [Künstliche Intelligenz Baden-Württemberg](https://www.digital-bw.de/-/kunstliche-intelligenz-baden-wurttemberg) initiative.
