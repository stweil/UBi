# Human Activity Recognition (HAR) Project

This page provides tools, datasets, and results related to the automatic detection of human activity recognition (e.g., walking) and activities of daily living (e.g., watering plants).

## Applications

The primary goal is to develop robust activity recognition methods utilizing mobile device sensors to achieve high-quality results in real-world settings. This section provides developed applications, including source code and documentation. We offer a comprehensive data collector app and are currently developing a HAR app for everyday life use.

## Data Sets

The available dataset covers sensor data for various activities performed by fifteen subjects. The recorded data includes acceleration, GPS, gyroscope, light, magnetic field, and sound level measurements. Activities covered include:

- Climbing stairs (up and down)
- Jumping
- Lying
- Standing
- Sitting
- Running/Jogging
- Walking

For each activity, on-body positions were simultaneously recorded for the chest, forearm, head, shin, thigh, upper arm, and waist.

## Frameworks

We have developed useful frameworks specifically for processing the recorded sensor data. These frameworks are available for free use and can be modified or enhanced without further requests. Our current focus is on deriving useful information from accelerometer data, specifically by segmenting the data stream and computing meaningful features.
