# Datasets for Activity Recognition

This page provides access to various datasets used for research in activity recognition.

## 1. RealWorld (HAR) Dataset (2016)

[Link to Dataset](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/activity-recognition/dataset/dataset-realworld/)

- **Subjects:** 15
- **Sensors:** 6
- **Data Type:** Video recordings
- **Activities Covered:** 7 Body Positions (Walking, Running, Sitting, Standing, Lying, Stairs Up, Stairs Down, Jumping)

## 2. Daily Log (ADL) Dataset (2016)

[Link to Dataset](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/activity-recognition/dataset/dataset-daily-log/)

- **Subjects:** 7
- **Data Type:** Postures, Accelerometer data
- **Duration:** Two weeks
- **Activities Covered:** Includes activities such as Housework, Sport, Grooming, Relaxing, Sleeping, Eating, etc.

## 3. First-Person View (ADL) Dataset (2018)

[Link to Dataset](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/activity-recognition/dataset/dataset-first-person-view/)

- **Subjects:** 2
- **Data Type:** Vision/Inertial data (Interleaved)
- **Features:** Includes Bounding Boxes
- **Activities Covered:** Examples include Eat Banana, Prepare Bread, Take Medicine, Drinking, Wipe mouth, etc.
