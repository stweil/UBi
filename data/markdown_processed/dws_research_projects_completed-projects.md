# Completed Research Projects

This section details various research projects completed by the group, covering advanced topics in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Semantic Web technologies, and Digital Humanities.

## Artificial Intelligence & Knowledge Representation

### Teaming.AI

TEAMING.AI developed a software platform for human-AI teaming. The core technologies focus on the Knowledge Graph Engine, the TEAMING.AI Engine, and mechanisms for exploiting the underlying knowledge graph for constraint checking and recommendation mining in dynamic settings. The project conceptualized human-AI teaming by modeling interdependencies, skills, tasks, and workflows using a knowledge graph, complemented by rule-based and advanced relational machine learning techniques.

- **Duration:** 2021–2024
- **Funded by:** European Commission
- **Partners:** TU Dublin, WU Vienna, IDEKO, Profactor, Software Competence Center Hagenberg

### Kartellrechtskonforme KI (KarekoKI)

This project, implemented within the research program for public welfare-oriented AI, aims to develop a legal framework and technical strategies to prevent AI-based price fixing. Using a market simulation supported by AI-controlled agents and data from online shops, the research addresses competition law compliance in AI systems.

- **Duration:** 2020-2023
- **Budget:** 383,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** BW Stiftung

### Responsible News Recommender Systems (ReNewRS)

As part of the research program for responsible AI, ReNewRS investigated the impact of news recommender systems on social polarization, political participation, and prosocial behavior through experimental studies. The goal was to derive guidelines for news recommender systems that promote balanced opinion formation.

- **Duration:** 2020-2022
- **Funded by:** BW Stiftung
- **Partners:** Institute for Media and Communication Studies (University of Mannheim), FIZ Karlsruhe, KIT Karlsruhe

### Distributed Inference in Probabilistic Knowledge Base

This project addressed the computational challenge of probabilistic inference in knowledge bases. It involved developing and implementing a distributed sampling-based inference algorithm. The system processes a large set of statements with confidence values and existing background knowledge, outputting statements with *a-posteriori* probabilities using a two-process approach on the Hadoop platform.

- **Duration:** 2012 – 2015
- **Budget:** $60,000
- **Funded by:** Google Faculty Research Award
- **Partner:** University of Washington, Seattle

### Vision and language understanding beyond literal meaning

This RiSC-funded project investigated computational methods to understand language and image usages beyond literal meaning, specifically focusing on iconic images and metaphors in context. Collaboration with communication science experts informed the development of complementary theories for the models.

- **Duration:** 2015 – 2016
- **Budget:** 64,400 EUR
- **Funded by:** MWK Baden Württemberg

### GOLD – Generating Ontologies from Linked Data

The project developed novel methods for acquiring structured knowledge representations from existing web data. By applying logical and statistical relational learning to vast amounts of linked data, the goal was to bootstrap the realization of the semantic web, focusing on automatic ontology engineering support and knowledge base debugging.

- **Duration:** 2013 – 2015
- **Budget:** 218,400 EUR (Group share: 109,200 EUR)
- **Funded by:** German Research Foundation (DFG)
- **Partner:** University of Leipzig, Germany
- **Link:** [http://gold.linkeddata.org](http://gold.linkeddata.org)

### Deep semantic models for high-end NLP applications

This project focused on complementing ontological information from general-purpose knowledge graphs with distributional information. This combination enabled a deep understanding of text for complex NLP applications such as summarization and text re-use.

- **Duration:** 2015 – 2017
- **Budget:** 180,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** MWK Baden Württemberg

### Linguistic Aspects of Symbolic Knowledge Representation

Funded by a Margarete-von-Wrangell habilitation scholarship, this project concerned the development of logical and statistical methods for extracting, debugging, integrating, and querying Semantic Web data.

- **Duration:** 2009–2014
- **Budget:** 360,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** MWK Baden-Württemberg (via European Social Fund)

## Data Management & Mining

### Data Search for Data Mining (DS4DM)

The DS4DM project addressed the difficulty analysts face in finding and integrating all relevant data for mining projects across the web or corporate intranets. It extended the Rapdiminer data mining platform with dedicated data search and data integration features.

- **Duration:** 2015 – 2018
- **Budget:** 400,000 EUR (Mannheim share: 200,000 EUR)
- **Funded by:** Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF)
- **Partner:** Rapidminer GmbH

### Mine@LOD – Data Mining with Linked Open Data (DFG)

This project aimed to develop automated means to enhance data mining problems by incorporating background knowledge sourced from Linked Open Data.

- **Duration:** 2013 – 2016
- **Budget:** 264,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG)

### LOD2 – Creating Knowledge out of Interlinked Data

The LOD2 project developed tools and methodologies for exposing and managing large volumes of structured data on the Web. The methods were applied to e-government, media, and enterprise search use cases, focusing on interlinking, fusing Web data, and assessing data quality.

- **Duration:** 2010–2014
- **Budget:** 7 million € (Mannheim share: 570,000 €)
- **Funded by:** European Commission, FP7-ICT-2009-5 (IP Project)
- **Partners:** Universität Leipzig (Germany), CWI (Netherlands), DERI (Ireland), OpenLink Software (UK), Semantic Web Company (Austria), Exalead (France), Wolters Kluwer (Germany), Open Knowledge Foundation (UK)

### PlanetData

The Network of Excellence PlanetData established a European community for large-scale data management. Research focused on methods for self-descriptive data publication and data quality assessment.

- **Duration:** 2010–2014
- **Budget:** 2 million € (Mannheim share: 160,000 €)
- **Funded by:** European Commission, FP7-ICT-2009-5 (Network of Excellence)
- **Partners:** Universität Innsbruck (Austria), CWI (Holland), Institut Jozef Stefan (Slovenia), Karlsruher Institut für Technologie (Germany), Universidad Politecnica de Madrid (Spain)

### MappingAssistant – An intelligent tool for interactive diagnosis and correction of data integration rules

This project developed the conflict resolution framework MappingAssistant. It detects and corrects erroneous data integration rules using instances, providing a user-centric and interactive design suitable for non-technical users.

- **Duration:** 2010 – 2012
- **Budget:** 500,000 EUR (Group share: 170,000 EUR)
- **Funded by:** BMBF (KMU Innovative program)

## Semantic Web & Information Extraction

### SyKo²W² (Synthesis of Completion and Correction for Web Knowledge Graphs)

This project examined how completion and correction methods for knowledge graphs—previously studied in isolation—could be combined into efficient joint methods.

- **Duration:** 2015 – 2017
- **Budget:** 150,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** MWK Baden Württemberg

### Identifying Semantic Relations between Models at Different Levels of Granularity

This research addressed the limitation in model matching approaches that assume similar granularity. The project developed and evaluated methods applicable to matching, for example, a chain of activities from a source model to a single activity in a target model.

- **Duration:** 2014 – 2016
- **Budget:** 270,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG)

### ProMap – Probabilistic Mappings for the Semantic Web (DFG)

This project tackled the challenge of answering a single query using multiple knowledge resources described by ontologies. It focused on automatically creating probabilistic mapping rules to account for inherent uncertainties in the knowledge integration.

- **Duration:** 2009 – 2012
- **Budget:** ca. 260,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** DFG

### SEALS: Semantic Evaluation at Large Scale (EU FP7)

A platform was developed for running evaluations and campaigns for Semantic Web tools (reasoners, matching tools, ontology editors, etc.). The group contributed to the Ontology Alignment Evaluation Initiative, an annual campaign.

- **Duration:** 2009 – 2012
- **Budget:** 3,500,000 EUR (Group share: 150,000 EUR)
- **Funded by:** European Union
- **Partners:** International group of universities

### Robust and Scalable Reasoning for Information Extraction

This project dealt with extracting information from unstructured/semi-structured sources. It employed knowledge-based and probabilistic reasoning methods to prevent logical inconsistencies within the extracted model.

- **Duration:** 2009 – 2011
- **Budget:** ~100,000 EUR
- **Partners:** Research Seed capital des Ministerium für Wissenschaft, Forschung und Kunst

### Enhencing the accessability for large text collections using semantic web technologies

This project aimed for automatic subject cataloguing for economics documents using a fixed subject vocabulary adjusted to the economics domain.

- **Duration:** 2007 – 2009
- **Budget:** ca. 170,000 EUR
- **Sponsor:** Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft
- **Partner:** Universitätsbibliothek Mannheim, SyynX Solutions GmbH

## Digital Humanities & Social Sciences

### InFoLiS (Consolidated Project)

This project series focused on integrating research data and literature in the social sciences. The goal was to provide an integrated search algorithm capable of searching for studies, surveys, and datasets in parallel, addressing the limited support for data searching compared to literature searching.

- **Duration:** 2011–2013 (Follow-up to 2014–2016)
- **Budget:** 300,000 EUR (Group share: 75,000 EUR)
- **Funded by:** DFG (Projekt für wissenschaftliche Informationsversorgung)
- **Partners:** Leibniz Institut for Social Science (GESIS), University Library Mannheim

### Linking and Populating the Digital Humanities (DFG/NEH)

A cooperation with Indiana University, Bloomington, to develop an application connecting and enriching humanities data with the Semantic Web (Linked Data). The initial test case was the Encyclopedia of Philosophy.

- **Duration:** 2012 – 2014
- **Budget:** 200,000 EUR (Group share: 120,000 EUR)
- **Funded by:** NEH/DFG for Bilateral Digital Humanities
- **Partner:** Indiana University, Bloomington, USA

### DM2E – Digitized Manuscripts to Europeana

This EU-ICT-PSP project applied Linked Data principles to cultural heritage artifacts, building an interconnected data space around Europeana. The focus was on developing an interoperability infrastructure to convert diverse library metadata formats into the Europeana Data Model (EDM).

- **Duration:** 2012–2015
- **Budget:** 2.5 million € (Mannheim share: 280,000 €)
- **Funded by:** European Commission, CIP-ICT-PSP-BPN (Support Programme)
- **Partners:** Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (Germany), Österreichische Nationalbibliothek (Austria), National Technical University of Athens (Greece), ExLibris (Germany), Stiftung Preussischer Kulturbesitz (Germany)

## Industry & Applied Science

### Pick-By-Vision – Enhancing the Picking Process in the After-Sales-Logistic

In cooperation with Daimler AG, this project developed a pick-by-vision prototype. The system utilized an AR-data-glass (Vuzix Corporation) and applied computer vision/AR methodologies to assist navigation, orderlist work, and active picker connection, incorporating direct feedback to prevent picking errors.

- **Duration:** 2014–2015
- **Partners:** Daimler AG, GLC Germersheim

### Demand Forecasting in the Retail Industry

This project focused on data mining time series data for fast-moving consumer goods (e.g., vegetables). It investigated demand forecasting at various aggregation levels (intra-day, daily) and methods to integrate external influencing factors like weather and public holidays.

- **Duration:** 2015 – 2016
- **Funded by:** BMWE EXIST – Existenzgründungen aus der Wissenschaft
- **Partner:** OPAL – Operational Analytics GmbH

### Polytos – Develop smart labels based on organic electronics

Sponsored by BMBF, the project aimed to develop new materials, concepts, and processes for printed organic circuits with integrated sensors (measuring temperature, humidity, etc.). These systems were intended for use as intelligent, flexible transponders (smart labels) in the packaging industry.

- **Duration:** 2009 – 2013
- **Budget:** 40,000,000 EUR (Total)
- **Funded by:** SAP AG (Sub-contract)

### Development of a risk management tool for complex technical systems

The aim was to develop a control unit that autonomously detects critical conditions in vital services and initiates countermeasures to ensure infrastructure stability. This required merging system configuration data from a CMDB with live system data, and modeling component dependencies for each datacenter.

- **Duration:** 2013 – 2015
- **Budget:** 175,000€
- **Funded by:** German Federal Ministry of Economics and Technology (BMWI), Program SME (ZIM)
- **Partner:** Softplant GmbH, Munich

### Emmy Noether Programme

- **Duration:** 2006 – 2012
- **Budget:** 700,000 EUR
- **Funded by:** Partial funding of a junior research group by the DFG

### InFoLiS 2 – Integration of research data and literature in the Social Sciences

- **Duration:** 2014–2016
- **Funded by:** Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG)
- **Partners:** Leibniz Institute for Social Science (GESIS), Mannheim University Library
