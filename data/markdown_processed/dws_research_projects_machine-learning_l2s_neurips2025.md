# Workshop on Learning to Sense (L2S) at NeurIPS 2025

The L2S workshop explores the fundamental shift in computer vision, moving beyond traditional image processing pipelines designed for human perception. We focus on the **joint optimization of sensors and deep learning models** to create more robust, efficient, and scalable vision systems for real-world deployment.

## Workshop Overview and Focus Areas

Recent advances in deep learning models are powerful, but they are constrained by the input data format. Traditional Image Signal Processing (ISP) pipelines often discard raw sensor information valuable for machine perception. This workshop addresses this gap by focusing on end-to-end optimization of image acquisition and processing.

We concentrate on three key areas:

1. **Sensor Optimization**: Including low-bit data capture (e.g., 4-bit, 6-bit, 8-bit) and learning sensor layouts co-optimized with network parameters.
1. **Reimagining ISPs**: Shifting from fixed hardware pipelines to learnable, task-adaptive ISPs or direct RAW-to-task pipelines.
1. **Task-Driven Sensor Design**: Guiding image capture based on the needs of downstream models rather than human aesthetics, utilizing novel sensor configurations or color filter arrangements.

### Key Topics Covered

Experts will discuss the following advanced topics:

- **Sensor Optimization for Vision Tasks**: Joint optimization of sensor design (pixel layout, bit depth, filters) and model parameters.
- **Reimagining the ISP Pipeline**: Implementing learnable or optimized ISPs that align better with deep learning models, bypassing traditional steps.
- **Task-Driven Sensor Design**: Designing sensors specifically optimized for model tasks, maximizing information capture for the model.
- **Joint Optimization**: Exploring synergies by optimizing both sensor hardware and model architecture simultaneously.
- **Quantization of RAW Data**: Investigating bit-depth reduction for efficient storage and processing while maintaining model performance.
- **Benchmarks**: Introducing new datasets and metrics tailored for evaluating sensor-model systems.
- **Generalization**: Ensuring optimized systems remain robust across diverse real-world environments and lighting conditions.
- **Negative Results**: Discussion of failure cases is welcomed to advance community understanding.

## Call for Papers & Submission Details

We invite original contributions and position papers on the joint optimization of sensors and ML models.

**Submission Categories:**

- **Extended Abstracts**: Up to 4 pages (non-archival). Accepted papers are presented as posters or short talks.
- **Full Papers**: Up to 9 pages (excluding references) (archival). Selected papers are published in the PMLR NeurIPS Workshop Proceedings and presented as talks or spotlight posters.

**Formatting Guidelines:**

- All submissions must follow the official **NeurIPS style**.
- Submissions undergo **double-blind peer review** via OpenReview.
- Supplementary materials are optional and do not count toward the page limit.

**Important Dates (AoE):**

- **Submission Deadline**: August 22, 2025
- **Workshop Date**: December 6 or 7, 2025 (In-person in San Diego, CA)
