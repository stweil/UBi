# Learning to Sense (L2S)

The "Learning to Sense" project focuses on the critical intersection of **Machine Learning** and **Sensor System Development**, aiming for the joint optimization of both components.

## Project Overview and Funding

L2S was selected by the German Research Foundation (DFG) as one of eight dedicated research units in Germany. This unit conducts fundamental research in Artificial Intelligence alongside an interdisciplinary partner field: sensor system development.

## Research Focus

The core objective of the project is to develop methodologies for jointly optimizing sensor system designs and the neural networks that process the resulting data.

## Institutional Context

The research is situated at the **Center for Sensors Systems (ZESS)** at the [University of Siegen](https://www.uni-siegen.de/start/index.html.en?lang=en). The work builds upon ZESS's extensive history of over 30 years in fundamental and application-oriented, interdisciplinary research.

## Key Personnel

- **External Principal Investigator (PI):** Margret Keuper, Head of the Machine Learning Group at the [University of Mannheim](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/projects/machine-learning/learning-to-sense/).
