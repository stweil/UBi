# Responsible News Recommender Systems (ReNewRS)

## Project Overview

This project investigates whether online news recommender systems contribute to social polarization or radicalization. As news consumption is increasingly curated by algorithmic recommender systems, the core question is whether users become trapped in "filter bubbles"—environments where only information confirming existing opinions is presented, while conflicting views are excluded.

The ReNewRS project examines the occurrence of filter bubbles and their effects on user opinion formation through a series of experimental studies.

**Interdisciplinary Approach:**
The research is highly interdisciplinary, combining expertise from:

- Media and Communication Studies (providing the foundation for analyzing user effects).
- Data Science and Computer Science (providing technical implementation expertise).

**Methodology:**
To trace potential polarizing effects, the project selects divisive news topics, drawing article samples from various news sources with different political slants. These samples are used to run different versions of a prototypical news recommender system. By comparing the outcomes of various recommendation algorithms, the goal is to determine how news recommender systems can be configured to mitigate polarization and foster more balanced opinion formation. The ultimate aim is to derive guidelines for responsible news recommender systems.
