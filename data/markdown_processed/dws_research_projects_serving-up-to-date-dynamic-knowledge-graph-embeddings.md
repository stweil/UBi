# Serving up-to-date Dynamic Knowledge Graph Embeddings

This project addresses the challenge of providing up-to-date embeddings for dynamic knowledge graphs (KGs) and ontologies. The core goal is to develop a robust framework capable of:

1. Periodically collecting new KG versions.
1. Computing the necessary embeddings.
1. Making these embeddings publicly available for further research.

### Impact and Benefits

Providing publicly available, up-to-date embeddings is expected to yield several benefits for the research community:

- **Democratization:** Increases researchers’ access to critical data resources.
- **Efficiency:** Empowers researchers to conduct faster experimentation cycles.
- **Performance:** Improves overall performance across various downstream tasks utilizing KGs.
- **Sustainability:** Reduces the carbon footprint by promoting the reuse of pre-trained embeddings.
