# SpatialBenchRAG: A Multimodal, Multilingual Benchmark for KG-grounded RAG over Diverse Geographies

Many modern tasks and decisions rely heavily on spatial data and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). For instance, when a conversational assistant provides directions from Wasserturm to Luisenpark with a stop at a highly rated café, it often utilizes background processes involving spatial data retrieval.

## Knowledge Graphs (KGs) and Spatial Reasoning

Knowledge Graphs (KGs) are a powerful data model used to search for and link information. They provide a rich source of spatial information, and their graph-based format enables advanced spatial queries and supports complex spatio-temporal reasoning.

## Project Goal and Scope

The **SpatialBenchRAG** project aims to develop the first multimodal, multilingual benchmark specifically designed for evaluating RAG systems that are grounded in spatial KGs.

Addressing the current lack of standardized resources for spatial reasoning, the benchmark achieves its goal by combining diverse geographic datasets, including:

- OpenStreetMap
- Wikidata

This combination supports realistic, context-aware evaluation across various complex tasks, such as:

- Spatial and visual question answering
- Multi-hop reasoning

## Impact and Applications

By enabling transparent and reproducible research, this initiative seeks to accelerate the development of geographically grounded AI systems for critical applications, including:

- Disaster response
- Geo-aware assistants
- Spatial fact-checking
