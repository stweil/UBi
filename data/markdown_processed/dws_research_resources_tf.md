# Multi-Relational Link Prediction with Bilinear Models

This paper investigates the application of bilinear embedding models for the complex task of multi-relational link prediction and knowledge graph completion.

## Overview of the Research

Bilinear models are presented as a foundational and highly efficient approach for this task, capable of achieving strong prediction performance. The core objective of the research is to thoroughly explore the expressive power and the interconnectedness among various bilinear models proposed in the academic literature.

Key areas of investigation include:

- Determining whether various constraints enforced on the embeddings lead to "universal models" capable of representing any set of relations.
- Identifying potential subsumption relationships between different model architectures.

The authors report the findings of an independent experimental study, evaluating recent bilinear models within a standardized experimental setup. Furthermore, the research provides evidence suggesting that combining multiple bilinear models through relation-level ensembles can achieve state-of-the-art prediction performance.
