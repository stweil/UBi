# Seminar CS 717: Understanding Explainable AI Methods (HWS 2026)

This seminar provides an in-depth look at classical attribution methods within Explainable AI (XAI). The course covers techniques such as saliency maps, feature importance, and gradient-based explanations, focusing on how these methods help interpret and analyze the decision-making process of machine learning models.

## Kursziele (Goals)

Participants in this seminar will be able to:

- Read, understand, and explore current scientific literature.
- Summarize a current research topic in a concise report (10 single-column pages + references).
- Deliver two presentations on the chosen topic: a 3-minute flash presentation and a 15-minute detailed presentation.
- Moderate a scientific discussion regarding a fellow student's topic.
- Review a draft report submitted by a fellow student.

## Organisation und Voraussetzungen (Organization and Prerequisites)

- **Verantwortlich:** Prof. Dr.-Ing. Margret Keuper
- **Voraussetzungen:** Ein solides Hintergrundwissen in Machine Learning.
- **Teilnehmerzahl:** Die maximale Teilnehmerzahl beträgt 6 Bachelor-Studierende.
