# Data Analytics Team Project – Your Project, Your Team (HWS25)

This team project is designed for students who wish to work on a project of their own choosing with a team of their choice.

## Project Scope and Focus

The project must be related to [machine learning and/or data processing](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/) and align with predefined scope and goals. However, the project maintains flexibility.

Ideal project candidates are those that:

1. Produce assets useful to a wider community, OR
1. Explore a relevant research question with the goal of publishing the results in relevant academic venues.

## Logistics and Requirements

- **Type of Course:** Team project
- **ECTS Points:** 12 ECTS
- **Topic:** Student choice
- **Participants:** 3 – 6 students
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Language:** English
- **Applicable For:** Master of Business Informatics (MMDS)

## Application and Timeline

To participate, students must submit a short proposal and a list of team members.

- **Application Deadline:** By **August 17, 2025**, latest.
- **Submission Contact:** Rainer Gemulla
- **Timeline:**
  - The project will be discussed and refined with the team during the rest of August.
  - Final decisions are expected by the beginning of September.
  - If accepted, the project starts alongside regular team projects in September.
  - *Note:* If the project is not accepted, students are advised to apply for regular team projects, as sufficient time will be available.

**Supervisor:** [Prof. Dr. Rainer Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-rainer-gemulla/)
