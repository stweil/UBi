# Data and Web Science Seminar: Graph Learning (SM 445/CS 707)

The Data and Web Science seminar covers recent topics in data and web science. The focus for this term is **graph learning**, with a special (but not exclusive) emphasis on graph neural networks (GNNs).

## 📚 Course Details and Organization

- **Course Code:** SM 445/CS 707
- **Organizers:**
  - [Prof. Dr. Rainer Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-rainer-gemulla/)
  - [Adrian Kochsiek](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/alumni/dr-adrian-kochsiek/)
- **Credits:**
  - Master students: 2 SWS, 4 ECTS
  - Bachelor students: 2 SWS, 5 ECTS
- **Prerequisites (MSc):** Solid background in machine learning.
- **Capacity:** Maximum of 5 BSc and 10 MSc students.

## 🎯 Learning Goals

Through this seminar, participants will be expected to:

- Read, understand, and explore current scientific literature.
- Summarize a current research topic in a concise report (10 single-column pages + references).
- Deliver two presentations on the topic: a 3-minute flash presentation and a 15-minute final presentation.
- Moderate a scientific discussion on a peer's topic.
- Review a fellow student's report draft.

## 📝 Seminar Topics (Graph Learning)

Each student works on a topic within the seminar area, focusing on an accompanying reference paper. The presentation and report must explore the topic broadly, using the reference paper as a core element, rather than summarizing the paper itself.

*Note: Topics marked “BSc topics” are restricted to BSc students. Unmarked topics are for MSc students but can be taken by BSc students with appropriate background.*
