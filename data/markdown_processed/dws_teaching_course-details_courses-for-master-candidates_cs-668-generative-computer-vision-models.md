# Generative Computer Vision Models (CS-668)

This course provides fundamental concepts of deep generative models, using image generation as a primary example.

## 🗓️ Schedule & Logistics

- **Lectures:** Start: February 2026
- **Exercises:** Start: February 2026

## 🧑‍🏫 Instructor

- **Prof. Margret Keuper:** [Link to Professor's Profile](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-ing-margret-keuper/)

## 🎓 Participation Requirements

- **Open To:** Students of the Master Business Informatics and Mannheim Master in Data Science (MMDS).
- **Capacity:** The course is restricted to 50 participants.
- **Enrollment:** Places are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.

## 📚 Course Content Outline

The curriculum covers a wide range of advanced topics in generative modeling, including:

### Core Generative Models

- Autoregressive Models (e.g., RixelRNN, PixelCNN)
- Normalizing Flows and RealNVP-like models
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)
- Diffusion Models

### Deep Learning Architectures & Techniques

- Autoencoders (including latent space visualization and deep clustering)
- Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Transformers

### Supporting Topics

- Clustering
- Crash-Course on Neural Networks
- Deep Clustering
