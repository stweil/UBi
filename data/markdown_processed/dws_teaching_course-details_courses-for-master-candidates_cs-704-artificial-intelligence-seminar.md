# Artificial Intelligence Seminar (HWS 2022)

This seminar explores topics across two distinct areas: one group focuses on rule-based knowledge base completion, while the other analyzes the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs).

## Organization and Participants

**Organizers:**
The seminar is organized by:

- Christian Meilicke (Contact Person)
- Patrick Betz
- [Prof. Dr. Heiner Stuckenschmidt](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-heiner-stuckenschmidt/)

**Eligibility:**
This seminar is available only for master students.
