# Seminar: Traffic Forecasting with Neural Networks (FSS 2025)

This seminar explores various advanced approaches for traffic forecasting. Accurate traffic prediction is crucial for improving transportation systems, reducing urban congestion, and enhancing public safety. The knowledge gained helps city planners and authorities develop more efficient and sustainable urban mobility solutions.

Upon completion of this seminar, students will be able to:

## 📚 Key Topics and Literature Review

The seminar covers advanced methodologies in spatio-temporal data analysis for traffic prediction.

### Core Methodologies Covered:

- **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs):** Applications for traffic forecasting.
- **Multivariate Time-Series Forecasting:** Deep learning approaches for complex time series.
- **Graph-based Traffic Forecasting:** Techniques for modeling spatial dependencies.
- **Data Imputation:** Methods for handling missing data in traffic time series.

### Featured Research Papers:

- **Jiang, W., & Luo, J.** (2022). *Graph neural network for traffic forecasting: A survey.* Expert systems with applications, 207.
- **Salinas et al.** (2020). *DeepAR: Probabilistic forecasting with autoregressive recurrent networks.* IJF.
- **Hollmann, N., et al.** (2025). *Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model.* Nature, 637.
- **Shao, Z., et al.** (2022). *Spatial-temporal identity: A simple yet effective baseline for multivariate time series forecasting.* CIKM.
- **Cini, M., Zambon, & Alippi** (2023). *Taming Local Effects in Graph-based Spatiotemporal Forecasting.* NeurIPS.
- **Wu et al.** (2022). *TraverseNet: Unifying Space and Time in Message Passing for Traffic Forecasting.* TNNLS.
- **Cini, A., Marisca, I., & Alippi, C.** (2022). *Filling the G_ap_s: Multivariate Time Series Imputation by Graph Neural Networks.* ICLR.
