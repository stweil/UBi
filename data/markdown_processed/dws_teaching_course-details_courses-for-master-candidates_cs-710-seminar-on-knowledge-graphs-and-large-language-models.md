# Seminar: Knowledge Graphs and Large Language Models (CS 710)

Large Language Models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, represent major advances in Artificial Intelligence. While versatile, they can suffer from drawbacks like hallucinations, lack of transparency, and non-factuality. Knowledge Graphs (KGs), conversely, are key drivers of "white box AI," known for their factual and transparent nature.

This seminar explores the intersection of KGs and LLMs, aiming to combine the strengths of both technologies. Areas of focus include:

- KGs for LLM training and tuning
- KG Enhanced LLM inference and explanations
- Prompt engineering utilizing KGs
- Completing or refining KGs with LLMs
- Question Answering and Text Generation using KGs and LLMs
- Joint Reasoning with KGs and LLMs

## Seminar Goals and Structure

The primary goal of this seminar is to familiarize participants with advanced approaches to knowledge graph construction. Learning involves reading research papers, specifications, and tool descriptions, conducting personal experiments where applicable, and discussing insights with peers.

**Participant Requirement:** Each participant is expected to introduce and present a specific technique for knowledge graph construction to the seminar group. All seminar papers undergo a peer review process. Presentations are scheduled for approximately 25 minutes.
