# CS 717: Seminar on Explainable AI Methods

This seminar focuses on Explainable AI (XAI) methods, with a specific emphasis on **Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs)**. Participants will explore the architecture of CBMs and their practical applications, particularly how these models enhance interpretability by linking high-level human concepts with machine learning predictions.

## 📚 Seminar Details

- **Organization:** Prof. Dr.-Ing. Margret Keuper
- **Prerequisites:** Background in Machine Learning.
- **Capacity:** Maximum of 6 Master's students.

## 🎯 Learning Goals

Upon completion of the seminar, students will be able to:

- Read, understand, and explore current scientific literature in the field.
- Summarize a current research topic in a concise report (10 single-column pages + references).
- Deliver two presentations on the chosen topic: a 3-minute flash presentation and a 15-minute final presentation.
- Moderate a scientific discussion regarding a fellow student's topic.
- Review a draft report submitted by a fellow student.

## 🔬 Seminar Topics (Concept Bottleneck Models)

Each student will work on a specific topic within the seminar area, guided by an accompanying reference paper. The presentation and report must explore the topic broadly, using the reference paper as a foundation, rather than summarizing the paper itself.
