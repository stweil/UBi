# Seminar CS 717: Understanding Explainable AI Methods (HWS 2026)

This seminar focuses on Explainable AI (XAI) methods, with a specific emphasis on Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs). The course will cover the architecture of CBMs and their practical applications, exploring how these models enhance interpretability by linking high-level human concepts directly with machine learning predictions.

## Seminar Goals

Participants in this seminar will be able to:

- Read, understand, and explore current scientific literature in the field.
- Summarize a current research topic in a concise report (10 single-column pages plus references).
- Deliver two presentations on the chosen topic: a 3-minute flash presentation and a 15-minute detailed presentation.
- Moderate a scientific discussion regarding a fellow student's topic.
- Review a draft report submitted by a fellow student.
