# AI and Data Science in Fiction and Society (HWS 2025)

This seminar explores the influence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data science on modern society by analyzing fictional works, particularly novels. Participants will discuss technological developments described in literature and evaluate their potential realism and societal impact.

## Seminar Goals and Format

The primary goal is for each participant to read and analyze a novel of their choice, identifying parallels between:

1. AI technology depicted in fiction and actual technological developments.
1. The interaction between technology and society.

Discussions will focus heavily on the societal and ethical implications of AI. Each participant must prepare a 25-minute presentation accompanied by a seminar paper, which will undergo a peer review process. Note that the workload requires reading the novels being peer-reviewed, resulting in a substantial reading commitment throughout the semester.
