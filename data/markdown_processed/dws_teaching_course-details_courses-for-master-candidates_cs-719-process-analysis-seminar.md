# Seminar Process Analysis (CS 719)

This seminar focuses on state-of-the-art research in process analysis. The curriculum covers several advanced subjects, including:

- Business Process Management (BPM)
- Process Mining
- Process Analysis
- Stream Processing
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA)

## 🎯 Learning Goals

Upon completion of this seminar, students will be able to:

- Read, understand, and explore scientific literature based on an assigned topic.
- Write a concise report summarizing the state of the art in the assigned topic (10–12 pages, excluding references).
- Deliver a presentation on the topic (15 minutes presentation + 10 minutes Q&A).
- Discuss research findings with fellow students.

## 📋 Prerequisites

To be eligible for the seminar, students must have completed or be currently enrolled in one of the following courses:

- IE692 (Advanced Process Mining)
- IS515 (Process Management & Analytics)

## 📚 Seminar Topics

Participants will be assigned a topic that serves as the starting point for a literature survey. Students should indicate three preferred topics using the dedicated sign-up form (provided later).

A list of potential topics can be found [here (PDF, 123 kB)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Einrichtungen/dws/Files_Teaching/Seminar_CS719/topics_process_analysis_seminar_hws23.pdf).

Alternatively, participants are free to propose their own topic/paper by reviewing publications from key venues such as:

- International conferences on Business Process Management (BPM)
- Advanced Information Systems Engineering (CAiSE)
- Process Mining (ICPM)
- Journals such as *Information Systems* and *Decision Support Systems*.
