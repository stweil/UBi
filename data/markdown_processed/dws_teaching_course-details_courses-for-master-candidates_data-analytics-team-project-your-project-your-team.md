# Data Analytics Team Project (FSS26)

This team project is designed for students who wish to work on a self-selected project with a chosen team. The core focus must be related to **machine learning and/or data processing** [Data Analytics Focus](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/research/focus-groups/data-analytics-prof-gemulla/).

## Project Scope and Goals

The project must align with predefined scope and goals but maintains flexibility. Ideal project candidates are those that:

- Produce assets useful to a wider community.
- Explore a relevant research question with the potential for publication in relevant academic venues.

## Logistics and Requirements

- **Course Type:** Team project
- **ECTS Points:** 12 ECTS
- **Topic:** Student choice (must fit ML/Data Processing scope)
- **Participants:** 3 – 6 students
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Language of Instruction:** English
- **Applicable Program:** Master of Business Informatics (MMDS)

## Application and Timeline

To participate, students must submit a short proposal and a list of team members.

- **Application Deadline:** By **January 18, 2026**, submitted to [Prof. Dr. Rainer Gemulla](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/professors/prof-dr-rainer-gemulla/).
- **Project Discussion:** The project will be discussed and refined during the remainder of January.
- **Finalization:** Decisions regarding the project will be finalized by the beginning of February.
- **Start Date:** Accepted projects commence alongside regular team projects in February.
- **Alternative:** If the proposed project is not accepted, students are advised that they can still apply for regular team projects, as sufficient time will be available.
