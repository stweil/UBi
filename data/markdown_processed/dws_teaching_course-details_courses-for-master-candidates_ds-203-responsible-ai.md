# DS 203: Responsible AI – Conceptual Foundations, Methods, and Applications

This course provides a comprehensive overview of the emerging transdisciplinary research field of Responsible AI, covering its conceptual foundations, methodological approaches, and practical applications.

## Course Content Overview

The lecture series is structured into three main parts:

1. **Conceptual Foundations:** Examination of core concepts crucial for human-AI interaction, including trust, fairness, bias, and responsibility, viewed from a transdisciplinary perspective.
1. **Methods and Approaches:** Focus on mixed-methods approaches for investigating human-AI interaction. This includes qualitative social science methods (e.g., interviewing, group discussions, observational methods) alongside techniques from design thinking and psychology, particularly for participatory AI development.
1. **Applications and Challenges:** Presentation of specific ethical and societal challenges and requirements for responsible AI development across various applications, such as AI systems in medicine.
