# Knowledge Graphs (HWS 2025)

Knowledge Graphs (KGs) are a universal method of data representation consumable by both humans and machines. They are a key component in many modern data-driven systems and AI applications, which frequently require domain-specific or general world knowledge. KGs are increasingly utilized by large organizations, with the Google Knowledge Graph being a prominent example. Furthermore, large-scale open knowledge graphs like DBpedia or Wikidata are available for free use in powerful AI applications.

This course provides an introduction to the underlying standards of knowledge graphs, covering knowledge representation, query languages, and logical inference.

### Course Contents

The curriculum covers the following core topics:

- History of Knowledge Graphs and the Semantic Web
- Graph Representation Languages (XML, RDF)
- Graph Inference and Logical Reasoning (RDF Schema, OWL)
- Knowledge Modeling: Ontologies, Linked Data, and Property Graphs
- Knowledge Integration
- Data Quality in Knowledge Graphs
- Commercial and Open Source Tools and Systems

In addition to theoretical lectures, the course includes hands-on exercises and a practical project where students build a small knowledge graph-backed application.
