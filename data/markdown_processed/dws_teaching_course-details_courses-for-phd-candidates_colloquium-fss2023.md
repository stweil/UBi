# DWS Colloquium (FSS 2023)

The DWS Colloquium is a recurring academic event featuring a series of scientific talks by local and international researchers, alongside poster sessions where PhD students from the DWS group present their ongoing research.

**Organization:**
The colloquium is jointly organized by the senior faculty members of the DWS group: Prof. Bizer, Prof. Gemulla, Prof. Paulheim, Prof. Ponzetto, and Prof. Stuckenschmidt.

**Format Details:**

- **Frequency:** Every Tuesday.
- **Time:** 12:00 to 13:30.
- **Location:** Most sessions take place offline in **Room B6 A1.01**.
- **Online Sessions:** Sessions featuring only remote speakers will be held online via Zoom. Please check the schedule for the specific format of each date.

## Colloquium Schedule Highlights

The following schedule details the events for the specified period:
