# Thesis Guidelines for DWS Students

These guidelines detail the necessary steps for students wishing to write their Bachelor or Master thesis under the supervision of a DWS professor.

## 1. Application Process

To begin the thesis process, you must submit an application package.

**Required Documents:**

- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Transcript of Records
- Master Candidate Profile form (Required only for Master thesis applications)

**Topic Selection:**
You may propose your own topic or select a topic suggested by the department. If choosing a suggested topic, please detail your general interests on the Master Candidate Profile form.

**Company Cooperation:**
If your thesis involves cooperation with an external company, you must submit the company's name and contact details. Crucially, ensure the company can provide all necessary resources (e.g., datasets, servers). Before starting, the company must sign a formal [supervision agreement](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Einrichtungen/dws/Files_Teaching/Theses/Supervision-Agreement-DWS-2019.docx).

## 3. Topic Proposal (Exposé)

Before commencing the thesis, a convincing topic proposal (exposé) is mandatory.

**Guidelines:**

- The proposal must adhere to the [SMART criteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMART_criteria).
- Use the official [proposal template](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Einrichtungen/dws/Files_Teaching/Theses/proposal_template.zip).
- The proposal must not exceed 2 pages (excluding references).
- Focus on *what* you intend to do, rather than spending excessive space on preliminaries.

**Structure:**
The proposal must be divided into three distinct subsections:

1. **Introduction:** A brief sketch (one paragraph) of the topic.
1. **Background:** A compact description of relevant concepts and a scientific chain of arguments leading to the proposal's core question ("what and why"). This section, combined with the Introduction, should not exceed one page.
1. **Goals and Work Plan:** This is the most critical section. It must clearly express and motivate the research questions, goals, and the methodology to address them. Structure this section by main research questions or tasks, including rough time estimates and milestones. *Focus on demonstrating understanding of the scope and challenges, rather than presenting intricate solutions.*

*Note: Technical details (formulas, long code listings) are not required. Prefer high-level, intuitive descriptions.*

## 4. Registration

Once your topic proposal has been accepted, immediately register your thesis with the [secretary of the respective chair](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/administration/). This step formally fixes the topic, advisor, starting date, and end date.

## 5. Supervision

- **Meetings:** You are responsible for scheduling all meetings with your advisor to discuss progress.
- **Milestone Reviews:** Schedule milestone meetings with the professor/senior advisor approximately every other month. These meetings require a short presentation with well-prepared slides and meaningful results demonstrating progress.
- **Preparation:** Always send presentation slides to the professor/senior advisor beforehand.
- **Scope:** Advisors generally will not read full drafts of your thesis, nor will they write or review your code.

## 6. Writing the Report

The final report is the primary document for grading.

- **Language & Format:** The report **must** be written in English using the provided [LATEX template](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Einrichtungen/dws/Files_Teaching/Theses/dws-templates.zip).
- **Writing Strategy:** Start writing early and maintain detailed notes, including parameter settings for all experiments.
- **Content Focus:** The target audience is peers in computer science; avoid repeating basic textbook material. Be concise and direct. Use examples and figures throughout.
- **Structure:** Include an abstract and discuss the overall thesis outline with your advisor. Use an appendix for relevant additional material, avoiding low-level details like long code listings.

## 7. Submission

Submit your thesis according to the following procedures:

1. **Appointment:** Make an appointment in advance.
1. **Physical Submission:** Hand in two printed copies to the [secretary's office](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/dws/people/administration/).
1. **Digital Submission:** Submit a digital copy (PDF) via email.
1. **Supplementary Material:** Along with the thesis, submit your source code, data, and instructions for running the code (via CD-ROM, memory stick, or online repository like GitHub). If data exceeds capacity, include a relevant sample.

*If a certificate of passing prior to final grading is required, inform the department at least two weeks before submission.*
