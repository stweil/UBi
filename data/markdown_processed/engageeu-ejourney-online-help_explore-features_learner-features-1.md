# Learner Features: ENGAGE.EU Application Guide

A learner is defined as a student or participant who engages in the educational activities and mobility opportunities offered by ENGAGE.EU. This guide provides comprehensive support for using the platform as a Learner.

## 📝 Application Prerequisites

Before applying for the Winter School, you must prepare the following information and documents:

**Required Personal Details:**

- Birthdate
- Gender
- Degree Title (e.g., Business Administration)

**Documents to Upload:**

- Transcript of Records
- Motivation Statement (must be in English)

***Tip:** Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and all necessary documents are ready before submitting your application.*

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Application Process

Follow these steps to successfully submit and manage your application:

**Step 1: Register/Log in**
Access the platform and log into your account.

**Step 2: Access the Application Form**
Click the designated “Apply here” button, typically located at the bottom left of the screen.

**Step 3: Complete the Application Form**

- **Automatically Filled Fields:** First Name, Surname, Email Address, Student ID.
- **Manually Complete:**
  - Select the desired course from the available options list.
  - Choose your gender from the drop-down menu.
  - Enter your birthdate.
  - Specify your degree title.
  - **Upload Documents:**
    - Transcript of Records
    - Motivation Letter in English (PDF and video formats are accepted)

**Step 4: Optional Fields**
You may optionally subscribe to the ENGAGE.EU newsletter. These fields are not required for submission.

**Step 5: Submit Your Application**
Navigate to the upper right corner of the form and click the “Apply” button.

- **Confirmation:** A confirmation message will appear on the screen, and you will receive a **confirmation email** verifying receipt of your application.

**Step 6: Cancelling or Withdrawing Your Application**

- **Before Application Phase Ends:** You can cancel your application under 'My Applications' by clicking the action button on the right side. You are free to re-apply.
- **After Application Phase Ends:** The action button will change from 'cancel' to 'withdraw'. Withdrawing an application is final; your home university will be notified, and you will not be able to re-apply.

**Step 7: Confirming or Rejecting Your Admission Offer**
Once the receiving Higher Education Institution (HEI) reviews your application, you will be notified of their decision. You must respond within the indicated deadline to:

- **Confirm** your admission to accept the offer, OR
- **Reject** the offer if you do not wish to proceed.

### ⚠️ Troubleshooting Tips

If a red box appears around any field, it indicates that the field is either empty or contains an invalid entry. Please complete the field or select a valid option from the drop-down menu.

## 📊 Checking Your Application Status

To view the status of your submissions:

1. Press the **„Learning Activity“** button from the left-hand menu.
1. Select **„My extracurricular Applications“**.
1. A list of all your applications and their current status will be displayed on the right.
