# eJourney Zugang: Login, Registrierung und Einstellungen

This guide covers how to access the eJourney platform, manage your settings, and securely log out.

## 🚀 Login und Registrierung (Accessing eJourney)

The registration process is automatic when you first log in, utilizing Single Sign-On (SSO) provided by your home university. You only need an **active local account** at one of the [ENGAGE.EU member universities](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/engageeu-ejourney-online-help/get-started/).

**To Log In or Register:**

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the eJourney login page: [https://ejourney-qa.engageuniversity.eu/ilasc/login.php?cmd=force_login](https://ejourney-qa.engageuniversity.eu/ilasc/login.php?cmd=force_login).
1. Click on „Select University“ and choose your home university.
1. Click on „Sign In“. The browser will redirect you to your home institution's login page.
1. Log in using your username and password, and agree to submit the required attributes.

**What you can do:**

- **Logged In:** Access personalized features like viewing learning activities or uploading files (if you are an administrator).
- **Logged Out:** Access public features, such as the Course Catalogue.

## ⚙️ Einstellungen verwalten (Managing Settings)

To adjust your personal preferences, open the **Settings** section.

1. Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner.
1. In the drop-down menu, select **Settings**.
1. **Language:** Choose your preferred language for the platform display.
1. **Last Visited:** Decide if the system should remember the page visited during the current session or keep it for future sessions.
1. **ILIAS Time Zone:** Select your local time zone (e.g., GMT+1:00 Amsterdam, Berlin, Bern, Rome, Stockholm, Vienna) to ensure deadlines are displayed correctly.
1. **Date Input Format:** Choose your preferred date format (e.g., 31.10.2025).
1. **Time Format:** Set the time format (e.g., 13:00 for 24-hour format).
1. After making all changes, click **Save** to apply them.

## 🚪 Logout (Logging Out)

Logging out ends your current eJourney session and returns you to the start page. After logging out, you lose access to learning activities, administrator functions, and other personalized features.

**Security Note:** The system automatically ends user sessions after a period of inactivity to protect user data.

**To Log Out:**

1. Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner of the screen.
1. From the dropdown menu, select „Logout“.
1. A confirmation page will appear stating: “You logged off from eJourney. Your session has been closed.” This page also provides a link to [Login to eJourney](https://ejourney-qa.engageuniversity.eu/ilasc/login.php?cmd=force_login) and an overview map of the ENGAGE.EU partner universities.
