# Roles and Permissions on ENGAGE.EU

The platform defines two primary user roles: **Learners** and **Managing Staff**. Access to specific features and functionalities is determined by the role assigned to the user.

## 🧑‍🎓 Learners

All users are assigned the **Learner** role by default upon registration. This group encompasses both students and staff members who participate in the programs.

Learners are able to:

- Browse available ENGAGE.EU programs.
- Submit applications to programs.
- Track the status of their applications.
- Manage their personal profile and application information.

## 🧑‍💼 Managing Staff

**Managing Staff** are staff members responsible for the administration of ENGAGE.EU short programs.

In addition to all Learner permissions, Managing Staff have elevated access, allowing them to:

- Access ENGAGE.EU administrative features.
- Review and manage program applications.
- Support the selection and administration processes.

### Functional Roles within Managing Staff

During the application process, Managing Staff may adopt different functional roles depending on their institutional context:

- **Sending Staff:** Staff members affiliated with an institution that nominates or sends applicants to programs hosted by another institution.
- **Receiving Staff:** Staff members affiliated with an institution that hosts a program and are responsible for reviewing and processing incoming applications.

## ⚙️ Role Assignment and Access

By default, every user is recognized as a Learner.

Staff members who require administrative access must be explicitly granted the **Managing Staff** role. To request this elevated access, users must contact the **eJourney team**, as they are responsible for assigning **Managing Staff** permissions.
