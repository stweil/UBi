# Joint Certificate Programme in Digital Transformation

This Joint Programme in Digital Transformation equips participants with the technological skills necessary to understand data, leverage digital upheavals, and exert societal influence as change makers.

Participants have the opportunity to collaborate with some of the largest tech and digital companies through innovative case studies and workshops. A highlight of the program is the final summer seminar held at one of the seven partner universities, where all participants gather to practically apply their knowledge and build networks.

**Program Details:**

- **Language:** The program is conducted in English.
- **Further Information:** Detailed information, the curriculum, and the application form can be found on the [ENGAGE.EU Homepage](https://www.engageuniversity.eu/engage-eu-joint-programme-in-digital-transformation/).
