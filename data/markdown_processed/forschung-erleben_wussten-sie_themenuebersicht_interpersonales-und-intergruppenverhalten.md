# Experience Science at the University of Mannheim

This section provides an overview of how the University of Mannheim integrates experiential learning and scientific inquiry across various disciplines.

## 🔬 What is Experience Science?

Experience Science focuses on the intersection of theory and practice, ensuring that academic learning is deeply connected to real-world application. It moves beyond traditional lecture formats to create immersive learning environments.

- **Core Principle:** Learning by doing.
- **Goal:** To equip students with practical skills alongside theoretical knowledge.

## 📚 Areas of Focus and Application

The concept of experiential learning is applied across multiple faculties and research groups.

### 1. Interdisciplinary Projects

These projects bring together students from different departments to solve complex, real-world problems.

- **[Project Example 1](link-to-project-1):** Focuses on sustainable urban development.
- **[Project Example 2](link-to-project-2):** Addresses digital ethics and behavioral science.

### 2. Curricular Integration

Experiential elements are woven directly into degree programs, ensuring continuous exposure to practical methods.

- **Workshops and Labs:** Hands-on sessions led by industry experts.
- **Case Studies:** Analysis of current industry challenges.
