# Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS)

The Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS) at the [University of Mannheim](http://www.uni-mannheim.de/1/english/) provides graduate training in empirical and quantitative methods, along with their application to business, economics, and the social sciences.

## Doctoral Programs

GESS coordinates doctoral studies across several specialized centers:

- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB):** Administers the doctoral program at the [Business School](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/). This school holds triple accreditation from AACSB, EFDM (EQUIS), and AMBA.
- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE):** Administers the doctoral program at the [Department of Economics](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/).
- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Social and Behavioral Sciences (CDSS):** Administers doctoral programs in Political Science, Psychology, Social Data Science, and Sociology, in close collaboration with the [School of Social Sciences](https://www.sowi.uni-mannheim.de/en/).
