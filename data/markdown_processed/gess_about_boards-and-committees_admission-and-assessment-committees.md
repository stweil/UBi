# Admission and Assessment Committees (AAC)

The Admission and Assessment Committees (AAC) serve as the governing bodies for the three associated Centers. Each committee is composed of program directors representing their respective departments. Furthermore, each Center elects a designated Academic Director who represents the Center at the GESS Board of Director Meetings.
