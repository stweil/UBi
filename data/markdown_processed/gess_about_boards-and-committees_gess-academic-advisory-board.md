# Academic Advisory Board (AAB)

The Academic Advisory Board (AAB) advises the GESS in all significant academic and organizational matters. It is composed of highly experienced and internationally renowned researchers who are familiar with successful graduate schools and the higher education systems in the USA, Europe, and Germany.

## AAB Mitglieder

The board comprises the following experts:

- **Dirk Bergemann**: Department of Economics, [Yale University](https://campuspress.yale.edu/dirkbergemann/)
- **Sir Richard Blundell**: Department of Economics, [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/~uctp39a/)
- **Marnik Dekimpe**: Department of Marketing, [Tilburg University](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/webwijs/show/m.g.dekimpe.htm)
- **Ruediger Fahlenbrach**: College of Management of Technology, [Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/sfi-rf/)

- **Norbert Schwarz**: Department of Psychology, [University of Southern California Marshall](https://dornsife.usc.edu/norbert-schwarz/)
- **Jan Stallaert**: Department of Operations and Information Management, [University of Connecticut](https://www.business.uconn.edu/person/jan-stallaert/)
