# GESS Board of Directors

The GESS Board of Directors serves as the governing body for the graduate school. It is committed to the principle of shared collegial governance.

## Governance Responsibilities

The Board is responsible for:

- Supervision of the graduate school’s academic programs.
- Administration of the school’s budget.
- Defining the long-term mission and vision of the school.

The Board is headed by the Dean of Graduate Studies, who reports directly to the Office of the President of the University of Mannheim and represents the school externally.

## Board Composition

The Board consists of:

1. The University Vice President for Research and Early-Stage Researchers.
1. Three Academic Directors, each representing one center for doctoral studies.

Academic Directors are elected by the graduate school members of their respective school or department.
