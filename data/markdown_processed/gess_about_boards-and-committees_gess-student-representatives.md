# GESS Student Representatives

GESS student representatives are elected by their respective Center's doctoral students to represent their interests at the Graduate School. This structure ensures that student interests, concerns, and needs are considered at every level of decision-making. By providing feedback, student representatives can drive positive change within the GESS and ensure that student needs are met.

## Responsibilities of Student Representatives

The key responsibilities and tasks include:

- **Representation:** Advocating for and communicating student interests to the GESS board and the administration.
- **Feedback Collection:** Proactively meeting with and voicing feedback from students.
- **Participation:** Attending meetings with the GESS board and administration to provide input on the student experience.
- **Information Dissemination:** Informing the student body about relevant discussions and decisions made at the meetings of the student representatives or the GESS board.
