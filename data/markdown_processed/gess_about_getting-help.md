# Getting Help and Support Services

At the GESS, we celebrate the diverse backgrounds and experiences of our doctoral students, recognizing them as key to maximizing human potential. In adherence to the [Code of Conduct of the University of Mannheim (PDF)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Universitaet/Dokumente/Forschung_Senatsrichtlinie_Partnerschaftliches_Verhalten_en.pdf), the GESS strictly prohibits behavior that violates another person’s dignity and integrity.

The university strongly supports the compatibility of family life and professional life. Doctoral students and staff are entitled to use parent-child-offices, and students with children benefit from discounts at the [Kinderhaus](https://www.stw-ma.de/en/studying-with-children/kinderhaus-en/) provided by the Studierendenwerk Mannheim.

## Gender and Diversity Committee

The Gender and Diversity Committee monitors and assesses the Graduate School's performance regarding gender and diversity, ensuring the GESS remains sensitive to these matters. It serves as a primary point of contact for doctoral students seeking advice and support concerning their studies and work. Committee members provide information on topics such as discrimination, parental leave, and childcare services.

**Contact:**
For questions or problems, committee members can be contacted via: gess.gdc-office@mail.uni-mannheim.de.

## Support for Specific Life Circumstances

### Students with Children

Reconciling studies and family life can be challenging. The University of Mannheim offers comprehensive support, including on-campus childcare services, special examination regulations for student parents, and a dedicated Parent and Child Room.

### Studying with a Disability or Chronic Illness

The commissioner and counselor for students with disabilities or chronic illnesses can provide advice and support for any difficulties encountered during studies due to such conditions.

### Physical and Mental Health Support

For students seeking help or information on physical and mental well-being, the University maintains a comprehensive list of support services. [Follow this link](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/gender-equality-and-equal-opportunity/counseling/good-mental-health-at-university/looking-after-your-mental-health-while-studying-or-pursuing-your-doctorate/) for more resources and guidance.

### Counseling Services

If you need advice regarding difficult situations among colleagues or conflicts with fellow students, the various counseling services at the university are available to offer support. [More information on Counseling](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/gender-equality-and-equal-opportunity/counseling/).
