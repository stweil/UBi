## Cost of Living in Mannheim

Living in Mannheim is considered relatively affordable compared to other major German cities. Students should budget approximately **EUR 850 to EUR 1,000 per month**. This budget estimate includes:

- **Rent:** EUR 250 to EUR 450
- **Health Insurance:** Approximately EUR 60 to EUR 200 (This amount varies based on individual circumstances and the chosen insurer, see [health insurance](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/welcome-center/relocation/preparation/health-insurance/)).
- **Necessities:** Remaining funds for groceries, clothing, etc.

*Note: Monthly expenses are highly dependent on personal lifestyle. Furthermore, studying at the GESS does not incur tuition fees.*

For detailed information regarding housing, including flat hunting, current rent prices, and neighborhood guides, please visit the university's [website](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/campus/mannheim/living-in-mannheim/) section on living in Mannheim.

## Scholarships and Financial Support

The GESS is dedicated to financing doctoral students' research activities through various scholarship opportunities.

### Guaranteed Funding

Admitted students are guaranteed **three to five years of funding**, depending on their specific program and academic performance. Additionally, doctoral students with children are eligible for supplementary financial support.

### Stipend Details

For the initial 1–2 years of coursework, all students receive a **EUR 1,500 monthly stipend**. During this period, students are generally exempt from teaching, administrative, or other service commitments (exceptions may apply for students holding RA or TA contracts in their second year).

### Available Scholarship Sources

The GESS administers and provides access to an extensive range of scholarships (totaling around EUR 18,000 annually), including:

- [German Academic Exchange Service](https://www.daad.de/en/) (DAAD)
- Fontana-Foundation (private donor)
- Funding program for the promotion of postgraduates of the Land of Baden-Württemberg (Landesgraduiertenförderung, LGF)
- Land of Baden-Württemberg
- [University of Mannheim Foundation](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/foundation/)
- Matching funds (private donors)

*Disclaimer: All financial assistance and scholarships are subject to the existing guidelines and provisions of the respective funding body and scholarship program.*

## Additional Funding Opportunities

Beyond the core scholarships, further funding can be secured for specific academic needs, including:

- Completion scholarships
- Funding for research stays abroad (periods between 3 to 6 months)
- Funding for scientific experiments
- Comprehensive travel support
- Awards
