# Student Life and Support at the University of Mannheim

GESS doctoral students are supported within a highly personalized academic and administrative environment. Students not only share offices with peers during the program phase but also regularly interact with colleagues from other doctoral centers through classes, social events, and brown-bag seminar series.

## Location and Context

The GESS is situated in the heart of [Mannheim](https://www.visit-mannheim.de/en). The University of [Mannheim](https://www.visit-mannheim.de/en) is primarily located within one of Europe's largest baroque palaces. Mannheim is a major urban center in the vibrant metropolitan area of the [Rhine-Neckar-Triangle](https://m-r-n.com/), a region that, together with Heidelberg and Ludwigshafen, boasts 2.4 million inhabitants, robust infrastructure, and global businesses. The city is surrounded by low mountain ranges belonging to the famous wine-growing region of the Electoral Palatinate.

## Student Resources and Services

The University of Mannheim offers comprehensive support services for its students:

- **Housing:** A wide choice of housing options is available to help students quickly become familiar with Mannheim and its surroundings ([Housing](https://www.stw-ma.de/en/Housing.html)).
- **German Language Courses:** Participants can access preparatory courses, courses during the semester, and the International Summer and Winter Academy. These courses also provide insight into German culture and popular mindsets ([German Language Courses](https://www.daf.uni-mannheim.de/en/)).
- **Welcome Center:** The Welcome Center provides essential information, advice, and assistance to international students and researchers regarding practical training, immigration, and housing issues ([Welcome Center](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/welcome-center/)).
- **Sports Facilities:** GESS doctoral students have access to a broad range of athletic and fitness facilities, exercise classes, and equipment offered by the University of Mannheim ([Sports Facilities](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/sports/)).

## Professional Development and Teaching Skills

For teaching activities, the [Teaching and Learning Center (ZLL)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/teaching-and-learning-center-zll/) offers various courses and individual consultations for educators. To enhance didactic skills, teachers can earn the '[Baden-Württemberg Certificate for Teaching and Learning](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/teaching-and-learning-center-zll/course-design/qualification-and-continuing-education/baden-wuerttemberg-certificate-for-teaching-and-learning-at-university-level/)' at the ZLL, a qualification recognized internationally.

## Equality, Diversity, and Inclusion

The University of Mannheim is deeply committed to advancing equality, diversity, and inclusion. Since establishing the [Department of Equal Opportunity and Social Diversity](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/gender-equality-and-equal-opportunity/equal-opportunity-commissioners/department-of-equal-opportunity-and-diversity/) in 2011, the department has worked to develop sustainable structures in gender equality and social diversity. It aims to create a pleasant, inclusive, and diverse working environment for all.

The department provides:

- Tailor-made mentoring programs for junior female scientists, covering personal development workshops and guidance on securing third-party funding.
- Resources to help achieve work-study-life balance.
- Individual counseling services when necessary.
