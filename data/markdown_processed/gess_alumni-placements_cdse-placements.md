# CDSE Alumni Placements History

This document summarizes the career placements of alumni from the CDSE program, organized by graduation year.

## 🎓 2025 Placements

The class of 2025 saw placements across academia, international finance, and major corporations.

**Notable Placements Include:**

- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at ISEG – Lisbon School of Economics and Management (University of Lisbon), CUNEF Universidad Madrid, and National University of Singapore.
- **Research:** Postdoctoral Researcher positions at ZEW Mannheim (focusing on Market Design and Labour Markets), University of Göttingen, and Max Planck Institute.
- **Finance/Consulting:** Roles at the International Monetary Fund (Thibault Cézanne), AMLA – Anti-Money Laundering Authority (David Müller), and senior economist positions at Simon-Kucher (Max Riegel).
- **Other:** Placements at the Bank of Canada (Hannes Twieling) and various roles at South China Normal University.

*(Note: The memory of Ulrich Roschitsch, who graduated in June 2025, is held with deep respect.)*

## 🎓 2024 Placements

Alumni placements in 2024 were concentrated in academic research and international institutions.

**Key Placements:**

- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at University Carlos III of Madrid (Lukas Diebold), Stockholm University (Laura Montenbruck), and Masaryk University (Jonathan Stäbler).
- **Research:** Postdoctoral positions at the University of St. Gallen (Giovanni Ballarin, Karl Schulz) and ETH Zürich (Lukas Hack).
- **Institutions:** Roles at CUNEF Universidad Spain (Andrés Plúas-López) and KU Leuven (Lukas Mahler).

## 🎓 2023 Placements

The 2023 cohort secured roles in major European financial bodies, consulting, and academia.

**Key Placements:**

- **Finance/Economics:** Research Economist roles at Banque de France (Tommaso Gasparini) and roles at the OECD Economics Department (Martin Reinhard).
- **Data/Tech:** Data Scientist roles at Daimler Truck AG (Anh Thi Dam) and FME (Mark Jeffrey Spils).
- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at CREST-ENSAE Paris (Suzanne Bellue) and University of Texas, Austin (Oliver Pfäuti).

## 🎓 2022 Placements

This year showed strong placements in consulting, central banking, and academic research.

**Key Placements:**

- **Consulting/Finance:** Roles at Charles River Associates (Johannes Dittrich) and Compass Lexecon (Jasper Haller).
- **Central Banking/Economics:** Research Economist roles at the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco (Timo Reinelt) and various roles at the European Central Bank (Timo Reinelt).
- **Academia/Research:** Placements at the University of Münster (Daniil Larionov), LMU Munich (Michaela Paffenholz, Karl Schulz), and the University of Konstanz (Alexander Matusche).

## 🎓 2021 Placements

Placements were diverse, spanning top-tier universities and major financial institutions.

**Key Placements:**

- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at Tor Vergata University of Rome (Andrey Alexandrov) and LMU Munich (Moritz Drechsel-Grau, Tomasz Olma).
- **Finance/Banking:** Roles at Deutsche Bundesbank (Johannes Wacks, Nils Wehrhöfer) and an economist role at Zalando SE (Majed Dodin).
- **Consulting/Research:** Placements at Princeton University (Andrey Alexandrov) and various roles at the Center for Evaluation and Development (C4ED).

## 🎓 2020 Placements

The 2020 cohort saw placements in leading German economic bodies and universities.

**Key Placements:**

- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at LMU Munich (Alessandra Allocca) and University of Amsterdam (Fabian Greimel).
- **Research:** Postdoc positions at ZEW (Robert Aue) and Johannes-Gutenberg University Mainz (Yasemin Özdemir).
- **Policy/Government:** Roles at the German Federal Chancellery (Sebastian Camarero Garcia) and the German Council of Economic Experts (Niklas Garnadt).

## 🎓 2019 Placements

Early placements included roles at major international financial centers and academic institutions.

**Key Placements:**

- **Finance/Banking:** Roles at the European Central Bank (Lukas Henkel) and Scope Ratings AG (Vahe Krrikyan).
- **Academia:** Assistant Professor roles at Boston College (Hanno Foerster) and University of Birmingham (Xin Gao).
- **Research:** Postdoc opportunities at the University of Cambridge and Compass Lexecon, Madrid.

## 🎓 2018 Placements

The 2018 class demonstrated strong entry into global economic consulting and banking.

**Key Placements:**

- **Banking/Finance:** Roles at the Bank of Italy (Francesco Paolo Conteduca) and Deutsche Bundesbank (Mario Meier, Tobias Etzel).
- **Consulting/Advisory:** Positions at McKinsey & Company (Senior Consultant, Associate Partner) and IDinsight.
- **Academia:** Placements at Cornell University (Marcel Preuß) and the University of Exeter Business School (Sebastian Merkel).
