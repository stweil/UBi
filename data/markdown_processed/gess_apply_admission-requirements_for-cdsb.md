# Admission to the CDSB Program

This guide outlines the requirements and deadlines for applying to the CDSB Program.

## Key Deadlines and Prerequisites

- **Application Deadline (Fall 2026 Intake):** 31 March 2026
- **Early Application Deadline:** 15 January 2026
- **Prerequisite:** A Master’s degree or a four-year Bachelor's degree in the respective field or an equivalent qualification.
- **Admission Basis:** Admission is based purely on academic performance and potential.
- **Submission Method:** Applications must be submitted via the [Online application portal](https://gess-application.uni-mannheim.de/portal/). Applications sent by mail will not be considered.

## Required Application Documents

Applicants must provide the following materials:

### 1. Personal Data

Standard personal information (Name, address, date of birth, etc.).

### 2. Letter of Motivation

A statement (in English, max 500 words) explaining your current goals, career plans, and interest in the doctoral program in Business. You should discuss your study plans and fields of interest.

### 3. Certificates and Transcripts of Grades

- A transcript is required from *each* college or university attended; the degree certificate alone is insufficient.
- The transcript must detail courses, grades, and degrees received, noting any rank or distinction.
- **Translations:** Any transcript not in English or German requires translation. At the application stage, notarization or apostille is not necessary. If accepted, notarized translations will be required.
- **Incomplete Studies:** If you have not finished your current degree, please attach a preliminary grade report (in English or German).

### 4. Standardized Tests (GMAT or GRE)

- All applicants must upload their test-taker's copy in the online portal and send the scores via the testing service.
- While GMAT is preferred, adequate GRE scores are acceptable.
- There is no minimum required score.
- **Program Codes:**
  - GMAT Customer Service: VLC-95-89
  - Educational Testing Service (ETS): 0933

### 5. Letters of Recommendation

Two letters of recommendation are required from faculty members who can assess your academic ability and potential.

### 6. Writing Sample

Please attach a writing sample (in English or German), such as a bachelor's/master's thesis or a longer paper from your studies, to demonstrate your research capabilities.

### 7. Supplementary Information

Any other information relevant to your application.

## Specific Program Option: Mannheim Master in Management

Students enrolled in the Mannheim Master in Management have the option to participate in the CDSB first-year program via the "Business Research" Program Option.

- **Application Deadline:** 15 May 2025
- For full details, please consult the respective [study regulation](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/examinations/rechtliche-grundlagen/examination-regulations/examination-regulations-for-masters-programs/).
