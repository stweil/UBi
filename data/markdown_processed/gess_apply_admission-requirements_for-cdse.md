# Admission to the CDSE Doctoral Program

This guide outlines the application process, required documents, and key dates for the doctoral program in Economics at the CDSE.

## 📅 Key Dates and Deadlines

- **Early Admission:** Applications can be submitted starting **January 15, 2026**.
- **Application Deadline:** The final deadline for the Fall 2026 intake is **March 31, 2026**.
- **Review Process:** Candidates not accepted in the early round will have their applications reviewed with the general pool after March 31, 2026.

**Funding:** CDSE commits to funding admitted students for five years, contingent upon successful course completion and academic standing. This funding may include scholarships in the first two years and research assistant positions in subsequent years.

**Application Submission:** Applications must be submitted exclusively via the [Online application portal](https://gess-application.uni-mannheim.de/portal/). Applications sent by mail will not be considered.

## 🎓 Admission Requirements

Admission is based purely on academic performance and potential.

### Prerequisites

In principle, applicants must hold a Master’s degree in the respective field or an equivalent qualification. Candidates from neighboring or quantitative fields (e.g., mathematics or engineering) may be considered if their background is deemed sufficient.

### Required Application Documents

1. **Personal Data:** Full name, address, date of birth, etc.
1. **Letter of Motivation:** A statement (in English, max 500 words) detailing current goals, career plans, and interest in the doctoral program. Applicants must discuss their study plans and fields of interest.
1. **Certificates and Transcripts of Grades:**
   - A transcript from *each* college or university is mandatory; the degree certificate alone is insufficient.
   - The transcript must detail courses, grades, and degrees received, noting any rank or distinction.
   - **Translations:** Transcripts in languages other than English or German require translation. No notarization or apostille is needed at this initial stage, but notarized translations *are* required upon acceptance.
   - **Incomplete Degrees:** If the current degree is not finished, attach a preliminary grade report (in English or German).
1. **GRE Scores:**
   - All applicants must upload the General GRE test-taker's copy via the online portal and send it through the testing service.
   - **Note:** Subject tests are not accepted, and the GMAT cannot substitute for the GRE.
   - Scores must not be older than two years. There is no minimum required score.
   - Please request ETS to report scores using code: **0933**.
   - *Warning: The GRE General Test taken at home is not accepted.*
1. **Letters of Recommendation:** Two letters are required from faculty members who can assess the applicant's academic ability and potential.
1. **Writing Sample:** A sample paper (in English or German) from the applicant's studies.
1. **Supplementary Information:** Any other relevant documents.

*For a comprehensive checklist, please consult the detailed [application requirements PDF](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/media/Einrichtungen/GESS/Dokumente/CDSE/CDSE_Application_Requirements_2025.pdf).*
