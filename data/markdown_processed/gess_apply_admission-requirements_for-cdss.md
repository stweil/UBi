# Admission to the CDSS Program

The CDSS and the School of Social Sciences emphasize an **empirical-analytical approach, which is informed by theory** and relies on **quantitative methods** for research and teaching.

## 🗓️ Application Timeline and Requirements

### Eligibility

- Highly qualified students of all nationalities holding a very good **research-oriented Master's degree** are welcome to apply.
- Applications from students with excellent (4-year) Bachelor degrees and applicants from neighboring fields are considered, provided their background is deemed sufficient.
- All applications are evaluated solely on the basis of qualification and scientific potential.

### Key Deadlines

- **Application Opening:** Applications for the fall semester open in **mid-November** of the previous year.
- **Early Application Deadline:** January 15, 2026. (At least one Letter of Recommendation must reach the university by this date.)
- **Final Application Deadline:** March 31, 2026.

**Important Notes:**

- Applications sent via e-mail will not be considered.
- Candidates not accepted in the early round will be reconsidered with all other applications after the final deadline.
- All applications must be submitted via the [Online application portal](https://gess-application.uni-mannheim.de/portal/).

## 📄 Required Application Documents

A complete application must include the following components:

### 1. Letter of Motivation

- Must be written in **English** and not exceed 700 words.
- Explain your current goals, career plans, and reasons for interest in a structured doctoral program in Social Science.
- Elaborate on your study plans and research interests, specifically showing how they align with the current research of the faculty member(s) you wish to work with.

### 2. Writing Sample

- Attach a writing sample of up to 10 pages (maximum 3500 words, bibliography excluded).
- The sample can be in **English or German** (e.g., a paper from your studies or an essay related to your research).
- **Do not** submit a research or dissertation proposal as the writing sample.

### 3. Certificates and Transcripts of Grades

- A transcript and degree certificate from *each* academic degree program attended is required.
- These documents **must include** the university's grading scheme.
- The transcript must detail courses attended, grades received, and the degree awarded (mentioning rank or distinction, if applicable).
- A translation is required for any transcript not in English or German.
- If you are currently studying, please attach a preliminary grade report (in English or German).

### 4. Language Proficiency Scores

Applicants whose native language is not English must provide proof of proficiency:

- **TOEFL:** Minimum score of 600 (paper test) or 100 (internet-based test). Scores are valid for five years. Ask ETS to report scores using code: 0933.
- **IELTS:** Minimum overall band score of 7.

### 5. Letters of Recommendation

- Two letters are required from faculty, scholars, or individuals who can assess your potential for academic research and teaching.
- **Crucial:** Contact your referees *before* applying to confirm their availability and willingness to write a reference.
- It is the **applicant's responsibility** to ensure these references reach the university by the specified deadlines.
