# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This guide addresses common questions regarding the application process for the program.

## 📝 Before You Apply

### Eligibility and Requirements

- **Document Review:** We do not assess any documents (resumes, qualifications) prior to submission or provide a forecast on your chances of admission. Please review the specific admission criteria for your field: [Business](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/apply/admission-requirements/for-cdsb/), [Economics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/apply/admission-requirements/for-cdse/), or [Social Sciences](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/apply/admission-requirements/for-cdss/).
- **Tuition Fees:** There are no tuition fees, but a small semester fee of 194 Euro is required per term (2025/26).
- **Funding Opportunities:** Students accepted into the program are guaranteed funding for the duration. The length varies (3 years in Social Sciences to 5 years in Economics), which may involve a scholarship in the first year and research assistant positions in subsequent years. [Further information on student financing](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/about/student-financing/).
- **Research Proposal:** A research proposal is **not** required at the time of application. However, your motivation letter must clearly demonstrate your research interests and how they align with the current research of the faculty members you wish to work with.
- **Supervisor:** You are **not** required to find a supervisor before the semester begins.
- **Language Requirement:** No, you do not need to speak German for admission, as the program is taught entirely in English.

## 📂 Application Materials Checklist

### Required Documentation

- **Transcripts:** Applicants must upload transcripts into the online application system. Official hard copy/paper transcripts are **not** required at the time of application.
- **Writing Sample:** Please attach a writing sample of up to 10 pages (maximum 3500 words, bibliography excluded). Acceptable examples include a paper from your studies, a representative excerpt from your thesis, or an essay related to your research interests.
- **Work Experience:** You are required to upload a reference issued by your employer for the Work Experience section.
- **Stays Abroad:** While we are most interested in academic stays abroad, we will also consider work experiences listed in this section.

### Standardized Tests

Depending on your intended program, you must provide the following test scores:

- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Business:** GMAT or GRE (No exemption)
- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics:** GRE (No exemption)
- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Social Studies:** TOEFL or IELTS

For detailed information, please refer to the [admission page of the respective program](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/apply/).

### Submission Guidelines

- **Test Scores Submission:** Upload your test-taker's copy in the online application, but you must also ask the testing service to send the official scores to the university.
- **TOEFL Exemption (CDSS):** If your previous degree was exclusively taught in English, you can submit an official letter from your university confirming this.
- **Incomplete Degrees:**
  - **Master's Thesis:** You can apply even if your Master's thesis is not yet complete, provided you submit proof that the degree will be finalized by November 30th of the year you are applying.
  - **Final Transcripts:** If you are completing your Bachelor's/Master's degree by August 202X, submit your most recent transcript.
- **Multiple Documents:** If the system only allows one upload slot, merge all necessary PDFs (e.g., transcripts and degree certificates) into a single document.
- **Deadlines:**
  - **Late Submissions:** Submissions after the application deadline will **not** be accepted.
  - **Missing Documents:** Do not submit your application if required documents are missing prior to the deadline.

## ✉️ Letters of Recommendation & Troubleshooting

### Letters of Recommendation

- **Quantity:** Two letters of recommendation are required. These must come from faculty or scholars who can assess your potential for academic research and teaching.
- **Submission Method:** We only accept reference letters submitted via our online system.
- **Referee Contact Issues:**
  - If your referees have not been contacted, ask them to check their spam folders. If the request is missing, contact us at [gess.application@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:gess.application@uni-mannheim.de) and we will resend the request.
  - To change referee details, please email [gess.application@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:gess.application@uni-mannheim.de) with the new contact information.

### Application Corrections & Technical Issues

- **Making Corrections:** If you realize you made a mistake after submitting your application, write an email to [gess.application@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:gess.application@uni-mannheim.de) clearly indicating the correction needed and the section it pertains to.
- **Technical Errors:** If you encounter a technical error, email [gess.application@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:gess.application@uni-mannheim.de) describing the error in as much detail as possible, and attach a screenshot if possible. Our IT support will contact you once the issue is resolved.

## 🎙️ Invited for Interview? (CDSS Only)

### Preparing for the Interview

The first meeting with a potential supervisor can be stressful, so treat it like any job interview and prepare thoroughly.

- **Research:** Review the potential supervisor’s webpage beforehand, as answers may already be available there.
- **Format:** There is no fixed format; every supervisor has a unique style. While questions will focus on research, remember that the interview is also your opportunity to interview the professor to ensure mutual fit.
- **Questions to Ask (Suggestions):** Consider asking about:
  - Opportunities for active involvement in their projects.
  - Their supervision style (e.g., 'hands-off' vs. weekly meetings).
  - How often they meet with doctoral students.
  - The average duration of a dissertation project.
  - Where the team typically aims to publish research.
  - How to organize contact with current doctoral students in their chair.

### Offer Without Interview

If you receive an offer but no interview was held, you have two options:

1. If you already know the professor and are satisfied with the offer, you are in a strong position.
1. If this is not the case, consider proactively contacting the potential supervisor to request a brief meeting to ensure you can make an informed decision.
