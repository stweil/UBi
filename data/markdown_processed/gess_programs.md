# Doctoral Programs at the Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS)

The Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS) offers 11 internationally competitive and research-oriented doctoral programs spanning business, economics, and social sciences.

These full-time programs typically last between three to five years and are designed to prepare students for academic careers at leading international universities and research institutions. GESS fosters a vibrant and international research community, currently comprising over 230 students from more than 40 countries.

## Doctoral Centers and Programs

The PhD programs are administered through specialized centers in collaboration with relevant departments at the University of Mannheim:

- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB)**

  - Administers the PhD program at the [Business School](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/) of the University of Mannheim.
  - *Accreditations:* The Business School is triple accredited by AACSB, EFDM (EQUIS), and AMBA.

- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE)**

  - Administers the PhD program at the [Department of Economics](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/) of the University of Mannheim.

- **Center for Doctoral Studies in Social and Behavioral Sciences (CDSS)**

  - Administers PhD programs in Political Science, Psychology, and Sociology.
  - This center works in close collaboration with the [School of Social Sciences](https://www.sowi.uni-mannheim.de/en/) at the University of Mannheim.
