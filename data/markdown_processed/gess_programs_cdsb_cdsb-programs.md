# Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB) Programs

The Center for Doctoral Studies in Business offers seven specialized fields of study:

- [Accounting](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/accounting/)
- [Finance](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/finance/)
- [Information Systems](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/information-systems/)
- [Management](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/management/)
- [Marketing](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/marketing/)
- [Operations Management](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/operations-management/)
- [Taxation](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/cdsb-programs/taxation/)

## Program Structure

The full-time doctoral program in business is divided into two main phases: the course phase and the research phase.

### Course Phase

This phase involves core and elective courses. Students must complete all scheduled core courses outlined in the [CDSB Program Regulations](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/student-information/regulations/). These courses teach essential empirical and quantitative research tools. Regular participation in the respective area seminar is mandatory starting from the first semester.

Additionally, students:

- Attend elective courses within their field of specialization.
- May choose courses from other CDSB study programs.
- Must take the program’s English Academic Writing course.
- Must complete a bridge course via the [Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/course-catalog/course-catalog/) or the [Center for Doctoral Studies in Behavioral and Social Sciences (CDSS)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/course-catalog/).

At the end of the first year, students must submit their dissertation proposal.

### Research Phase

During the thesis writing phase, students work independently on their research agenda under close supervision from dedicated faculty members. Students are expected to write independent research papers, present them in seminar form, and submit them to international conferences and journals.

## Program Requirements Timeline

The successful completion of the CDSB programs requires meeting specific milestones across the years:

**Year One**

- Passing exams in all scheduled courses.
- Attending the Area Seminar.
- Submitting the Dissertation Proposal by August 31st.

**Year Two**

- Passing exams in scheduled courses, including the Bridge Course from the CDSE or CDSS course catalogue.
- Attending the Area Seminar.
- Passing the English Academic Writing Course (3 ECTS).

**Year Three Onwards**

- Maintaining regular participation in Area Seminars.

*Grading Scheme:* The CDSB uses a grading scale from 1.0 (excellent) to 4.0 (pass), with 5.0 indicating a fail. Differentiation is possible in 0.3 steps (e.g., 1.3, 1.7).

## Degree Requirements and Award

To earn a doctoral degree, the following steps must be completed:

1. Completion of the entire CDSB course program.
1. Completion, submission, and acceptance of the thesis.
1. Successful thesis defense.

The degree awarded is **Dr. rer. pol.**

**Thesis Format:**
Doctoral theses typically consist of three original essays focused on one area, intended for publication in esteemed scholarly journals. However, the classical dissertation format (a longer text analyzing one topic in depth) is also permissible, provided the content is structured for future journal articles. The final format decision is made jointly by the student and their supervisor. For detailed submission guidelines, please consult the [Degree Process](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/for-active-students/cdsb/degree-process/).

## Doctoral Titles in Germany

In Germany, doctoral degrees often use specific Latin designations depending on the field of study.

- **Faculty of Business and Economics:** The title conferred is *Dr. rer. pol* (doctor rerum politicarum).
- **Faculty of Social Sciences:** The title conferred is *Dr. rer. soc.* (doctor rerum socialium).

Both titles are awarded after an intensive study and research period culminating in the successful submission and defense of a doctoral thesis.
