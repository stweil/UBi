# Doctoral Program in Accounting

The doctoral program in Accounting is a competitive program covering three core fields: Financial Accounting, Managerial Accounting, and Auditing.

## Curriculum and Competencies

The curriculum is uniquely rich, providing doctoral students with:

- Core competencies in analytical, empirical, and normative research methods.
- Profound knowledge of the relevant institutional details within the field.
- Integration with other disciplines, including law and finance, into a coherent curriculum that conforms to top international standards.

## Academic Department and Research Focus

The accounting department is situated within the "Accounting & Taxation" area at the Business School of the University of Mannheim. This department is recognized as one of the top-ranked business departments in Germany.

Faculty members in this area conduct high-level scientific research and applied research that contributes significantly to industry, standard setting, and political discourse.
