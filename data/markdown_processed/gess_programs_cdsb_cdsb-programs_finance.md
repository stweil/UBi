# Finance Program Overview and Research Areas

The field of Finance is highly interdisciplinary, possessing a strong theoretical foundation, broad empirical basis, and significant practical relevance. It addresses fundamental problems concerning individual decisions regarding risky financial instruments. From classical and behavioral perspectives, it analyzes the existence, optimal management, and regulation of financial institutions (like banks and insurance companies), and studies the structure and valuation of optimal financial contracts under various market regimes. Methodologically, the field successfully combines advanced mathematical modeling techniques with numerical solution procedures, econometric estimation methods, and statistical testing. Finance is unique in economics for its deep confrontation of theoretical results with empirical facts, facilitated by excellent market databases, which are often supplemented by data on individual decision-making and non-traded financial contracts, all available at the University of Mannheim.

## Core Research Fields

The research conducted by the [Finance Group](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/finance/) (including faculty such as Albrecht, [Bucher-Koenen](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/bucher-koenen/), [Maug](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/maug/), [Niessen-Ruenzi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/niessen-ruenzi/), [Ruenzi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/ruenzi/), [Spalt](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/spalt/), [Theissen](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/theissen/), [von Thadden](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/von-thadden/) (Economics), and [Weber](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/weber/)) covers all major areas of finance:

- **Asset Pricing:** Focuses on theories of fair values for financial instruments and the impact of price, credit, and liquidity risk on asset prices, contributing to a deeper understanding of capital market price movements.
- **Behavioral Finance:** An increasingly popular field that examines how individuals form expectations and behave. While traditionally focused on retail investors, it now analyzes the behavior of corporate managers and professional investors as well.
- **Corporate Finance & Governance:** Corporate Finance deals with the optimal structuring of contracts involving agents, equity, and debt holders under conditions of symmetric and asymmetric information. Corporate Governance addresses the rules and regulations governing the firm and its optimal control.
- **Investment Management:** Examines optimal investment strategies for both individual investors and institutions, as well as the dynamics and challenges that arise when investment decisions are delegated (e.g., to mutual or hedge funds).
- **Risk Management:** Closely related to asset pricing, banking, and corporate finance, this area studies the exposure of both financial and non-financial firms to market, credit, liquidity, operational, and economic risks.

## Program Structure and Curriculum

The Finance curriculum is designed to reflect the demanding quantitative methods used in these fields, built upon strong microeconomic foundations.

**First Semester Core Courses:**
The initial courses establish a strong foundation in the relevant mathematical, econometric, and microeconomic principles of finance. The course on “Discrete-Time Finance” specifically covers the central problem of allocating consumption and investment from a financial viewpoint, including no-arbitrage equilibria, linear pricing rules, pricing kernels, and the change of numéraire.

**Second Semester Core Courses:**
The second semester delves into the major subfields of Finance, covering:

- Individual decision-making (under neoclassical and behavioral views).
- Investment-consumption decisions and asset pricing in continuous time.
- Theoretical and empirical corporate finance problems.

The course **“Econometrics of Financial Markets”** extends the general econometrics course to address typical financial issues, such as estimating dynamic market processes, testing asset pricing and market microstructure models, and designing event studies.

**Research Engagement:**
Throughout both semesters, students participate in the Finance research seminar series. This provides regular presentations by renowned researchers from top global universities, allowing students to engage with cutting-edge research from day one.
