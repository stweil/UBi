# Management Area Research Fields

The Management Area within the Business School of the University of Mannheim covers a broad spectrum of research fields, including corporate social responsibility, human resource management, international management, public and non-profit management, and small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). Research in this area requires an interfunctional and interdisciplinary approach, drawing heavily from business administration, economics, and the social sciences. Given that insights often originate from industrial practice, survey design and data collection methods play a prominent role in business strategy and management research.

The area conducts fundamental research as well as cutting-edge research applied to industry, maintaining a dynamic environment essential for state-of-the-art teaching.

## Specialized Research Chairs

The research focus is distributed across several specialized chairs:

### Chair of Strategic and International Management

Research interests center on:

- Strategic corporate transformation and restructuring.
- Corporate strategy and governance.
- Management of growth.
- Effectiveness of management tools and practices in strategy formulation and implementation.
- Strategic decision-making.
  *(Associated Faculty: [Brauer](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/brauer/))*

### Chair of Business Administration, Human Resource Management and Leadership

Main research fields include:

- Leadership studies.
- Strategic and international human resource management.
- Careers and career patterns.
- Methods in management research.
- Knowledge transfer in teams.
  *(Associated Faculty: [Biemann](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/biemann/))*

### Chair of Business Administration, Public and Nonprofit Management

This chair focuses on developing and improving management concepts and instruments for:

- Public administrations.
- State-owned enterprises.
- Private nonprofit organizations.
  Key research fields are strategic management, human resource management, and organization theory for the public and nonprofit sectors.
  *(Associated Faculty: [Helmig](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/public-and-nonprofit-management/))*

### Chair of SME Research and Entrepreneurship

Focus research fields cover:

- Management concepts specific to small and medium-sized businesses.
- The role of information and communication technology in SMEs.
- Family businesses and self-employment.
- Entrepreneurship.
  *(Associated Faculty: [Woywode](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/woywode/))*

### Chair of Organization and Innovation

Main research fields include:

- Organizational creativity & innovation.
- Knowledge management.
- Gender gaps in science and innovation.
- IP strategy & management.
- Entrepreneurial finance.
  *(Associated Faculty: [Hoisl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/hoisl/))*
