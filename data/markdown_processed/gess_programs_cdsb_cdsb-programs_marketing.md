# Marketing Area: Research and Teaching Excellence

The Marketing area at the University of Mannheim covers the discipline of marketing through both research and teaching. The core focus areas include **marketing management**, **marketing strategy**, and **consumer behavior**.

The faculty, including professors such as [Homburg](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/homburg/), [Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/kraus/), [Kuester](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/kuester/), and [Stahl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/quantitativemarketing/), strives for excellence by delivering cutting-edge insights in the classroom and producing significant scholarly contributions, positioning the department highly within Europe.

## Areas of Expertise and Research Fields

The full-time faculty represents the marketing discipline across several specialized areas of expertise, including:

- Sales Management
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Consumer Behavior
- Strategic Marketing
- Services Marketing
- International Marketing
- Business-to-Business (B2B) Marketing
- Branding
- Advertising
- Innovation Marketing
- Quantitative Marketing
- Market Research
