# Taxation Program Overview

The Taxation program places a strong emphasis on demanding quantitative methods, which serve as the essential toolkit for state-of-the-art research in this field.

## Curriculum Structure

The program is structured to provide students with a comprehensive foundation before specializing:

- **First Semester:** Students gain fundamental knowledge of relevant mathematical, econometric, and microeconomic principles.
- **Second Semester:** The focus shifts to core insights and the current research frontiers of taxation research.
- **Electives and Research:** Subsequently, students develop their specific strengths and interests through elective courses. The coursework then gradually phases out to create ample room for the students’ own research agendas.

## Research Collaboration and Expertise

Graduate students work closely with researchers affiliated to the [Leibniz ScienceCampus MannheimTaxation](https://www.mannheimtaxation.eu/mannheim-taxation/home). This campus is a joint initiative between the University of Mannheim and the ZEW – Leibniz Centre for European Economic Research, designed to consolidate expertise across multiple disciplines:

- Business Administration
- Economics
- Law
- Political Science

### Key Research Areas

MaTax researchers contribute to a wide range of topics within tax research, including:

- Company taxation
- Public finance
- Innovation taxation
- Taxation of the financial sector
- Tax harmonization in the European Union
- International and European tax law
- Tax competition
- Behavioral responses to taxation
- Tax incidence
