## 🔬 Core Research Methods & Skills (General Offerings)

These courses focus on developing advanced research skills applicable across multiple disciplines.

### Systematic Literature Review Methods (Cross-Disciplinary)

This course provides participants with an intensive introduction to systematic literature reviews, a fundamental and independent research genre. Students learn to critically evaluate existing reviews and develop their own systematic review protocols using guidelines like PRISMA.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Participants complete a systematic review related to their PhD topic by the end of the course.
- **Assessment:** Class Participation & Discussion (20%), Presentation (10%), Paper (70%).
- **Schedule:** Varies (e.g., 12.02.25 – 28.05.25, Wednesday, 13:45 – 17:00).

### Empirical Data Analysis & Causal Inference (General)

This reading course offers a hands-on, paper-based approach to data analysis, focusing on experimental designs and causal inference. It emphasizes moving beyond standard econometric assumptions to examine the interplay between sampling and experimental methods.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Develop skills in designing questionnaires and survey experiments, data analysis, and quantitative methods.
- **Assessment:** Varies (e.g., Technical Report, Presentation).
- **Schedule:** Varies (e.g., 12.02.25 – 28.05.25, Wednesday, 13:45 – 17:00).

### Data Science & Textual Analysis Lab

This lab guides students through the full cycle of a scientific data-science project, with a specific focus on analyzing textual data (e.g., news or social media) using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques.

- **Learning Goals:** Practical experience in data-science projects, presentation skills, and research communication.
- **Assessment:** Written elaboration (90%) and presentation (10%).
- **Schedule:** Varies (e.g., 11.02.25 – 27.05.25, Tuesday, 15:30 – 17:00).

## 💰 Finance & Accounting Research

### Empirical Accounting Research Methods

A comprehensive overview of research topics and methods in empirical accounting, covering seminal and contemporary literature. Topics include Earnings Management, Valuation, Voluntary/Mandatory Disclosure, IFRS, and Corporate Narratives.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Ability to critically evaluate current research, place new research into the literature, and develop meaningful research ideas.
- **Assessment:** Exam (50%), Paper Presentations (50%).
- **Prerequisites:** Recommended: Empirical Accounting Research I, Advanced Econometrics I, Applied Econometrics.

### Advanced Empirical Tax Research

Focuses on core empirical methods in business taxation, including Potential Outcome Framework, RDD, IV, Discrete Choice Models, and Difference-in-Difference.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Proficiency in causal identification methods for tax research and ability to execute research projects.
- **Assessment:** Presentations (40%), Research Paper (40%), Participation (20%).
- **Prerequisites:** Advanced Econometrics I or Applied Econometrics I.

### Climate/Sustainable Finance Seminar

Introduces graduate students to research questions and methods in the rapidly evolving fields of climate/sustainable finance.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Understanding current research paradigms and identifying promising avenues for future research in sustainability.
- **Assessment:** Participation (20%), Paper presentations and discussions (80%).
- **Prerequisites:** Assumes background in core graduate-level finance.

### Behavioral Finance

Reviews recent research on how psychological biases affect financial decisions, moving beyond the standard rational investor paradigm.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Understanding how behavioral findings integrate with neo-classical theory to explain market anomalies.
- **Assessment:** Exam (60 minutes).
- **Prerequisites:** Formal: E 701, E 703, FIN 801.

### Finance Research Seminar (General)

A seminar format discussing current research in finance, presenting working papers from academia and industry.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Ability to follow and discuss current research topics, developing own research ideas.
- **Assessment:** Oral participation.

## 💼 Management & Marketing Research

### Management Research Seminar

A seminar discussing the latest advances and challenges in the transition towards a decarbonized energy economy, covering sustainability activities and clean energy technologies.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Introduction to current research paradigms and training in presenting/evaluating papers in seminars and conferences.
- **Assessment:** Participation (20%), Paper presentations and discussions (80%).

### Strategic Management Theory Seminar

Focuses on the most relevant theoretical perspectives in strategic management research, discussing theoretical lenses, assumptions, and conceptual strengths.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Developing advanced understanding of established theories and learning to build substantial theoretical contributions.
- **Assessment:** Essay (50%), Presentation (50%).

### Operations Management Empirical Research

Provides an overview of empirical research approaches in operations management, focusing on moving from a basic research question to an appropriate research design and methodology.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Competence to initiate, design, implement, and evaluate empirical research in operations management.
- **Assessment:** Oral exam (60%), Presentation (40%).

### Advances in Marketing Research

A highly critical course designed to prepare doctoral candidates for publishing in leading journals. Students learn to accept and utilize rigorous academic criticism.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Developing research judgment and skills to execute scientific research on behavioral and organizational issues in marketing.
- **Assessment:** Essay (50%), Presentation (30%), Discussion/Simulation (20%).

## 📊 Cross-Disciplinary & Specialized Topics

### Information Systems (IS) Research Seminar

Discusses current research topics in IS and Operations Management, focusing on the variety of empirical and theoretical research methods.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Ability to follow and discuss current research topics in IS and OM, developing own research ideas.
- **Assessment:** Oral Participation.

### Theoretical Contributions in IS Research

This course guides participants through the nature and role of theory in IS research, helping students build and extend their understanding of theory construction.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Understanding the importance of theory, learning theorizing strategies, and developing basic theorizing skills.
- **Assessment:** Written elaboration (60%), Presentation (20%), Discussion (20%).

### Entrepreneurship & Management Experiments

Focuses on experiments as a key tool in entrepreneurship and management research. Students learn to design, implement, and analyze their own experimental studies.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Ability to design and conduct experimental studies, culminating in a short paper for the PhD thesis.
- **Assessment:** Presentation (20%), Term Paper (80%).

### Taxation Research Seminar (General)

A forum for discussing recent state-of-the-art papers in applied empirical taxation research.

- **Learning Outcomes:** Developing critical evaluation skills, understanding the literature, and building a reading routine for the thesis.
- **Assessment:** Paper (referee report) (40%), Presentation (30%), Class Participation (30%).
