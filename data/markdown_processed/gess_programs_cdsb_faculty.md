# The Business School, University of Mannheim

The Business School at the University of Mannheim is one of the largest and most renowned business schools in Europe. It boasts a large faculty, including over 36 fully ordained professors, one senior professor, numerous assistant professors, and more than 200 additional faculty members, serving approximately 3,500 students.

The school's core principles, known as the "Mannheim Philosophy," focus on research and teaching at the highest international level, consistent quality management, international focus, and distinctive practical orientation. Research efforts are supported by major institutional grants from the German Research Foundation (DFG) and European Union research and training grants, supplemented by funds from other public and private institutions.

## Associated Research and Academic Centers

The Business School maintains extensive international contacts and is associated with numerous important research centers located in Mannheim, fostering strong dialogue between academia and industry.

- **Institute for Small and Medium Enterprise Research (ifm)**
  The ifm conducts specialized research on current and structural issues related to Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs).
  [Learn more about ifm](http://www.institut-fuer-mittelstandsforschung.de/)

- **Institute for Market-Oriented Management (IMU)**
  IMU serves as a key forum for dialogue between scientific theory and practical application. Its research aims to generate scientific results highly relevant to market-oriented management.
  [Visit IMU](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/imu/)

- **Institute for Enterprise Systems (InES)**
  InES actively promotes the valuable exchange between science and practice within the field of enterprise systems. The institute was jointly founded by the Ministry of Science, Research and Arts of Baden-Württemberg (MWK) and the University of Mannheim.
  [Learn more about InES](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/ines/)

- **Leibniz ScienceCampus MannheimTax**
  This campus unites outstanding expertise in corporate taxation, public finance, and fiscal law. It intensifies the dialogue between researchers and industry representatives, supporting both theoretical perspectives and the development of new research talent.
  [Visit MannheimTax](https://www.mannheimtaxation.eu/)

- **Management Analytics Center**
  The Management Analytics Center assists companies by providing research and practical transfer services. It helps organizations identify potentials, introduce new methods, and make data-driven decisions.
  [Details on Management Analytics](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/management-analytics/)

- **Mannheim Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation (MCEI)**
  MCEI provides a dedicated platform for young entrepreneurs, potential founders, students, sponsors, and investors to connect and develop innovative ideas.
  [Visit MCEI](https://www.mcei.de/)

- **Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)**
  MISES examines the challenges and innovation opportunities arising from the global transition toward a decarbonized energy future.
  [Learn more about MISES](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/mises/)

- **Centre for European Economic Research (ZEW)**
  ZEW is recognized as a leading institute for applied economic research in Europe. It focuses on internationally comparative issues within the European context and maintains several important databases.
  [Visit ZEW](https://www.zew.de/en/)
