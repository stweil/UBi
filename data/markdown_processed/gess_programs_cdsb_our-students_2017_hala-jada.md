# Hala Jada

Hala Jada is a Research Assistant at the University of Mannheim. Her academic background and professional experience have driven her interest in accounting research, particularly focusing on financial accounting and empirical research.

**Supervisor:** [Dirk Simons](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/simons/)

## Academic Background & Experience

- **Origin:** Born in Palestine (1991).
- **Undergraduate Studies:** Graduated with a degree in accounting from Birzeit University-Palestine.
- **Graduate Studies:** Received the HESPAL award, enabling her to pursue an MSc degree in Accounting and Finance with distinction at the University of Manchester, UK.
- **Professional Experience:** Following her studies, she worked for three years as an instructor in the accounting department at Birzeit University.
- **Motivation:** Her studies and work experience fostered a strong interest in accounting research, leading her to Mannheim to pursue a structured PhD program.
