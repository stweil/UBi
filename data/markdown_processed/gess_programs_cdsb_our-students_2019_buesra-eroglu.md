# Büşra Eroğlu, M.Sc.

Büşra Eroğlu is a Doctoral Student in the Business School at the University of Mannheim. She is currently part of the Finance Track of the Center for Doctoral Studies in Business.

## Academic Profile & Research Interests

Büşra is passionate about interdisciplinary work in **Behavioral Finance**, combining psychology and economics to analyze markets and financial decision-making processes.

- **Research Interests:**
  - Behavioral Finance
  - Asset Pricing
- **Master's Thesis Focus:** She developed a credit mechanism aimed at encouraging pessimistic entrepreneurs to participate in economic activities.

### Education

- **Bachelor's Degree:** Mathematics from Marmara University (Istanbul, Turkey).
- **Master's Degree:** Economics from Istanbul Bilgi University.

### Professional Experience

Previously, Büşra worked as a research and teaching assistant at Bahçeşehir University in Turkey.
