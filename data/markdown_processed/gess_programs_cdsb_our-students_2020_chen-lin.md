# Chen Lin, M.Sc.

**Role:** PhD Student
**Affiliation:** University of Mannheim, Business School

## 🎓 Academic Profile & Background

Chen Lin is a PhD student with a strong background in finance and economics.

- **Education:**
  - Completed his Bachelor's degree from the Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2013.
  - Studied for a Master of Economics and Management Science at the Humboldt University of Berlin. During his master's studies, he served as a student assistant for both the Finance Faculty and the International Research Training Group 1792 (“High Dimensional Nonstationary Time Series”).
- **Professional Experience:** Prior to joining the GESS, he worked for five years as a logistics analyst and subsequently as a buy-side equity research analyst.
- **Origin:** Born in China.

## 🔬 Research Interests

His primary research interests lie in quantitative and behavioral finance:

- Asset pricing
- Behavioral finance
- Investment management
