# Yufang Sun, M.Sc.

Yufang "Sabrina" Sun is a PhD Student at the University of Mannheim Business School. Her research focuses on the intersection of social psychology and economic decision-making.

## 🔬 Forschungsschwerpunkte (Research Interests)

Yufang's academic focus lies in applying social science theories and experimental methods to the study of economic and financial behavior.

- Social preferences in economic behaviors
- Experimental methods

During her master's thesis at the Chair of Financial Markets and Financial Institutions at Mannheim, she developed a strong interest in the role of social preferences in economic behaviors, a line of research she intends to pursue through her doctoral studies.

## 🎓 Akademischer Werdegang (Academic Background)

Yufang grew up in Dalian, a harbor city on the Chinese East Coast. Her educational journey includes:

- **University of Mannheim:** Studied the Business Bachelor and MMM programs.
- **Indiana University Bloomington:** Obtained bachelor’s degrees in Psychology and Mathematics.
- **Harvard University:** Worked as a full-time research fellow, investigating the social psychological underpinnings of institutional corruption.

Beyond her academic pursuits, Yufang is passionate about creating fusion cuisine and introducing children to Chinese language and culture.
