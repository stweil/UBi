# Nicola Schmitt

**Research Associate**
University of Mannheim, Schloss, Ehrenhof Ost, 68161 Mannheim

Nicola Schmitt is a doctoral candidate in the Management track of the Center for Doctoral Studies in Business at the Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences. She commenced her studies in September 2024.

## Forschungsschwerpunkte (Research Focus)

Nicola's doctoral research focuses on the intersection of **Digital Transformation** and **Innovation**, with a specific emphasis on the practical applications of **Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI)** within organizational settings.

**Interests include:**

- Innovation
- Digital Transformation
- Entrepreneurship
- Organizational Behavior

## Akademischer Werdegang und Erfahrung (Academic Background and Experience)

Nicola Schmitt was born in Rheinfelden-Baden, Germany. Her academic journey includes:

- **Master of Business Administration (M.B.A.):** Specialization in Corporate Development.
- **Bachelor of Business Administration:** University of Cologne.

During her studies, she gained diverse international and professional experience:

- **International Experience:** Completed a semester abroad at ALBA Graduate Business School in Athens, Greece, where she focused on Strategic Management for Shipping Services.
- **Professional Experience:** Gained practical insights through internships in the consulting industry, including a placement at [EY Switzerland](https://www.ey.com/en_ch/services/consulting).
- **Research Experience:** Served as a research assistant at the Chair of Organizational Behavior.
