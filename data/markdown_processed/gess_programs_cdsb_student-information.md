# Information for Prospective Students: CDSB Doctoral Program

This page provides an overview of key topics for prospective students interested in the Doctoral Program at the CDSB.

**Note for Active Students:**
For information relevant to currently enrolled students, please visit the [page for active students](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/for-active-students/).

**Student Guidebook:**
Prospective students are strongly advised to consult the comprehensive **CDSB Student Guidebook**. This guide covers general information about GESS, the CDSB program structure, details for international students, and living in Mannheim. The new edition is scheduled for publication in August 2024.

**Contact:**
For any questions not covered on this website or in the Guidebook, current and prospective students should contact the Center Management of the CDSB via email.
