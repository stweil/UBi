# CDSB Doctoral Student Awards

The Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB) annually honors its doctoral students through the CDSB Doctoral Student Awards. These awards recognize outstanding achievements across various areas, including research quality, academic performance, publications, and teaching contributions.

## Arten der Auszeichnungen

### Best Paper Award

This award recognizes an outstanding paper that has been accepted or has received an invitation for resubmission in a peer-reviewed journal.

- [CDSB Best Paper Award](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/student-information/cdsb-awards/)

### Award for Interdisciplinarity

This award is given to recognize outstanding performances and innovative projects by doctoral students in business that successfully foster interdisciplinarity.

- [2025 CDSB Interdisciplinary Award](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/student-information/cdsb-awards/)

### Teaching Award

This award honors the substantial contributions of CDSB doctoral students to teaching and learning activities at the Business School.

- [2025 CDSB Teaching Award](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/student-information/cdsb-awards/)
