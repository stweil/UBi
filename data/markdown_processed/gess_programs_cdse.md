# Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE)

The Center for Doctoral Studies in Economics (CDSE) administers the doctoral program at the [Department of Economics](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/) at the University of Mannheim.

[Learn more about the CDSE program](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/cdse-program/).

## ZEW Research Track Collaboration

The CDSE also runs a specialized doctoral track in cooperation with the [Centre for European Economic Research (ZEW)](https://www.zew.de/en/). This track is designed to provide participants with two key areas of expertise:

1. An excellent academic education through the CDSE program within the GESS.
1. Key skills in the field of economic policy advice, an area where ZEW holds a strong international reputation.

**Program Details:**

- **First Year:** Students opting for this track receive scholarships from the ZEW (€1,500/month). Scholarship holders benefit from close exchange with ZEW researchers and a dedicated research unit.
- **Second Year Onwards:** Students become fully integrated into the ZEW as researchers, conditional on successfully completing core courses and passing an interview with their preferred research unit at the ZEW, while continuing their CDSE program.

For comprehensive details on this track, please visit [this ZEW page](https://www.zew.de/en/career/phd-track/cdse-phd-track-in-cooperation-with-the-zew).
