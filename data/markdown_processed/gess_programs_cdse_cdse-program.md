# CDSE Doctoral Program Structure

The doctoral program in economics is a comprehensive program spanning five years. It consists of a two-year course phase followed by a three-year research phase dedicated to the dissertation. The CDSE commits to funding students for this entire duration, contingent upon successful completion of the course phase and maintaining overall academic standing.

## Program Structure by Year

### First Year

Students must complete compulsory coursework in:

- Mathematics
- Microeconomics (I-III)
- Macroeconomics (I-III)
- Econometrics (I-III)

### Second Year

The focus shifts to elective coursework and proposal development:

- **Electives:** Students attend elective field courses, earning 40–46 ECTS credit points.
- **Proposal:** Students must submit their dissertation proposal by the end of the second year.
- *For an overview of past field courses, please consult the [course catalogues of recent semesters](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/course-catalog/course-catalog/).*

### Third Year Onwards (Years 3, 4, and 5)

These years are primarily devoted to the writing and completion of the doctoral thesis.

**Core Course Requirements (Minimum):**
Students must complete the following minimum coursework:

1. **Bridge Course:** One bridge course (5 ECTS) from either the [Center for Doctoral Studies in Business (CDSB)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/course-catalog/) or the [Center for Doctoral Studies in Behavioral and Social Sciences (CDSS)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/course-catalog/).
1. **English Academic Writing:** The program’s dedicated English Academic Writing course (3 ECTS).
1. **Electives:** Two further electives supporting thesis writing (10 ECTS).

**Academic Engagement:**
From the second year onward, participation in the following seminars is required:

- The weekly [CDSE Seminar](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/course-catalog/cdse-seminar/), where students present and act as discussants (starting in the third year).
- The [faculty seminar](http://fakultaetsseminar.uni-mannheim.de/), which is highly regarded and encourages learning from distinguished visitors, regardless of specialization.

**Thesis Focus:**

- Students determine their thesis supervisor and topic during the 4th semester.
- The remaining members of the doctoral committee may be selected later.

## Program Completion Requirements

The successful completion of the CDSE program requires meeting specific academic milestones:

### Year One Requirements

- **Exams:** Achieve a pass grade in all 10 core courses.
- **GPA:** Maintain a GPA of 2.5 in Semester 1, and an adjusted GPA of 2.5 in Semester 2 (based on 5 out of 6 core courses).

### Year Two Requirements

- **Electives:** Accumulate 40–46 ECTS credit points from elective courses listed in the GESS course catalogue. At least 4 of these electives must originate from the CDSE course catalogue.
- **Proposal:** Submit a research paper accompanied by a formal dissertation proposal.

### Degree Awarding Requirements

To earn the doctoral degree, the following must be achieved:

1. Completion of the entire CDSE course program.
1. Completion, submission, and acceptance of the thesis.
1. Successful defense of the thesis.

**Degree Awarded:** Dr. rer. pol.

**Thesis Format:**
The doctoral thesis can take two forms:

- **Standard:** Three original essays focused on one area, intended for publication in reputable scholarly journals.
- **Classical:** A longer, in-depth analysis of a single topic. The student and supervisor decide the format, with the understanding that the content will be used for journal articles.

*For detailed regulations, please refer to the [CDSE Regulations Section](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/student-information/regulations/).*

## Grading System and German Degrees

**CDSE Grading Scheme:**
The grading scale used at the CDSE ranges from 1.0 (excellent) to 4.0 (pass), with 5.0 indicating failure. Differentiation is possible in 0.3 steps (e.g., 1.3, 1.7).

**Doctoral Titles in Germany:**
In Germany, doctoral degrees often use specific Latin designations based on the field of study.

- **Faculty of Business and Economics:** Dr. rer. pol (doctor rerum politicarum)
- **Faculty of Social Sciences:** Dr. rer. soc. (doctor rerum socialium)
