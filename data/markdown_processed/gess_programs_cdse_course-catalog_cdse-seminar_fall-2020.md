# CDSE Seminar Fall 2020 Schedule

This catalog details the schedule for the CDSE Seminar held in Fall 2020. All sessions were conducted online.

## Seminar Sessions Overview

The seminar featured presentations on various topics in economics and finance, presented by both internal and external guests.

- **September 8, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Capital taxation and stock market volatility
  - **Speaker:** Pau Belda (ENTER Guest from UAB)
- **September 15, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Falling natural rates, rising housing volatility and the optimal inflation target
  - **Speaker:** Oliver Pfäuti
- **September 22, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Frictional disclosure and market power
  - **Speaker:** Jacopo Gambato
- **September 29, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Learning about the benefits of moving: The effects of neighborhood on mobility decision
  - **Speaker:** Suzanne Bellue
- **October 6, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** What's the missing link? The role of information in changing Covid-19 related behaviour
  - **Speaker:** Laura Montenbruck
- **October 13, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Sabotage in contests with group size uncertainty: An experimental analysis
  - **Speaker:** Jonathan Stäbler
- **October 20, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Capital requirements, bank competition and stability
  - **Speaker:** Tommaso Gasparini
- **October 27, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Gender diversity in corporate boards: Evidence from quota-implied discontinuities
  - **Speaker:** Valentina Melentyeva
- **November 10, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Inequality in health and wealth over the lifecycle in Germany
  - **Speaker:** Lukas Mahler
- **November 17, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Ridge regularized estimation of var models for inference and forecasting
  - **Speaker:** Giovanni Ballarin
- **November 24, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Legal or illegal cannabis? Hard drugs access and use
  - **Speaker:** Andres Daniel Pluas Lopez
- **December 1, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** An efficient dynamic mechanism with information acquisition
  - **Speaker:** Jasmina Simon
- **December 8, 2020** (3:30 pm – 5 pm)
  - **Topic:** Cannibalization and competition: Market entry following Sweden's pharmacy deregulation
  - **Speaker:** Isak Kupersmidt (ENTER Guest from SSE)
