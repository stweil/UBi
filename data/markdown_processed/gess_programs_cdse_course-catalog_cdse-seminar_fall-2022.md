# CDSE Seminar Fall 2022 Schedule

This document outlines the schedule for the CDSE Seminar held in Fall 2022. All sessions took place at L7, 3–5, 001.

## Seminar Sessions

- **September 6, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Levelling the Playing Field? Knowledge Production in the Digital Age (Speaker: Maximilian Mähr)
- **September 13, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Pay-as-bid Auctions with Private Information (Speaker: Daniel Linnenbrink)
- **September 20, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Can Work from Home Reduce the Gender Wage Gap? (Speaker: Ursula Berresheim)
- **September 27, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Do Stereotypes Cause the Gender Competition Gap? / More Now, Less Later? The Behavioral Effects of Parliamentary Pension Reform (Speakers: Michael Hilweg / Felix Köhler)
- **October 4, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Access to Renewable Energy: Micro-Finance for Small Businesses and Micro Enterprises in Pakistan. (Speaker: David Müller)
- **October 11, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): On the Relevance of Perceived Relative Incomes: Spousal Income Splitting and Biased Intra-Household Bargaining (Speaker: Jakob Schmidhäuser)
- **October 18, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Road to Green? The Effect of Highway Expansion on Exporting Firms' Emissions (Speaker: Siyu Zhang)
- **October 25, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Physical Climate Risk, Mortgage Lending and Bank Solvency (Speaker: Marina Hoch)
- **November 8, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Tiebout in the 21st Century – Local Fiscal Competition, Foot Voting and Legacy Debt (Speaker: Paul Steger)
- **November 15, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Prescription Drug Co-payments and Health Outcomes: Evidence from Insulin (Speaker: Matilde Cappelletti)
- **November 22, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): From Confiscation to Competition: the Effect of Goods Re-allocation in Italy (Speaker: Luca Currado)
- **November 29, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Why Don't You Deduct to Donate More? A Field Experiment with Carol Singers / The Consequences of Rural-to-urban Migration: Evidence from the Indiana Gas Boom (Speakers: Argun Aman / Assaf Abraham)
- **December 6, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Household Trading & Performance along Stock Price Cycles (Speaker: Ulrich Roschitsch)
