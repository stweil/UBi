# CDSE Seminar Schedule: Fall 2024

The CDSE Seminar for Fall 2024 will take place every Tuesday from **3:30 pm – 5:00 pm** in **S031**.

Below is the schedule of presentations for the semester:

### Seminar Dates and Topics

- **September 3, 2024:** OLS with Machine Learning Predictions (Speaker: Taro Fujita)
- **September 10, 2024:** Capital Intensity as a barrier to Technology Adoption: The Case of Computers (Speaker: Tanay Agarwal)
- **September 17, 2024:** Spatial Heterogeneity of House Price Responses to Monetary Polics Shocks (Speaker: Steffen Zetzmann)
- **September 24, 2024:** The General Equilibrium Effects of College Expansion in Developing Economies: Congestion Versus Composition (Speaker: Mohammed Khalfallah)
- **October 1, 2024:** The Novel Vehicle Tax on Fine Particulate Matter Emissions (Speaker: Nico Lukas Kasparetz)
- **October 8, 2024:** *No scheduled topic.*
- **October 15, 2024:** The Legacy of Rubber: Long-Term Effects of Colonial Experience in the Democratic Republic of Congo (Speaker: Adrià Mateu Romero)
- **October 22, 2024:** Tax incentives and labor market power: Evidence from China's Value-added Tax Reform (Speaker: Lishuang Huang)
- **October 29, 2024:**
  - Equal Inheritance Right: Implication for Fertility, Human Capital, and Growth in Sub-Saharan Africa (Speaker: Mingjie Xu)
  - Systematic Monetary Policy and Innovation (Speaker: Giacomo Ranzato)
- **November 5, 2024:**
  - Multi-market consumer data sharing (Speaker: Paul Wegener)
  - Strategically Waiving Returns in Refund Policies (Speaker: Julian Klix)
- **November 12, 2024:**
  - Employer switching and job quality (Speaker: Qinkun Yao)
  - Industrial Zones for Peace and Development in the Middle East? (Speaker: Ayman Tibi)
- **November 19, 2024:** Husband Wage Growth and Racial Gap in Household Division of Labor (Speakers: Jiaqi Li, University of Warwick)
- **November 26, 2024:**
  - The Impact of Advertisement Policy Design on Newspaper Behavior (Speaker: Pooja Singh)
  - Risk-based selection in a Public Pension Scheme (Speaker: Henning Schatz)
- **December 3, 2024:** Uncertainty about Uncertainty: Stochastic Insurance Markets and Quantity-Based Regulation (Speaker: Freya Cook)
