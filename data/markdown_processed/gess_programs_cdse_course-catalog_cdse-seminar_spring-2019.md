# CDSE Seminar Spring 2019 Schedule

This catalog details the schedule for the CDSE Seminar held in Spring 2019 at the University of Mannheim. All sessions took place in the L7,3–5, PO44 classroom.

## Seminar Overview

The seminar ran from February to May 2019, featuring presentations on various topics ranging from public health and labor economics to monetary policy.
