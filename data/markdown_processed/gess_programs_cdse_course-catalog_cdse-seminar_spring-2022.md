# CDSE Seminar Spring 2022 Schedule

This document outlines the schedule for the CDSE Seminar held in Spring 2022.

## Seminar Overview

- **Event:** CDSE Seminar Spring 2022
- **Location:** L7, 3–5, S031

## Seminar Schedule Details

The seminar ran from February 15, 2022, through May 31, 2022.

### February 2022

- **February 15, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Kick-Off and Q&A
- **February 22, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Monetary policy counterfactuals: Time series evidence on the fiscal multiplier
  - *Speakers:* Lukas Hack, Giovanni Ballarin

### March 2022

- **March 1, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Linked out? Measuring discrimination in job network formation / The housing channel of monetary policy in the euro area
  - *Speakers:* Felix Rusche, Hannes Twieling
- **March 8, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Can social comparisons and moral appeals change transportation behavior: A field experiment / Do filter bubbles impact social utility? An experimental approach
  - *Speakers:* Johannes Gessner, Giacomo Ferraro
- **March 15, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Taxing FDI in a developing economy: the case of informality
  - *Speakers:* Daniel Overbeck, Karl Schulz
- **March 22, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): (Dis)Inflation Targeting
  - *Speaker:* Mridula Duggal (ENTER Guest from UAB)
- **March 29, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): The Impact of Pension Income on Mortality: Evidence from Germany
  - *Speakers:* Chiara Malavasi, Matilde Cappelletti

### April 2022

- **April 5, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Bank managers' sentiment and loan growth
  - *Speakers:* Thibault Cézanne, Alexander Kann
- **April 26, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Optimal Advertisement for Information
  - *Speaker:* Yulia Evsyukova

### May 2022

- **May 3, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Granular Time Series Detection: A Geometric Approach
  - *Speaker:* So Jin Lee
- **May 10, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Letting (Beer-)Advocates Speak: Using Reviews to Define Nests in a Nested Logit Demand Estimation
  - *Speakers:* Alexander Kann, Andres Daniel Pluas Lopez
- **May 17, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): The impact of resource demand shocks on manufacturing sectors: Evidence from Indonesia / Efficient Allocation of Greenhouse Gas Emissions
  - *Speakers:* Sandra Kurniawati, Chang Liu
- **May 24, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Assessing the Effects of Size-dependent Financial Audits on Firm Behavior: Evidence from Ecuador/Offshoring Emissions: Did Trade Make German Firms Cleaner?
  - *Speakers:* Tania Guerra, Sabine Stillger
- **May 31, 2022** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Input Trade Liberalization with Strategic Sourcing
  - *Speaker:* Andrii Tarasenko
