# CDSE Seminar Spring 2023 Schedule

This document outlines the schedule for the CDSE Seminar held in Spring 2023. All sessions took place in Room L7, 3–5, 001.

## Seminar Sessions

**February 2023**

- **14.02.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Tenure(d) Gap: Affirmative Action in Academia.
  - *Speaker:* Maximilian Mähr
- **21.02.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Enough Bang for the Buck? The Pass-Through of Transfers on Real-World Public Investment.
  - *Speaker:* Felix Köhler
- **28.02.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Can Index Insurances foster Technology Adoption? The Case of Solar Panels in Pakistan.
  - *Speaker:* David Müller

**March 2023**

- **07.03.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Can Firms Reduce the Child Penalty? Consequences of a Mandated Increase in Firm Family-Friendliness.
  - *Speaker:* Pia Heckl (Vienna University)
- **14.03.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Optimal Monetary Policy in a Currency Union with Asymmetric House Price Booms.
  - *Speaker:* Ulrich Roschitsch
- **21.03.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Don't Hate the Player, Hate Luck's Shape. Optimal Contest Size: Theory and Experimental Design.
  - *Speaker:* Argun Aman
- **28.03.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Monetary Policy, Economic Uncertainty, and Firms R&D Expenditure.
  - *Speaker:* Morteza Ghomi (ENTER Guest)

**April 2023**

- **18.04.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Trickling Away? The Interaction of Local and Federal Taxes – Evidence from Swiss Cantonal Tax Reforms.
  - *Speaker:* Paul Steger
- **25.04.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): The Price Channel of Financial Contagion.
  - *Speaker:* Daniel Linnenbrink

**May 2023**

- **02.05.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Work from Home, Work for Less? Location Flexibility and Gender Wage Inequality / Natural Disasters and Household Default.
  - *Speakers:* Ursula Berresheim / Marina Hoch
- **09.05.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Uncovering the Role of Moral Hazard: Evidence from the Insulin Price Cap.
  - *Speaker:* Matilde Cappelletti
- **16.05.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): Road to Green? The Effect of Highway Expansion on Exporting Firms’ Pollution Emissions in China / Why Don't You Deduct to Donate More? A Field Experiment with Carol Singers.
  - *Speakers:* Siyu Zhang / Michael Hilweg
- **23.05.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): From Confiscation to Competition: The Economic Effect of Public Goods.
  - *Speaker:* Luca Currado
- **30.05.2023** (3:30 pm – 5 pm): The Local Economic Impact of US Troop Withdrawals in Germany / The Intergenerational Consequences of Occupational Decline: Evidence from the Industrialization of the American Workforce during the 19th Century.
  - *Speakers:* Jakob Schmidhäuser / Assaf Abraham
