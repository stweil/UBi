# CDSE Course Catalog Information

This guide outlines the procedures for course registration and evaluation for the CDSE program.

## Course Registration

### Deadlines (Portal²)

Please adhere to the following registration deadlines via Portal²:

- **Spring Semesters:** January 31
- **Fall Semesters:** August 31

**Important Notes:**

- For CDSB or CDSS courses, registration must also be completed through Portal².
- If you wish to take a course not listed in the GESS course catalog, please contact your Center Manager.

**Doctoral Students:**
Doctoral students from [ENGAGE.EU partner universities](https://www.engageuniversity.eu/partners/) are eligible to attend selected courses at the Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences.
To apply, please contact gess.registration@uni-mannheim.de. You must indicate the desired courses and provide a confirmation of enrollment or other documentation of admission/participation in the doctoral program at your home institution upon request.

## Course Evaluation

The program continuously strives for improvement, and all students are asked to evaluate every course attended. These evaluations provide invaluable feedback and are kept anonymous.

- **Process:** Username and password for evaluations will be sent to students via email after the courses have concluded.
- **Data Protection:** Information regarding data handling can be found [according to Article 13 GDPR](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/data-protection-declaration/).
