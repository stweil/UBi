## Fall 2024 Course Listings

### 1. Course: Basic Mathematical Concepts (Economics)

- **Prerequisites:** Basic mathematical knowledge
- **Course Content:** The course covers four chapters: 1) Basic mathematical concepts (sets, functions, relations) and strict reasoning; 2) Metric and normed spaces (convergence, continuity, compact sets); 3) Vector spaces (matrix algebra, linear transformation, eigenvalues); and 4) Multivariate differentiability and optimization (unconstrained and constrained).
- **Competences Acquired:** Students gain knowledge of basic mathematical concepts in analysis and linear algebra, understanding abstract concepts like metric and linear spaces, and applying knowledge to solve nonlinear optimization problems.
- **Assessment:** Exam (120 min)
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 02.09.24 – 23.09.24 (Monday, 10:15 – 11:45) | 03.09.24 – 24.09.24 (Tuesday, 10:15 – 11:45) | 04.09.24 – 25.09.24 (Wednesday, 10:15 – 11:45) | 05.09.24 – 26.09.24 (Thursday, 10:15 – 11:45)
  - **Exam:** 04.10.24 (Friday, 09:30 – 11:30)
  - **Retake Exam:** 04.12.24 (Wednesday, 10:00 – 12:00)
  - **Tutorials:** Scheduled across Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday for various groups.
- **Teaching Assistants:** [So Jin Lee](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/our-students/2019/so-jin-lee/), [Chang Liu](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/our-students/2019/chang-liu/)

### 2. Course: Microeconomic Foundations (Game Theory)

- **Prerequisites:** E700
- **Course Content:** Provides a foundation for Ph.D. level microeconomics. Part one covers decision theory (choice, preferences, expected utility, behavioral models). Part two covers game theory (static games of complete/incomplete information, dynamic games, equilibrium concepts).
- **Competences Acquired:** Students learn to model decision-making and strategic interactions, acquiring skills in decision theory and game theory, and writing rigorous formal proofs.
- **Assessment:** Written exam (120 min, 90% weighting); Exercises (10% weighting).
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 07.10.24 – 02.12.24 (Monday, 10:15 – 11:45) | 09.10.24 – 04.12.24 (Wednesday, 10:15 – 11:45)
  - **Exam:** 09.12.24 (Monday, 11:00 – 13:00)
  - **Retake Exam:** 22.01.25 (Wednesday, 10:00 – 12:00)
  - **Tutorials:** Scheduled on Wednesday (13:45 – 15:15 and 15:30 – 17:00).
- **Contact:** [Nicolas Schutz](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/schutz/); Phone: (0621) 181 1872; Email: schutz@uni-mannheim.de

### 3. Course: Macroeconomic Foundations

- **Prerequisites:** E700
- **Course Content:** Introduces structural dynamic models, focusing on how microeconomic foundations determine macroeconomic aggregates.
- **Competences Acquired:** Familiarity with recursive methods and dynamic programming, and basic macroeconomic models.
- **Assessment:** Problem sets (15%); Midterm (90 min, 35%); Final exam (120 min, 50%).
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 10.10.24 – 28.11.24 (Thursday, 15:30 – 17:00) | 11.10.24 – 29.11.24 (Friday, 08:30 – 10:00)
  - **Exam:** 13.12.24 (Friday, 11:00 – 13:00)
  - **Retake Exam:** 24.01.25 (Friday, 10:00 – 12:00)
  - **Tutorials:** Scheduled on Monday and Tuesday.
- **Teaching Assistant:** [Qinkun Yao](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/our-students/2022/qinkun-yao/)

### 4. Course: Advanced Econometric Theory

- **Prerequisites:** E700
- **Course Content:** Covers modern econometric theory foundations, including probability theory, asymptotic theory, point estimation, hypothesis testing, modern linear regression theory, instrumental variables, and GMM.
- **Competences Acquired:** Advanced theoretical knowledge in econometrics, ability to handle probabilistic backgrounds, and capacity for independent theoretical research.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 08.10.24 – 03.12.24 (Tuesday, 10:15 – 11:45) | 10.10.24 – 05.12.24 (Thursday, 10:15 – 11:45)
  - **Exam:** 17.12.24 (Tuesday, 11:00 – 13:00)
  - **Retake Exam:** 27.01.25 (Monday, 10:00 – 12:00)
  - **Tutorials:** Scheduled on Tuesday and Thursday.
- **Contact:** [Prof. Dr. Christoph Rothe](https://www.vwl.uni-mannheim.de/prof-dr-christoph-rothe/); Phone: (0621) 181 -1923; Email: rothe@vwl.uni-mannheim.de

- **Prerequisites:** E700-E703, E801-E806
- **Course Content:** A forum for discussing recent research in econometrics, suitable for students working on econometric or empirical projects.
- **Competences Acquired:** Ability to present and discuss current research in econometrics.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 05.09.24 – 05.12.24 (Thursday, 15:30 – 17:00)

### 8. Course: Empirical Macroeconomics

- **Prerequisites:** E700-E703, E801-E806
- **Course Content:** Covers methods and applications in empirical macroeconomics, focusing on Structural VAR (SVAR) models, identification strategies, factor models, and analyzing shocks (technology, monetary, fiscal).
- **Competences Acquired:** Introduction to econometric methods and macroeconomic applications, particularly concerning business cycles.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:** Multiple dates listed across September to November 2024.

### 9. Course: Contract Theory

- **Prerequisites:** Advanced Microeconomics I – III
- **Course Content:** Reviews classic topics in contract theory (e.g., debt contracts, hidden actions, incomplete contracts) within an abstract general framework, bridging finance and economics.
- **Competences Acquired:** Understanding the deep structure of hidden information and actions, and applying contract theory tools to research.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 02.09.24 – 02.12.24 (Monday, 10:15 – 11:45)

### 10. Course: Introduction to Machine Learning for Economists

- **Prerequisites:** E700-E703, E801-E806
- **Course Content:** Introduces supervised and unsupervised ML methods, focusing on challenges and opportunities for integration into empirical economics and causal inference.
- **Competences Acquired:** Understanding ML methods, their advantages/disadvantages in empirical research, and applying them to personal projects.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 03.09.24 – 03.12.24 (Tuesday, 08:30 – 10:00)

### 11. Course: Public Economics I

- **Prerequisites:** Successful completion of first two years of PhD programme
- **Course Content:** Core ideas in market failures and public intervention, covering optimal taxation, public goods, tax incidence, and tax evasion.
- **Competences Acquired:** Introduction to core topics in Public Economics, useful for policy analysis.
- **Assessment:** Written seminar paper and presentation.
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 02.09.24 – 02.12.24 (Monday, 08:30 – 10:00) | 04.09.24 – 27.11.24 (Wednesday, 08:30 – 10:00)

- **Course Content:** Covers multivariate time series models, including VAR models, cointegration, and factor models, addressing both asymptotic theory and implementation issues.
- **Competences Acquired:** Advanced econometric and statistical techniques for time series analysis, ability to synthesize specialized literature.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:** Multiple dates listed across September to December 2024.

- **Course Content:** A general forum for Ph.D. students to present and discuss their own research and receive feedback.
- **Competences Acquired:** Developing the ability to start, write up, and present original research.

- **Prerequisites:** Dissertation advised by Ager or Ciccone, or by invitation
- **Course Content:** Guides students through the dissertation research process in Industrial Organization.
- **Competences Acquired:** Ability to manage the entire research process, from proposal to presentation.

  - **Lecture:** 05.09.24 – 05.12.24 (Thursday, 11:00 – 13:45)

### 17. Course: Mechanism Design Theory

- **Prerequisites:** E700-E703, E801-E806
- **Course Content:** Covers mechanisms in information aggregation, including public goods provision, optimal taxation, and mechanisms for fiscal stability.
- **Competences Acquired:** Applying theories of information aggregation to practical policy problems.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 02.09.24 – 02.12.24 (Monday, 13:00 – 15:15)

### 18. Course: Behavioral Game Theory and Learning

- **Prerequisites:** First year courses
- **Course Content:** Introduces behavioral game theory and learning concepts (e.g., Information Cascades, Quantal-response equilibrium, Reinforcement Learning).
- **Competences Acquired:** Ability to read and understand literature on learning in games, providing a basis for independent Ph.D. work.
- **Assessment:** Written exam (90 min).
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 15.10.24 – 03.12.24 (Tuesday, 12:00 – 13:30)

### 19. Course: Experimental and Behavioral Economics Seminar

- **Prerequisites:** E700-E703, E801-E806
- **Course Content:** Focuses on current research in experimental and behavioral economics, involving student-led presentations and joint literature review.
- **Competences Acquired:** Improving presentation skills, critically assessing research ideas, and developing research proposals.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 05.09.24 – 05.12.24 (Thursday, 13:45 – 17:00)

### 20. Course: Labor Market Heterogeneity

- **Prerequisites:** Students interested in Economic History (3rd year and higher). By invitation.
- **Course Content:** Discusses how heterogeneity (workers, firms, locations) affects labor market outcomes, starting with search models.
- **Competences Acquired:** Ability to work with and extend heterogeneous agent models, connecting theory to empirical evidence.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**
  - **Lecture:** 03.09.24 – 03.12.24 (Tuesday, 10:15 – 11:45)

### 21. Course: Information Dynamics Seminar

- **Prerequisites:** First year PhD courses
- **Course Content:** Covers dynamic models of learning in economics, including games of experimentation and information design.
- **Competences Acquired:** Understanding how information is acquired over time and applying dynamic optimization techniques.
- **Assessment:** Presentations (based on literature review).
- **Schedule:** Multiple dates listed across September to December 2024.

### 22. Course: Market Design Seminar

- **Prerequisites:** E601-E603 (or equivalent)
- **Course Content:** Focuses on market design applications rooted in matching theory, allowing students to present and discuss state-of-the-art research.
- **Competences Acquired:** Applying expertise to analyze debates in academic and policy-oriented literature regarding market design.
- **Assessment:** N/A
- **Schedule:**

  - **Block seminar:** 05.12.24 – 06.12.24

### 23. Course: Data Science in Action (Ringvorlesung)

- **Prerequisites:** None (Open to GESS doctoral students)
- **Course Content:** A lecture series on data science applications, taught online.
- **Competences Acquired:** Practical skills in data science methods.
- **Assessment:** 15-page essay (Pass/Fail).
- **Schedule:** Online, starting TBC.

### 24. Course: Mental Health in Academia Seminar

- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Course Content:** Addresses typical risk factors and challenges related to mental health during doctoral dissertations through guided group discussions.
- **Competences Acquired:** Knowledge about mental health challenges in academia and developing self-reflection skills.
