# Department of Economics

The Department of Economics hosts twenty-two full-time senior and numerous junior faculty members. The department is highly active in research across various fields, including:

- Micro- and macroeconomics
- Econometrics and statistical methods
- Labor economics and industrial organization
- Financial economics and public economics
- Economic history and international trade

Research efforts are supported by major institutional grants from the German Research Foundation (DFG) and European Union research and training grants, supplemented by funding from other public and private institutions.

## Associated Research Centers and Institutions

The department maintains extensive international contacts and collaborates with numerous universities and research institutions worldwide. Key associated research centers located in Mannheim include:

### SFB 884: The Political Economy of Reforms

This is an interdisciplinary collaborative research center dedicated to investigating the success and failure of policy reforms. The research analyzes competing interests, contextual factors, and the political processes involved in reform-making.

### ZEW: Centre for European Economic Research

ZEW is recognized as a leading institute for applied economic research in Europe. Its focus lies on internationally comparative issues within the European context, and it maintains several important databases.
