# European Network for Training in Economic Research (ENTER)

The CDSE offers students the opportunity to conduct part of their doctoral studies abroad through the **European Network for Training in Economic Research (ENTER)** program. ENTER is a joint initiative involving top departments and schools of economics from several partner universities:

- [Katholieke Universiteit Brabant (Tilburg, Netherlands)](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/research/institutes-and-research-groups/center/)
- [Stockholm School of Economics (Sweden)](https://www.hhs.se/)
- [Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (Spain)](http://economics.uc3m.es/)
- [Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (Spain)](http://idea.uab.es/)
- Universität Mannheim (Germany)
- [Université des Sciences Sociales de Toulouse (France)](http://www.tse-fr.eu/)
- [Université Libre de Bruxelles (Belgium)](http://www.ecares.org/)
- [University College London (Great Britain)](http://www.ucl.ac.uk/economics)
- [University of Stockholm](http://www.ne.su.se/english/)

## ENTER Program Opportunities

The network offers three main avenues for doctoral students:

### 1. ENTER Long-Term Exchanges (Fellowships)

Visiting research fellowships are available at the ENTER nodes. These fellowships are designed for doctoral students seeking advanced graduate education or research training in economics and its applications.

- **Duration:** Typically one or two semesters, usually during the third or fourth year at the CDSE.
- **Selection:** Selection is based exclusively on academic excellence.
- **Funding:** Stays can be financed by the network via the CDSE, pending merit review.
- **Status:** Successful applicants are treated by the hosting institutions as regular PhD students.

### 2. ENTER Exchange Seminars

This series allows advanced dissertation students the chance to present their research work in seminars at any participating university during a short-term stay. The CDSE seminar in Mannheim regularly hosts two to three external speakers from the ENTER network each semester.

### 3. ENTER Jamboree

These annual events take place in March or April and serve multiple purposes:

- Familiarizing doctoral students with conference-style presentations and discussions in a focused setting.
- Fostering interaction between students and faculty from the different participating networks.
- Coordinating the overall programs.

*Note: Slots for CDSE students are allocated by the ENTER coordinator based on the student's academic standing and participation in CDSE seminars and other ENTER-related activities (e.g., acting as a discussant for incoming speakers).*
