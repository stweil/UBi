# Taro Fujita

**Academic Year:** 2022
**Center:** Economics

## Profile Summary

Taro Fujita was born in 1990 in Shizuoka, Japan. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Hitotsubashi University and a Master’s degree in International Economics and Public Policy from Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz. Prior to joining CDSE, he gained eight years of experience as a financial analyst at the Bank of Japan.

## Research Interests

His primary academic interests lie in microeconometrics, with a specific focus on the intersection of causal inference and machine learning.
