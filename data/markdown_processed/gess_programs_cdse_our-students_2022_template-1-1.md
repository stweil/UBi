# Siyu Zhang

Siyu Zhang is a student focusing on economic research at the University of Mannheim.

## 🎓 Academic Background & Profile

- **Origin:** Born in 2001 in Jiaxing, Zhejiang Province, China.
- **Undergraduate Studies:** Completed an undergraduate degree in Economics at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China (2019–2023), graduating with first-class honours.
- **Current Program:** Joined the CDSE through the Master of Economic Research (MAER) track at the University of Mannheim in 2024.

## 🔬 Research Interests

Siyu's academic focus lies in labor economics, behavioral economics, and experimental economics.

- **Bachelor's Thesis:** Examined the long-run educational impacts of the 1976 Tangshan earthquake on rural middle school-aged children, utilizing 1% microdata from China's national census in 2000.
