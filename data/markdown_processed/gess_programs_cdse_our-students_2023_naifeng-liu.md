# Naifeng Liu

**Academic Year:** 2023
**Center:** Economics

## Academic Background and Research Focus

Naifeng Liu was born in Shanghai, China. His academic journey began with studies in computer science, earning degrees from the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, The State University of New York at Albany, and the CUNY Graduate Center.

While his initial studies focused on computer science, his academic interests gradually shifted toward economics. He subsequently pursued further studies at the CDSE.

**Current Research Interests:**

- Game Theory
- Political Economy
- Industrial Organization

He is particularly interested in applying advanced techniques from theoretical computer science, such as approximation algorithms and complexity theory, to solve problems within the field of economics.
