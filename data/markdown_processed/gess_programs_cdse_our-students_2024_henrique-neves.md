# Henrique Neves

Henrique Neves is a student whose research interests lie at the intersection of labour, health, and family economics.

## Academic Background & Research Focus

- **Origin:** Born in 1998 in Lisbon, Portugal.
- **Education:**
  - He holds a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the Nova School of Business and Economics. During his studies, he completed an exchange semester in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
  - He obtained his Master’s degree in economics also from the Nova School of Business and Economics.
- **Thesis Work:** His Master's thesis investigated the main drivers of wage inequality in Portugal, with a specific focus on the impact of increasing educational levels on inequality measures.
- **Professional Experience:** Before joining the doctoral program at GESS, Henrique worked as a Pre-doctoral Researcher at the Stone Centre for the Study of Wealth Inequality at INSEAD and the Institute for Fiscal Studies. His work during this period focused on projects concerning health and labour market inequalities, as well as early child development.
- **Primary Research Interests:** Labour, health, and family economics.
