# Hao Wu

Hao Wu is a student whose academic profile focuses on economics, with specific research interests in Macroeconomics and Industrial Organization.

## 🎓 Akademischer Hintergrund

- **Bachelorstudium:** Economics from Minzu University of China.
- **Masterstudium:** Master of Science in Economics and Social Sciences from Bocconi University.
- **Forschungserfahrung:** During his time at Bocconi, he was a visiting student at the IGIER research center, where he participated in several research projects.

## 🔬 Forschungsinteressen

Hao's primary research interests lie in:

- Macroeconomics
- Industrial Organization

His master’s thesis investigates how intergenerational differences in life experiences can distort household financial decisions and broader economic outcomes.
