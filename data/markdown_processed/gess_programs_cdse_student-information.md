# Information for Prospective Students: CDSE Doctoral Program

This page provides prospective students with an overview of various topics related to the Doctoral Program at the CDSE.

- **For Active Students:** Please refer to the dedicated page for active students: [Active Students Information](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/for-active-students/).
- **CDSE Info Booklet:** Additional, detailed information is available in the CDSE Info Booklet (PDF, 600 kB). This booklet covers the GESS in general, the CDSE program structure, information for international students, and living in Mannheim.
- **General Queries:** For any further questions, please contact the CDSE Center Management via email.
