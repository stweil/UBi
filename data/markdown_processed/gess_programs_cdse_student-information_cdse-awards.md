# CDSE Teaching Award

The Teaching Award recognizes and honors the substantial contributions of CDSE doctoral students to teaching and learning within the Graduate School.

## 🏆 2024 Award Winner

The winner for the 2024 Teaching Award is **[Pablo Zarate Cisternas](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdse/our-students/2021/pablo-zarate-cisternas/)**.

He was recognized for his teaching of **“Microeconomics A”** during the Academic Year 2023/2024. Congratulations!
