# CDSS Associate Member Program for Master's Students

The CDSS Associate Member Program is designed for Master's students at the University of Mannheim who are planning to pursue doctoral research at the GESS.

## Program Overview

This membership status is available to first-year Master's students in the following fields:

- Political Science
- Psychology
- Sociology
- Social Data Science (M.A./M.Sc.)

**Benefits of Membership:**

- Opportunity to join GESS graduate-level courses during the membership year.
- Access to a research and conference budget of €100 for attending events like summer schools or conferences.

## Eligibility Requirements

To apply for CDSS Associate Member status, you must meet the following criteria:

1. Be a first-year University of Mannheim M.A./M.Sc. student in Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, or Social Data Science.
1. Maintain a Grade Point Average (GPA) of at least 2.0.
1. Demonstrate a clear and demonstrable wish to pursue a doctorate in one of these subjects.

## Application Procedure

Applications must be submitted as a **single PDF file** via email to [cdss.gess@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:cdss.gess@uni-mannheim.de).

**Required Documents:**

- **CV:** Must include the names and contact details of up to two referees familiar with your academic potential.
- **Letter of Motivation:** Maximum 700 words.
- **Writing Sample:** Maximum 3,500 words.
- **Transcripts:** Your final bachelor’s transcript and your current (1st-year) master’s transcript.

**Application Deadline:** June 15 of any given year.

______________________________________________________________________

**Important Note:**
Associate Membership does **not** automatically guarantee a place in the CDSS doctoral program. You must separately [apply](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/apply/) to join the CDSS program after the completion of your master's degree. Any courses attended during the membership year will be credited against the requirements for the CDSS course curriculum.
