# CDSS Doctoral Program Overview

The CDSS doctoral programs span three years and consist of coursework across two semesters. Doctoral students must engage in advanced, graduate-level studies through mandatory and elective courses. The first year requires students to complete a literature review, which guides the development of their dissertation proposal. Students determine their specific thesis topic and supervisor during this initial year.

## Available Programs

The CDSS program offers specialization in the following fields:

- [Political Science](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/cdss-programs/political-science/)
- [Psychology](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/cdss-programs/psychology/)
- [Sociology](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/cdss-programs/sociology/)
- [Social Data Science](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/cdss-programs/social-data-science/)

## Program Curriculum Requirements

The coursework is structured across the initial semesters:

### First Semester

- Mathematics for Social Scientists (2 ECTS)
- Current Research Perspectives (2 ECTS)
- Crafting Social Science Research (6 ECTS)
- Literature review (6 ECTS)
- CDSS Workshop in respective discipline (2 ECTS)

### Second Semester

- Theory Building and Causal Inference (6 ECTS)
- English Academic Writing (3 ECTS)
- Dissertation Proposal Workshop (2 ECTS)
- Dissertation proposal (8 ECTS)
- CDSS Workshop in respective discipline (2 ECTS)

### Third Semester Onwards

- Research colloquia (2 ECTS per semester)
- CDSS Workshops in respective discipline (2 ECTS per semester)
- Dissertation work leading to thesis submission and defense (90 ECTS)

**Note:** Further mandatory and elective course requirements are detailed in the subject-specific attachments to the [CDSS program regulations](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/student-information/program-regulations/).

## Degree and Thesis Requirements

To successfully complete the program, students must fulfill the following criteria:

1. Completion of the entire CDSS course program.
1. Completion, submission, and acceptance of the thesis.
1. Successful thesis defense.

**Awarded Degree:** Dr. rer. soc. (Doctor rerum socialium / Doctor of Social Sciences)

### CDSS Doctoral Thesis Format

A CDSS doctoral thesis can take one of two forms:

- **Collection of Papers:** The thesis may consist of several original papers within one area, intended for publication in reputable scholarly journals.
- **Traditional Monograph:** A traditional dissertation format is also possible, provided the content is suitable for subsequent publication as journal articles.

The final format will be mutually decided by the student and their supervisor.

## Doctoral Degrees in Germany

In Germany, doctoral degrees often use specific Latin designations to indicate the field of study, which differs from the general *Ph.D.* (doctor philosophiae). Both titles are awarded after intensive study and research culminating in a successful thesis defense.

At the GESS, the following doctoral titles are conferred:

- **Faculty of Business and Economics:** Dr. rer. pol (doctor rerum politicarum)
- **Faculty of Social Sciences:** Dr. rer. soc. (doctor rerum socialium)
