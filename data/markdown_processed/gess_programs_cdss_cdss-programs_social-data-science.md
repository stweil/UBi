# CDSS Doctoral Program in Social Data Science

The CDSS Doctoral Program in Social Data Science offers advanced coursework in data analysis methods tailored for the social sciences, integrating expertise from statistics and computer science.

The program is designed to enable students to address substantive research questions more effectively by utilizing new data types and advanced methodologies. Students have the opportunity to specialize deeply in machine learning and its applications across various data modalities, including text, images, videos, and networks.

## Research at the Mannheim Center for Data Science

The [Mannheim Center for Data Science](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/datascience/) serves as a key platform for interdisciplinary research in data science, bringing together experts from diverse academic disciplines.

The Center's research efforts are focused on several core areas:

- **Data Understanding and Valuation:** Advancing the understanding and valuation of data, particularly in conjunction with artificial intelligence methodologies.
- **Digital Transformation:** Investigating the digital transformation of individuals, the economy, and society by analyzing both structured and unstructured data.
- **Methodological Development:** Developing and refining appropriate methodological approaches to support these research objectives.
- **Responsible Data Use:** Identifying frameworks for the responsible use of data and analytical methods that serve the public interest.

### Doctoral Supervision

Researchers associated with the Center act as doctoral supervisors for the Social Data Science track. These supervisors are characterized by a strong methodological background, a keen interest in answering substantive research questions, and a commitment to an interdisciplinary approach to science.
