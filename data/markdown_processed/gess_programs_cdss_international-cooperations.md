# Doctoral Studies Abroad for CDSS Students

CDSS students frequently utilize the opportunity to conduct part of their doctoral studies internationally.

**Program Structure:**
The CDSS does not offer an organized exchange program with one specific institution. Instead, doctoral students, in collaboration with their supervisors, arrange visits to internationally recognized universities and research centers.

**Examples of Visited Institutions:**
CDSS students have recently completed visits to the following universities:

- University College London
- University of Oxford
- Johns-Hopkins-University
- University of Montreal
- University of Barcelona
- Berkeley University of California, Simons Institute
- Oxford Brookes University
- Rice University
- University of Pavia
