# Pirmin Stöckle

Pirmin Stöckle is a doctoral student at the Center for Doctoral Studies in Social and Behavioral Sciences (CDSS) at the University of Mannheim.

## Academic Background

- **Bachelor's Degree:** Politics and Public Administration from the University of Konstanz.
- **Master's Double Degree:** Political Science from the University of Konstanz and Pompeu Fabra University Barcelona.

## Professional Experience

Before joining the CDSS, Pirmin gained professional experience as a consultant in the market research industry. During this time, he focused on conducting and analyzing empirical survey studies concerning:

- Pricing strategies
- Customers' decision-making processes
- Brand image
- Applying insights from Behavioral Economics

## Research Focus

His current research interests center around:

- The dynamics of attitude formation.
- Social behavior and its measurement.
- Utilizing advanced methods and new modes of data collection.

**Supervisor:** [Thomas Bräuninger](https://www.sowi.uni-mannheim.de/braeuninger/)
