# Anna Sokolova

Anna Sokolova is a Doctoral Candidate in the School of Social Sciences at the University of Mannheim.

## Academic Background & Experience

Anna Sokolova obtained her Bachelor’s degree in Sociology, specializing in Economic Sociology, from the National Research University Higher School of Economics in Moscow, Russia. She subsequently completed a Research Master in Sociology and Social Research at Utrecht University in the Netherlands.

In Moscow, she gained experience as a research assistant at the Laboratory for Studies in Economic Sociology, focusing on projects related to the sociology of medicine and healthcare. She also served as a teaching assistant at the Department of Sociology.

During her studies, she developed game-theoretic models of investments in dyadic and institutional embeddedness for her master thesis, testing predictions using data from a laboratory experiment. Furthermore, during a research internship at Utrecht University, she investigated how the network structure of online marketplace communities affects the cooperative behavior of sellers, collecting textual and network data from a social networking site.

## Research Interests

Anna’s primary research interests lie in:

- Economic and analytical sociology
- Social networks
- Trust and cooperation
- Experimental methods in sociology
- Formal theoretical modelling
- Network analysis
