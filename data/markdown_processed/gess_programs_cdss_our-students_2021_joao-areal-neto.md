# João Areal Neto

João Areal Neto is a PhD candidate in Political Science within the CDSS.

**Academic Background & Research Focus**
João holds a Bachelor's degree in Politics and Philosophy from the University of Sheffield (UK) and a Master's degree in Social Sciences (Research) from the University of Amsterdam (NL). His master's thesis focused on affective polarization in Brazil, specifically examining the concept of negative political identities.

During his time in Amsterdam, he gained experience as a research assistant and completed an internship at the [Hot Politics Lab](https://www.hotpolitics.eu/). There, he collaborated on developing a measure of elite hostility using parliamentary speech data.

Methodologically, his primary interests lie in Computational Social Sciences, with a strong focus on automated text analysis. His broader research interests include:

- Affective polarization
- Positive and negative partisanship
- Comparative political behaviour
- Brazilian politics
