# CDSS Doctoral Student Awards

The Center for Doctoral Studies in Social and Behavioral Sciences (CDSS) annually honors its doctoral students through the CDSS Doctoral Student Awards. These awards recognize outstanding achievements in areas such as research, academic performance, publications, and teaching contributions.

## GESS Young Scholar Award in the Social Sciences

This award recognizes exceptional ideas and achievements in doctoral research and supports professional activities aimed at advancing the professionalization and networking of young scientists.

**Notable Winners:**

- **Lisa-Marie Müller** ([Link](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/our-students/2023/lisa-marie-mueller/))
- **Hannah Rajski** ([Link](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/our-students/2022/hannah-rajski/))
- **Malena Ullrich** ([Link](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/our-students/2024/malena-ullrich/))
  - *Project:* 'Women's Square – Mannheim Network of Female Social Scientists'
- **Leonard Wendering** ([Link](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdss/our-students/2023/leonard-wendering/))
  - *Project:* 'Triadic Competition: An Experiment on Triadic Contact among East, West, and Turkish-Germans'

## Publication of the Year Award

This award recognizes an outstanding publication that has been published or accepted for publication in a peer-reviewed journal during the academic year.

**Notable Winner:**

- **Johanna Hölzl** et al. for '[The (mis)use of Google Trends data in the social sciences – ](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0049089X24001212)' in Social Science Research
  - *Details:* [A systematic review, critique, and recommendations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0049089X24001212)
