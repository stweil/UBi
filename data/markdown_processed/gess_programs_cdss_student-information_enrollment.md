# Enrollment for Doctoral Candidates

All doctoral candidates must enroll at the University of Mannheim due to the Act on Higher Education of the Land of Baden-Württemberg (LHG).

## Required Documents for Enrollment

Please submit the following documents:

- Completed enrollment application form
- Confirmation of admission at the GESS
- Signed Milestones (doctoral advising agreement)
- If applicable, copy of your current employment contract
- Copy of your university entrance qualification (e.g., 'Abitur', 'baccalaureate') (Only required if you are not already enrolled at the University of Mannheim)
- Copy of your degree certificates (Bachelor and Master) (Only required if you did not obtain the degree at the University of Mannheim)
- ID card/passport (including visa, if applicable)

## Fees

**Semester Fees:**
The current semester fee amount is **169 Euro** and must be paid after you have obtained a student ID number.

**Doctoral Candidates:**
Doctoral candidates pay **no tuition fees**.

## Important Notes for Employed Candidates

**Doctoral candidates employed by the University of Mannheim** on work contracts of 50% or more (based on a full-time position and according to the Collective Agreement for Public Service Employees of the German Länder (TV-L)) have the **option** to choose whether to enroll or be exempted from enrollment.

- **To be Exempted:** If you wish to be exempted from enrollment, please contact Johanna Fatokun to obtain the form 'Exemption from obligation to enroll'. You must inform her immediately about any changes to your work contract.
- **To Enroll:** If you wish to enroll, please submit the required documents and follow the procedure outlined above.
