# AI-Powered Webcrawler for Business Information Extraction

[https://www.uni-mannheim.de/ines/lehre/european-master-team-project/projekt-ai-powered-webcrawler-for-business-information-extraction/](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/ines/lehre/european-master-team-project/projekt-ai-powered-webcrawler-for-business-information-extraction/)

## Project Context: Supply Chain Due Diligence Act

The German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act mandates improved protection of human rights and the environment throughout global supply chains. As part of this act, detailed information about all suppliers must be gathered to assess their risk of non-compliance.

This required information is extensive and includes various data points such as:

- Locations and owners
- Responsible key personnel
- Industries of operation
- Revenue and number of employees
- Availability of certificates
- And more.

Crucially, this necessary data is often presented in an unstructured format across company websites, necessitating advanced processing techniques.

## Project Objective

The goal of this project is to design, implement, and deploy an AI-powered webcrawler. This tool must efficiently navigate business websites in a hierarchical manner, identify relevant data points, and utilize Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to extract crucial information regarding a company's operations and structure. The resulting webcrawler must be robust enough to handle diverse website types while delivering accurate and comprehensive results.

## Key Deliverables

The project scope encompasses several technical components:

- **AI-powered Webcrawler Prototype:** Developing a functional webcrawler capable of scanning websites and using NLP to extract specific data points, including:
  - Industry classification
  - ISO certificates
  - Top management details
  - Sublocations, products, services, customers, and subfirms.
- **Data Processing and Visualization:** Implementing a complete data pipeline to clean, organize, and present the extracted data in a user-friendly format suitable for subsequent analysis and visualization.
- **NLP Processing of Crawled Data:** Utilizing advanced NLP techniques to process the raw crawled data and structure it into a pre-processed format ready for further steps.
- **Neural Network-based Categorization:** Employing neural networks to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of information classification within the extracted dataset.

## Required Skills

Successful completion of this project requires a diverse skill set, including:

- **Programming Proficiency:** Expertise in languages such as Python or JavaScript for web development and data processing.
- **NLP Understanding:** Solid knowledge of NLP tools and their processing capabilities.
- **Machine Learning:** Base knowledge of Neural Networks and their application in information classification.
- **Web Technologies:** Familiarity with web crawling techniques, data scraping, and handling various web formats (HTML, XML, etc.).
- **Data Management:** Experience with databases and data management practices for storing and retrieving extracted information.
- **Soft Skills:** Ability to work effectively in a team, manage project timelines, and collaborate efficiently.
