# Intelligent Hospital Logistics Project (European Master Team Project)

This section details the scope, objectives, and structure of the European Master Team Project focused on optimizing logistics within hospital environments.

## 🎯 Project Overview and Goals

The primary goal of this project is to develop and implement advanced, intelligent logistics solutions for modern hospitals. The project integrates cutting-edge technologies to improve efficiency, reduce operational costs, and enhance patient care pathways.

- **Scope:** Analyzing and optimizing complex material and personnel flows within a hospital setting.
- **Methodology:** Utilizing a European Master Team approach, combining academic theory with real-world industrial application.
- **Key Objectives:**
  - Streamlining supply chain management (e.g., medical supplies, linens).
  - Optimizing internal transport routes for personnel and equipment.
  - Implementing smart tracking and inventory systems.
