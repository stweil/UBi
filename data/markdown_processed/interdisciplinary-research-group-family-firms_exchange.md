# Scholarly Exchange and Research Focus

Science thrives on the exchange of ideas. Our group facilitates inquiry in organizational social science through transnational networks of scholars, emphasizing openness and transdisciplinarity.

## Research Foundations and Tradition

Our research roots are deep, built upon the tradition of organization research at Mannheim University. The foundation of our work is rooted in the principles of Organization & the Carnegie School (including scholars like H.A. Simon, J. March, and A. Kieser).

Scholarly advancement in organizational studies requires nurturing the development of social scientists and their scholarship over entire careers while actively encouraging worldwide collaborations.

## Key Collaborations and Networks

We actively cultivate our research roots through partnerships with leading international institutions:

- **SCANCOR – Scandinavian Consortium for Organizational Research:** We maintain active exchange with [SCANCOR](https://scancor.org/) at Stanford University and Harvard University.
- **European Family Business Center (EUFBC):** We are an integral part of the [European Family Business Research Centers Network](https://eufbc.org/). This network embodies mutual exchange among Europe's leading research centers focusing on family firm research. The EUFBC supports discussion, collaboration, and exchange through bi-weekly virtual research seminars, with a particular focus on supporting early-career scholars.
