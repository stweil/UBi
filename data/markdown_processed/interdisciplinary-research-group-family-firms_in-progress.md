# Interdisciplinary Research Group: Family Firms, Technology, and Society

The research group operates at the intersection of multiple disciplines—including Business Studies, Sociology, Psychology, Information Systems, and Equality & Intercultural Studies—to provide a holistic and deep understanding of complex organizational systems, particularly family firms.

## Research Philosophy and Methodology

The study of family firms requires acknowledging multiple perspectives and truths, as the social system within a family often contains as many views as family members.

- **Multi-Paradigmatic Approach:** Research is inherently multi-paradigmatic and inter-disciplinary. While recognizing that meanings (e.g., "legacy") can vary across generations, time, and business models, the research aims to explain the influence of these concepts on family, strategy, and performance.
- **Methodological Rigor:** The approach is both **wholistic and inductive**, yet ultimately seeks to produce practical, actionable recommendations, grounding findings in empirical testing, making the research fundamentally **positivistic**.
- **Tools:** The group utilizes advanced tools, including supercomputing capabilities, to conduct in-depth case studies, integrating quantitative, qualitative, positivist, and inductive research methods.

## Ongoing Research Projects

The group showcases selected ongoing projects categorized by core themes:

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Firms & Entrepreneurship

- **Ambidexterity:** Examining stability and agility within family firms (Fritsch).
- **Strategic Alliances:** Research into strategic alliances among family firms (Kulandaisamy).
- **Leadership & Crisis:** Investigating leader identity and crisis performance in family firms (Ahrens et al.).
- **Crowdfunding:** Theorizing and understanding the role of crowdfunding (Ahrens).
- **Succession:** Analyzing succession planning and leader attributes (Riefolo & Ahrens et al.).

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Human Organizations

- **Intelligent Organization:** Viewing the organization as an intelligent entity (Riefolo, Grosskopf, Ahrens).
- **Global Influence Mapping:** Utilizing supercomputing for the global identification of family influence (Ahrens, Istipliler, Kowalzick).
- **Management Science:** Applications of supercomputer systems in management science (Baumgardt).

### ♻️ AI & Supercomputing for Sustainability

- **CEO Insiderness:** Linking CEO insider status to sustainability practices (Sami, Fritsch, Ahrens).
- **Midlife Crisis:** Examining the impact of the midlife crisis on sustainability (Riefolo, Manganaro, Fritsch, Ahrens).
- **Twin Transformation:** Research into the twin transformation process (Fröhlich, de Groote, Felden, Ahrens).

### ⚖️ Diversity, Equality, & Intercultural Management

- **Gender Equality:** Comprehensive studies on gender equality in both general organizations and family firms (Ahrens, Welch et al.).
- **Cultural Diversity:** Examining cultural diversity and its link to sustainability (Grosskopf, Riefolo, Ahrens).
- **Culture & Segregation:** Analyzing the interplay between culture, segregation, and sustainability (Glowka, Fritsch, Stangej, Geissinger, Bromley, & Ahrens).

### 🧠 Personality, Well-being, Values, Brands, & Organizations

- **Crowdfunding & Personality:** Analyzing the role of personality in crowdfunding behavior (Schenkenhofer, Uebbing, Manganaro, Ahrens).
- **Self-Transformation:** Viewing entrepreneurship as a transformation of the Self (Radtke).
- **Branding & Image:** Exploring personal brands versus organizational image, focusing on familiness and sustainability in hospitality and luxury (Manganaro & Ahrens).
- **Family Branding:** Strategies for branding and marketing within family firms (Finkbeiner).
- **Relationships:** Investigating marriage and modern relationships within entrepreneurial families (Stamm, Bernhard, Ahrens, Istipliler).
