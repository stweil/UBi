# The Journal of Family Business Strategy (JFBS)

The Interdisciplinary Research Group Family Firms actively serves the field's key academic journals, notably the [Journal of Family Business Strategy (JFBS)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/journal/). The JFBS is dedicated to publishing research that advances knowledge and understanding within the field of family business.

## Journal Scope and Focus

The JFBS maintains an international scope, welcoming submissions that explore all facets of the relationship between family influence on business and business influence on family.

**Key Areas of Publication:**

- **Theoretical and Empirical Work:** The journal aims to be the primary outlet for high-quality theoretical and empirical papers across a wide spectrum of strategy topics. It welcomes submissions from both emerging scholars exploring new areas and experienced scholars extending existing paradigms.
- **Interdisciplinary Approach:** JFBS publishes top-tier theory papers on family business strategy drawn from multiple disciplines, including:
  - Organizational Behavior and Theory
  - Sociology and Anthropology
  - Psychology and Social Psychology
  - Strategic Management
  - Economics and Finance
  - Industrial Relations
    The journal intentionally transcends disciplinary and geographical boundaries to engage a broad academic audience.
- **Methodological Variety:** The journal accepts and publishes a range of formats, including quantitative research, qualitative work, and purely theoretical/conceptual papers. Research published in JFBS must meet the highest standards of rigor, regardless of the methodological approach. Topics range from micro-level studies (e.g., behavioral economics and finance experiments) to macro-level issues impacting public policy.

## Editorial Leadership

The journal is currently led by the Editor-in-Chief, Torsten Pieper, supported by Senior Associate Editors Mathew Hughes and Jan-Philipp Ahrens. Multiple members of the interdisciplinary research group contribute to the editorial review board of the JFBS.
