# Research Excellence: Pushing the Frontiers of Knowledge

Our research is guided by the search for truth, reflecting a deep passion for pushing the frontiers of knowledge. With over 30 international peer-reviewed awards and nominations, we are dedicated to sharing our insights through leading journals and fostering a vibrant academic community by organizing and participating in international academic conferences.

Discover our diverse research topics and get inspired by positive change and new discoveries.

## Core Research Areas

We maintain an interdisciplinary approach where family dynamics meet modern business challenges. Our expertise spans several critical areas:

- [AI & Supercomputing for Sustainability](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/research/ai-supercomputing-for-sustainability/)
- [Artificial Intelligence & Human Organizations](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/research/artificial-intelligence-human-organizations/)
- [Diversity, Equality, & Intercultural Management](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/research/diversity-equality-intercultural-management/)
- [Personality, Well-being, Values, Brands, & Organizations](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/research/personality-well-being-values-brands-organizations/)
- [Family Firms & Entrepreneurship](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/interdisciplinary-research-group-family-firms/research/entrepreneurship-family-firms/)

## Academic Scope and Methodology

Our work bridges tradition and modernity. Historically, our research has been published in prestigious journals such as *Organization Science*, *Administrative Science Quarterly*, *Entrepreneurship Theory & Practice*, *Journal of Management*, and *Journal of Management Studies*.

Today, we leverage a supercomputer to capture and analyze family influence worldwide, ensuring a truly interdisciplinary approach when family meets business.
