# Entrepreneurship & Family Firms Research

Family firms are recognized as a backbone of the economy, driven by a unique blend of long-term management perspectives, emotional ties, and the human element. Understanding how family dynamics and personal relationships influence decision-making and resilience is key to unlocking their long-term success and adaptability across generations.

As international experts in this field, our research group focuses on the strategic dynamics that shape business leadership, innovation, and performance, particularly within the context of evolving social, gender-related, and regional factors.

## Core Research Areas

Our research topics cover a broad spectrum of modern business challenges, including:

- **Leadership and Governance:** Family leadership, governance structures, strategy development, and CEO succession planning.
- **Gender Dynamics:** Female entrepreneurship and addressing gender bias in business.
- **Venture Creation:** Crowdfunding mechanisms and new venture forms.
- **Sustainability:** Social, sustainable, and international entrepreneurship models.
- **Social Capital:** The role of social capital, socio-emotional wealth, and relationships in entrepreneurship.

By exploring topics such as family firm succession, female entrepreneurship, crowdfunding, and the future of work, our research aims to foster more inclusive, resilient, and sustainable entrepreneurial ecosystems.
