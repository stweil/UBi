# LWDA 2018 Conference Overview

The conference, titled **Lernen, Wissen, Daten, Analysen (LWDA)**, was held from August 22–24, 2018, in Mannheim. It was hosted by the [Data and Web Science group](http://dws.informatik.uni-mannheim.de/en/home/) of the [University of Mannheim](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/).

LWDA expands to **"Learning, Knowledge, Data, Analytics"**, covering recent research in key areas including:

- Knowledge discovery
- Machine learning & data mining
- Knowledge management
- Database management & information systems
- Information retrieval

The conference was organized by and brought together various special interest groups of the [Gesellschaft für Informatik (GI)](https://gi.de/) (German Computer Science Society).

## Conference Program Structure

The program featured joint research sessions and [keynotes](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/program/keynotes/), alongside specialized workshops organized by each special interest group.

**Workshops presented in 2018:**

- [Workshop on „Information Retrieval“ (FGIR 2018)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/calls/fgir-2018/)
- [Workshop on „Knowledge Management“ (FGWM 2018)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/calls/fgwm-2018/)
- [Workshop on „Knowledge Discovery, Data Mining, and Machine Learning“ (KDML 2018)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/calls/kdml-2018/)
- [Workshop on „Business Intelligence“ (WSBI 2018)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/calls/wsbi-2018/)
- [Workshop on „Large-Scale Data Management and Processing – Applications in Research and Industry“ (FGDB 2018)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/lwda-2018/calls/fgdb-2018/)
