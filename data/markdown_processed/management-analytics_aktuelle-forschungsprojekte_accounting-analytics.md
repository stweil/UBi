# Accounting Analytics Research Projects

This section outlines current research projects focusing on accounting analytics, tax law, and corporate finance, conducted by the University of Mannheim.

## German Business Panel (GBP)

**Authors:**
[Jannis Bischof](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/bischof/), [Dirk Simons](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/simons/), [Johannes Voget](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/voget/), Davud Rostam-Afschar, [Philipp Dörrenberg](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/doerrenberg/team/prof-dr-philipp-doerrenberg/)

**Cooperation Partners:**
DFG (German Research Foundation), Paderborn University, HU Berlin, LMU Munich, Frankfurt School of Finance and Management, WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management, European School of Management and Technology Berlin, Goethe University Frankfurt, Bundesverband der Deutschen Industrie, Institut für Mittelstand, Zentralverband des Deutschen Handwerks.

**Description:**
The German Business Panel (GBP) is a representative panel of managers responsible for accounting and tax matters in German firms. These representatives are surveyed semi-annually on topics covering financial accounting, managerial accounting, and taxation. The panel invites both firms and researchers to submit survey questions for potential inclusion. A scientific committee selects the most suitable questions, and firms can vote on top research questions. The project regularly provides indicators for tax sentiments and other relevant issues.

**Website:** [https://gbpanel.org/](https://gbpanel.org/)

## Digitalization of the Tax Function

**Authors:**
[Christoph Spengel](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/spengel/), Alexander Maedche, Sven Scheu, Sarah Winter

**Cooperation Partners:**
Institute of Information Systems and Marketing (IISM) at KIT, EnBW AG

**Description:**
Due to increasing regulatory requirements, tax departments must process structured and unstructured tax data efficiently to improve decision-making. This project collaborates with a major German energy supplier to develop concepts for the digital transformation of the tax function. The focus is on optimizing tax department processes using digital means, enhancing existing tax information systems, and improving intragroup information processing. Furthermore, the project aims to develop data-driven intelligent assistant functions using Process Mining, Business Intelligence, and general Data Science techniques.

## Towards Financial Transparency: EU Directive on Public Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR)

**Authors:**
[Hannah Gundert](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/programs/cdsb/our-students/2022/hannah-gundert/), [Christoph Spengel](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/spengel/), [Stefan Weck](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/spengel/)

**Cooperation Partners:**
Chair of Business Administration and Taxation II, Taxation and Finance

**Description:**
The EU Directive 2021/2101 introduced a public Country-by-Country Reporting (CbCR) regime to promote a level playing field for businesses within the EU Single Market and bolster tax transparency for multinational enterprises (MNEs). This study examines whether the public CbCR Directive achieves its goal of reducing international tax planning disparities. The research specifically investigates whether the directive introduces unintended disparities between (1) MNEs domiciled in different EU member states and (2) MNEs operating within versus outside the EU. The findings suggest that the current form and implementation of the directive likely create unintended disparities, recommending stronger guidance and fewer transposition options.
