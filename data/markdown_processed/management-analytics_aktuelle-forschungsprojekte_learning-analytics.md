# Learning Analytics Research Projects

This document outlines current research initiatives focusing on the application, theory, and development of learning analytics within educational and vocational contexts.

## 📚 Featured Research Projects

### LeAP – Learning Analytics Profile

This project explores the field of learning analytics, which is rapidly growing at the intersection of data and computer science, and is becoming an important aspect of modern e-learning environments.

**Description:**
Implementing learning analytics into existing legacy systems within learning environments, especially when dealing with high data privacy concerns, presents a significant challenge. This research demonstrates how a learning analytics application can be implemented into an existing university environment by adding a plugin to the local digital learning environment. This plugin injects user-centric information to specific objects within the learning environment. The LeAP platform can be used in various learning and teaching scenarios to provide adaptive support for learners and teachers.

**Key Personnel:**

- **Dirk Ifenthaler:** [Profile Link](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/ifenthaler/)
- **Cooperation Partners:** Chair of Learning, Design and Technology

### Educational Data Literacy

This research project focuses on developing and validating a framework and providing professional learning opportunities related to Educational Data Literacy (EDL).

**Description:**
Educational Data Literacy (EDL) is defined as the ethically responsible collection, management, analysis, comprehension, interpretation, and application of data from educational contexts. Given the growing availability of educational data from digital learning environments, instructional designers, e-Trainers, and teachers gain significant information for enhancing personalized educational support and reflective course (re)design. The Learn2Analyze project has developed a comprehensive proposal for an Educational Data Literacy Competence Framework, comprising 6 competence dimensions and 17 competence statements, designed to enhance existing competence frameworks.

### Competence Development in VET Enculturation Processes

This DFG-funded research project analyzes competence development within Vocational Education Training (VET) and identifies relevant success factors for commercial apprentices.

**Description:**
The project models VET enculturation processes at both group and individual levels, including the description of company learning conditions. A validated competence assessment is used simultaneously with an instrument for recording the enculturation conditions at the beginning, middle, and end of initial vocational training (longitudinal design). This design generates practical findings about the genesis of professional competences, helping companies to improve their educational programs.
