# Marketing Analytics Research Projects

This page outlines current research projects investigating advanced topics in marketing analytics, consumer behavior, and media consumption.

## Advertising Over Time: Moving in the speed-of-life?

**Authors:** Leonie Gehrmann, [Florian Stahl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/quantitativemarketing/researchgroup/florianstahl/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Hebrew University (Israel), Economist
**Description:** This research analyzes how print advertising content has adapted to changes in the "speed-of-life"—defined as the rapidity and density of experiences, meanings, perceptions, and activities. The developed image mining algorithm is highly extendable, allowing analysis of social media postings and other magazines. The findings also offer direct insights into the existing competition for consumer attention due to increasing advertising clutter.

## The Impact of the Offered Product Mix on the Acquisition, Usage and Retention of Information Goods

**Authors:** [Daniela Schmitt](https://www.novasbe.unl.pt/en/faculty-research/faculty/faculty-detail/id/1170/daniela-schmitt), Raghuram Iyengar, [Florian Stahl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/quantitativemarketing/researchgroup/florianstahl/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Wharton School (Philadelphia)
**Description:** This project addresses the challenge for information goods providers (e.g., news websites) of balancing free users and paid subscribers. It investigates what content users consume and how inter-session consumption dynamics affect subscription likelihood. The model-free evidence suggests that increased paid content raises acquisition and retention probability, though this effect is moderated by consumption volume, and some users abandon the site when paid content increases. The results provide actionable implications for firms using the freemium business model.

## A friendly turn: advertising bias in the news media

**Authors:** Florens Focke, [Alexandra Niessen-Ruenzi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/niessen-ruenzi/), [Stefan Ruenzi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/ruenzi/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Chair of International Finance & Chair of Corporate Governance
**Description:** This paper investigates whether newspapers report more favorably about advertising corporate clients compared to other firms. Using high-dimensional fixed effects and high-frequency advertising data, the study finds that advertising leads to more positive press coverage in both local and national newspapers. The bias is particularly evident in less negative reporting following bad news events (e.g., negative earnings surprises).

## Prediction of Consumer Behavior

**Authors:** Yasid Soufi, [Florian Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/kraus/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Chair of Sales & Services Marketing
**Description:** By utilizing data on behavioral patterns, consumer characteristics, and self-reported traits, this research aims to anticipate customer behavior in an app-based context. Applying machine learning algorithms to enriched datasets helps understand the mechanisms underlying consumer decisions, optimizing the sales process and leading to higher customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## Marketing in Finance

**Authors:** Yasid Soufi, [Florian Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/kraus/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Chair of Sales & Services Marketing
**Description:** This research explores how skillful communication in financial markets can develop new financing sources and improve sentiment toward a company's value potential. High-quality Investor Relations (IR) is critical for firms undergoing an Initial Public Offering (IPO). By crawling and analyzing large amounts of textual data, the project aims to define how marketing communication should be framed within the context of Investor Relations.

## Maximizing Customer Lifetime Value through Strategic Channel Management: How to Incentivize Customers to Use a Mobile App versus a Website

**Authors:** Gokhan Gecer, [Florian Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/kraus/)
**Cooperation Partners:** Chair of Sales & Services Marketing
**Description:** Given that mobile apps increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) in online purchasing, this study suggests that online retailers should steer customers to the mobile channel by offering a permanent discount. The model determines the optimal discount value and the target customer segment. The findings suggest that discounts should be used primarily to increase customer retention, rather than for new customer acquisition.

## Variation in review writing behavior across time-of-the-day, week day, season, weather conditions, a city’s life-quality and other factors

**Authors:** [Andreas Bayerl](https://www.eur.nl/en/people/andreas-bayerl), [Florian Stahl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/quantitativemarketing/researchgroup/florianstahl/), Jacob Goldenberg
**Cooperation Partners:** IDC Israel, Kununu.de
**Description:** Analyzing over 10 million online reviews from two platforms, this project seeks to identify differences in review writing behavior based on external factors such as time of day, day of the week, season, weather, and city life quality. The research demonstrates how online reviews, intended to be unbiased, can be influenced by externalities. Methods include web scraping, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Machine Learning to predict review scores based on these external factors.

## Ad-blockers and Content Differentiation

**Authors:** Gokhan Gecer, [Florian Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/kraus/), Pinar Yildirim
**Cooperation Partners:** Wharton School (Philadelphia)
**Description:** Addressing the threat posed by ad-blockers, this project develops a game-theoretical model of a media ecosystem involving publishers and users. Publishers maximize profit from advertising and/or subscriptions. The model analyzes two publisher strategies: showing content only to those who disable ad-blockers, or showing it to both paying subscribers and those who disable ad-blockers. Results show that content cost influences quality, and the decision to offer a subscription option depends on the content's substitutability and production cost.
