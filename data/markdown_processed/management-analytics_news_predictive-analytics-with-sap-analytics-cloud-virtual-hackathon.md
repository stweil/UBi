# Predictive Analytics with SAP Analytics Cloud: Virtual Hackathon

This document summarizes the details for the Predictive Analytics with SAP Analytics Cloud Virtual Hackathon, an event designed for University of Mannheim students.

## Event Overview

The hackathon provided students with a unique, hands-on opportunity to participate in an SAP Smart Predict Hackathon, typically reserved for SAP customers.

- **Date & Time:** May 4, 2021, 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Format:** Virtual via Zoom
- **Participants:** Open to University of Mannheim students regardless of their current coding expertise level.

## Learning Objectives & Activities

The one-day concept combined theoretical learning with intensive practical application:

1. **Expert Presentations:** Short introductory presentations delivered by SAP data science experts.
1. **Hands-on Learning:** Assisted learning and group work sessions utilizing advanced capabilities of SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC).
1. **Core Skills:** Participants used SAC to:
   - Identify complex data patterns.
   - Develop suitable analytical models for predictions.
   - Visualize results effectively.
   - Recommend actionable courses of action based on provided business problems.

## Organization and Key Personnel

- **Prof. Dr. Hartmut Hoehle:** Chair of Enterprise Systems and Co-Director of the Management Analytics Center.
- **Hannah Sperling:** PhD candidate and SAP Analytics Expert.
