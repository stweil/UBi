# WoMen in Tech Mannheim: An Inclusive Network for IT Careers

WoMen in Tech Mannheim is an inclusive student network designed for students of all disciplines at the University of Mannheim. Recognizing the increasing importance of IT literacy for a fulfilling career, the network serves as a vital platform for discussion, exchange, and upskilling in the tech sector.

The network aims to excite upcoming generations about technology by fostering multifaceted discussions around topics such as:

- Analytics
- Coding
- Data Science
- Corporate IT
- Big Tech in Politics

While the network welcomes all students regardless of gender expression, ethnicity, background, age, or field of study, female students are particularly encouraged to participate to enrich the perspectives within the tech world.

## Featured Event: Demystifying IT – Job Profiles of the Future

Students from all faculties are invited to the spring semester's WIT event, **“Demystifying IT – Job Profiles of the Future.”** This session offers a unique opportunity to engage directly with leading IT practitioners.

**Event Details:**

- **Date:** May 6th
- **Time:** 8:00 PM
- **Format:** The event includes a discussion round on the future of IT and promising job profiles, followed by break-out rooms for direct interaction and Q&A.

**Featured Industry Experts:**
Attendees will have the opportunity to meet and speak with:

- **Nina Weingarten:** Business Lead Cloud Migration at Microsoft
- **Sean Kask, Ph.D.:** Artificial Intelligence Strategy Officer at SAP
- **Anne Lauscher:** NLP Doctoral Researcher at University of Mannheim
- **Philipp Wortmann:** Program Manager at Google Cloud

**Discussion Facilitator:**
The discussion will be facilitated by **Hannah Sperling**, Doctoral Student at the University of Mannheim & Analytics Specialist at SAP SE.
