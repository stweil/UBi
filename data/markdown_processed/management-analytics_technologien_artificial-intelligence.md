## Research Projects in Management Analytics

This document summarizes two research projects focusing on the application of advanced technologies, such as image mining and machine learning, to analyze consumer behavior and marketing effectiveness.

### 1. Advertising Over Time: Adapting to the Speed-of-Life

This study analyzes how print advertising content has adapted to changes in the "speed-of-life," defined as the "rapidity and density of experiences, meanings, perceptions, and activities" (Werner et al. 1985).

**Researchers:**

- Leonie Gehrmann
- [Florian Stahl](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/quantitativemarketing/researchgroup/florianstahl/)

**Partners:**
Hebrew University (Israel), Economist

**Key Findings & Implications:**

- **Image Mining:** The developed image mining algorithm is highly relevant for various use cases and can be easily extended beyond print advertisements to analyze images from other magazines or social media postings.
- **Marketing Practice:** The research addresses the recognized phenomenon of increasing advertising clutter, providing insights into the existing competition for consumer attention.

### 2. Prediction of Consumer Behavior

This project aims to gain deep insights into the predictors of a customer’s purchase behavior and personal traits to enable a more flexible and adaptive sales process.

**Researchers:**

- Yasid Soufi
- [Florian Kraus](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/kraus/)

**Partners:**
Chair of Sales & Services Marketing

**Methodology & Goals:**
By utilizing machine learning algorithms on enriched datasets—combining behavioral patterns, consumer characteristics, and self-reported traits—the research seeks to anticipate customer behavior within an app-based context. This leads to a better understanding of the mechanisms underlying consumer decisions.

**Applications:**
The insights derived are valuable for both customers and providers. Optimized processes, driven by predictive abilities, are expected to lead to higher customer satisfaction and improved operational efficiency.
