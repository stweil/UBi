# Blockchain Technology as an Enabler for Supply Chain Sustainability

**Authors:**

- [Prof. Dr. Christoph Bode](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/bode/)
- [Jonas Ronellenfitsch](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/bode/team/alumni/dr-ronellenfitsch-jonas/)

## Research Overview

The increasing prominence of cryptocurrencies has generated significant momentum for Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLT), such as Blockchain. Similarly, environmental movements, like the Friday for Future protests, have driven focus toward sustainability. Both topics are currently among the most discussed subjects within academic and industry supply chain circles.

This research project addresses this intersection by reviewing the enabling roles of blockchain technology in achieving supply chain sustainability and conceptualizing the corresponding operational domains.

## Key Contributions and Findings

The study illuminates how the adoption of this novel technology can drive sustainability by creating causal relationships across various fields of supply chain management. Specifically, the technology is shown to facilitate increases in:

- Value
- Trust
- Inclusiveness
- Economic Efficiency
- Environmental Efficiency

These combined improvements are key drivers for overall supply chain sustainability.
