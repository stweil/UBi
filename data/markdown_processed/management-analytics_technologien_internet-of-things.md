# Advanced Computing Paradigms for the Internet of Things (IoT)

The increasing demand for computational power in modern applications, such as machine learning and computer vision, necessitates advanced computing architectures that can operate efficiently on resource-constrained devices. This research area focuses on optimizing computation through offloading techniques and managing complex, interconnected systems like Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS).

## Computation Offloading for Edge Computing

Computation offloading addresses the limitations of local processing power by intelligently distributing workloads.

- **Concept:** Devices offload intensive tasks to remote resource providers, which can be located in the cloud or at the edge network. The results are then returned to the device via the network.
- **Benefits:**
  - **Performance:** Accelerates computationally intensive tasks.
  - **Efficiency:** Significantly reduces the energy consumption of the local offloading device.
- **Tasklet Project:** This DFG-funded project develops a comprehensive computation offloading system designed for fast and energy-efficient edge computing. The Tasklet system is notable for its ability to allow heterogeneous devices to offload arbitrary tasks to a heterogeneous pool of resources, making it highly attractive for diverse IoT applications.

## Managing Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and Mixed-Critical Networks

As computing devices become increasingly mobile and interconnected, the research focus has shifted to managing complex, interacting systems known as Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS).

- **CPS Context:** CPS are omnipresent in modern life, found in smart home environments, remote patient monitoring, and traffic management systems. In these scenarios, multiple CPSs must cooperate and interact.
- **Challenge (MC-CPNs):** A significant challenge is managing Mixed-Critical Cyber-Physical Networks (MC-CPNs), where tasks with different levels of criticality must be handled simultaneously within the network.
- **Research Goal:** To simplify the design, operation, and maintenance of MC-CPNs, a comprehensive approach is needed to handle complexity autonomously at runtime.
- **Solution Focus:** The development of learning self-adaptation mechanisms. These mechanisms aim to optimize task execution according to their specific Quality-of-Service (QoS) requirements relative to their criticality level. This entire management and adaptation capability is encapsulated within a middleware framework, ensuring a robust and flexible MC-CPN.
