# Research Projects: Applying Process Mining to Business Transformation

This document summarizes several research projects demonstrating the application of Process Mining, Business Intelligence, and Data Science to optimize core corporate functions, including Tax, Procurement, and Production.

## 1. Digitalization of the Tax Function

This project addresses the increasing regulatory demands on tax departments to process large volumes of structured and unstructured tax data efficiently.

- **Goal:** To develop concepts for the digital transformation of the tax function by optimizing processes and enhancing existing tax information systems.
- **Methodology:** The project utilizes Process Mining, Business Intelligence, and Data Science to develop data-driven intelligent assistant functions that support complex decision-making processes.
- **Collaboration:** Institute of Information Systems and Marketing (IISM) at KIT, EnBW AG.
- **Authors:** [Christoph Spengel](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/spengel/), Alexander Maedche, Sven Scheu, Sarah Winter.

## 2. Employee Responses to Error in Production Settings

This study examines how production staff react to errors when monitored by emergent technologies.

- **Goal:** To measure and understand employee behavior in response to reclamations (errors) within a core production environment.
- **Methodology:** Process Mining is applied to analyze production-level behavior data collected over a three-year period. The analysis uses a difference-in-difference (DID) design, combining statistical methods with process mining on large-scale ERP data (1.4 million production cases).
- **Contribution:** Provides practitioners with new insights on making effective decisions both before and after an error is reported.
- **Collaboration:** Aalberts Material Technology GmbH.
- **Authors:** Rajiv Sabherwal, [Hartmut Höhle](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/), [Tobias Nisius](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/), [Florian Pethig](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/team/wissenschaftliche-mitarbeiter/alumni/florian-pethig/), [Fareed Zandkarimi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/).

## 3. Process Mining Meets Procurement: Measuring Inefficiencies in P2P Processes

This research focuses on identifying and quantifying inefficiencies within critical procurement processes, such as Purchase-to-Pay (P2P).

- **Goal:** To apply process mining methods to analyze process execution data from large industrial companies to identify, measure, explain, and eliminate inefficiencies in procurement workflows.
- **Methodology:** An interdisciplinary team analyzes P2P data from three major industrial companies. The research results in the development of new Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that precisely quantify process inefficiency, allowing for the identification of temporal patterns or the impact of external events (e.g., the Corona pandemic).
- **Collaboration:** Schott AG, Hilti AG, HeidelbergCement AG, and Heidelberger Druckmaschinen AG.
- **Authors:** [Prof. Dr. Christoph Bode](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/bode/), [Prof. Dr. Hartmut Höhle](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/), [Jun.-Prof. Dr. Jana-Rebecca Rehse](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/rehse/), [Jonas Ronellenfitsch](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/bode/team/alumni/dr-ronellenfitsch-jonas/), [Fareed Zandkarimi](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/hoehle/).
