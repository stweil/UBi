## Dissertation Project

**Title:** Scientific Observation in Perspective (Working Title)

The dissertation project focuses on developing a perspectivist account of scientific observation. The core question addressed is how the observational ability of human observers has been profoundly enlarged and enhanced by sophisticated apparatus.

The research investigates whether observational results gained using apparatus can be treated as a simple continuum of human sensory perception. The project argues from a perspectivist viewpoint that there is no such simple continuum. The primary goal is to develop a perspectivist theoretical framework that bridges the gap between apparatus-based observation and human sense-based observation.

Within this project, Lyu Xingyu specifically investigates self-ascriptions of visual experiences, particularly visual experiences of colors (e.g., "I see a red door" or "This lamp seems black to me").
