## Dissertation Project: Expressing Myself: Disavowals and the Mind (Working Title)

The dissertation project focuses on developing an expressivist analysis of negated avowals. While existing expressivist theories (citing Wittgenstein 1953; Bar-On 2004, 2015; Finkelstein 2003; Freitag 2014, 2018) have primarily addressed positive avowals, negated avowals (disavowals)—such as, “I don’t hope that it is raining”—present a significant challenge to avowal expressivism. The core problem is determining what the utterance of a negated avowal is supposed to express, specifically how one can express the *absence* of a mental state.

The purpose of this dissertation is to examine negated avowals. The author tentatively claims that, despite the noted difficulties, these disavowals can indeed receive an expressivist interpretation. The proposal is that disavowals constitute cases of expressive denegation, thereby avoiding a descriptivist backlash. Furthermore, an expressivist interpretation of disavowals is expected to contribute to a deeper understanding of various philosophical problems, including Moore’s paradox and the suspension of belief.
