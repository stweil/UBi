## Research Focus

My research interests are centered on the history of modern philosophy, with a special focus on Immanuel Kant, and on the philosophy of mind and psychology.

**Key Areas of Interest:**

- Kant’s conception of empirical self-knowledge and the first-person perspective, including its special epistemic status, its relation to rationality, and its relevance for scientific psychology.
- Philosophy of science, epistemology, and moral psychology.
