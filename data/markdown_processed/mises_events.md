# Events and Seminars at MISES

The MISES page lists various seminars and events focusing on sustainable energy topics.

## Featured Seminars and Topics

The following topics have been featured in recent seminars:

- The Distributional Impacts of Real-Time Pricing in the Spanish Residential Electricity Market (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Environmental justice and climate, air pollution, and economics of decisions in the U.S. power sector (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Corporate Sustainability: A Strategy? (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Deregulation, Market Power, and Prices: Evidence from the Electricity Sector (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Are New Vehicle Emissions Standards Effective and Efficient? (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Gains from Trade in Global Electricity Markets (Decarbonization Seminar)
- Mandatory Energy Efficiency Disclosure in Housing Markets (Decarbonization Seminar)
- *Renewable vs Traditional Power Generation: State of the Race* (Presented by Gunther Glenk) (Decarbonization Seminar)

## Featured Presentations

- **Vorstellung des MISES bei der Sitzung des Universitätsrats:** Stefan Reichelstein presents the Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES) during the University Council meeting.
- **Reducing the Cost of Renewable Hydrogen:** Stefan Reichelstein was a speaker at the web seminar organized by Ata Insights.
