# Decarbonization Seminar

The Decarbonization Seminar hosts speakers and participants from academia and industry to discuss the latest advances and challenges associated with the transition towards a decarbonized economy. Topics covered include the economics and management of corporate emissions, decarbonization pathways, and incentives for climate action.

## Seminar Logistics and Participation

**Frequency and Timing:**
Seminar sessions are usually held on **Monday afternoons** from 05:15 to 06:30 pm (CET or CEST).

**Participation Options:**

- **In Person:** Attendees can join in the room indicated on the respective speaker page.
- **Virtual:** Advance registration is required using the link provided with each [talk announcement](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/mises/newsletter-decarbonization-seminar/).

**Stay Informed:**
To receive updates on upcoming presentations, please sign up for the seminar’s mailing list via [this form](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/newsletter-decarbonization-seminar/).

## Organization and Sponsorship

The seminar is jointly sponsored by:

- Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)
- Center for European Economic Research (ZEW)

**Organizing Team:**
The team includes Amadeus Bach (MISES), Gunther Glenk (MISES), Marion Ott (ZEW), and Nikolas Wölfing (MISES).

**Contact:**
For questions or suggestions, please contact Yvonne Münch.
