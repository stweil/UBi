# Decarbonizing Transport – The Contribution of Biofuels: An Industry Perspective from CropEnergies

- **Topic:** Decarbonizing Transport – The Contribution of Biofuels. An industry perspective from CropEnergies.
- **Speaker:** Stephan Meeder
- **Company:** CropEnergies AG
- **Date & Time:** September 13, 2021, 17:00 – 18:15 (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Note: Registration was no longer available at the time of viewing.)

## Speaker Biography: Stephan Meeder

Stephan Meeder holds a degree in Business Administration from the University of Mannheim and the Ecole Supérieure des Sciences Economiques et Commerciales, Cergy-Pontoise, France.

His professional experience includes:

- **1996–2000:** ABB Structured Finance GmbH
- **1999:** Earned his doctorate from the University of Mannheim (Faculty of Logistics).
- **2000–2001:** KPMG (Corporate Finance Department)
- **2001–2006:** Robert Bosch GmbH (Corporate Finance and M&A)
- **November 2006:** Joined the Corporate Finance department of Südzucker AG.
- **May 2007:** Appointed Chief Financial Officer of Saint Louis Sucre SA, Paris.
- **April 2015:** Appointed to the executive board of CropEnergies AG as Chief Financial Officer.
- **July 2020–Present:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of CropEnergies AG.

Additionally, Stephan Meeder serves as the vice chairman of the European ethanol association ePURE.
