# From Paris to Mannheim

## Event Details

**Speaker:** Georg Müller
**Affiliation:** MVV AG (CEO)
**Date & Time:** November 1, 2021, 17:00 – 18:15 (CET)
**Format:** Live virtual event
*Note: Registration is no longer available.*

## Speaker Biography

Georg Müller serves as the Chairman of the Executive Board (CEO) of MVV Energie AG. Previously, Mr. Müller held roles such as Chairman of the Executive Board of RWE Rhein-Ruhr AG in Essen and Member of the Executive Board of VSE AG in Saarbrücken. He is also a member of the supervisory boards of several comparable German and foreign companies, including Energieversorgung Offenbach AG, Grosskraftwerk Mannheim AG, and MVV Umwelt GmbH, Mannheim.
