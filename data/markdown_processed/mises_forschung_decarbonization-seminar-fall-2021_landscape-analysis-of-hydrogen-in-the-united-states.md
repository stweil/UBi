# Landscape Analysis of Hydrogen in the United States

**Speaker:** Stephen Comello
**Affiliation:** Stanford University
**Date:** December 6, 2021
**Time:** 05:00 – 06:15 PM (CET)
**Format:** Live virtual event (Registration no longer available)

## Speaker Biography

Stephen Comello is the director of the Energy Business Innovations focus area at Stanford Graduate School of Business and a senior research fellow at the Steyer-Taylor Center for Energy, Policy and Finance at Stanford University. He also serves as a techno-economic and policy lead for Stanford University’s Bits & Watts Initiative and StorageX Initiative, and is a co-instructor for the Building Energy Efficiency online course. His research focuses broadly on energy technology economics, policy, and innovation. Specifically, he investigates the innovation and competitiveness of low-carbon energy solutions in both developed and emerging economies by applying tools and methods from finance, economics, and systems analysis.

## Seminar Abstract

This seminar presents an industry analysis designed to understand the current and potential future states of the hydrogen supply chain within the United States. The analysis addresses two core questions:

1. To what extent is hydrogen a viable decarbonization option for the United States?
1. How might this decarbonization manifest across various industries?

The methodology employed is qualitative social research, emphasizing contextual accuracy through direct observation and communication with participants. The primary tool used was a semi-structured interviewing approach, which gathered data from seventy-two interviews conducted between April and July 2021, resulting in over 55 hours of discussion. This primary data was supplemented and supported by reviews of cost, business model, and policy literature.

The findings are structured into three main areas:

- **Cross-Cutting Themes:** Eight general themes synthesizing the current state and outlook of hydrogen in the U.S.
- **Industry View:** An assessment of which value chains and specific components have reached broad commercial viability and identifying areas needing further development.
- **Firm-Level View:** A description of generalized business models for hydrogen value chains that are currently viable or rapidly emerging.
