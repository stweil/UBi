# Scope 3 Carbon Emissions: Out of Sight Out of Mind

This document summarizes details from the seminar featuring George Serafeim on Scope 3 Carbon Emissions.

## Event Details

- **Topic:** Scope 3 Carbon Emissions: Out of Sight Out of Mind
- **Speaker:** George Serafeim
- **Date & Time:** October 18, 2021 – 05:00 – 06:15 PM (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Note: Registration was no longer available at the time of the original posting.)

## Speaker Profile: George Serafeim

George Serafeim is the Charles M. Williams Professor of Business Administration and the Faculty Chair of the Impact-Weighted Accounts Project at Harvard Business School.

**Experience Highlights:**

- He has taught courses in the MBA, executive education, and doctoral programs.
- He currently teaches the elective course “Reimagining Capitalism: Business and Big Problems” in the MBA curriculum, which received the Ideas Worth Teaching Award from the Aspen Institute and the Grand Page Prize.
- He has presented his research in over 60 countries globally, including to world leaders in government and business at major events such as the World Economic Forum at Davos and the Aspen Ideas Festival.
- He is recognized as a top author on the Social Science Research Network.
