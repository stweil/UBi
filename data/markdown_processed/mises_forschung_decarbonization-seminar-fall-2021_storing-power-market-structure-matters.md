# Storing Power: Market Structure Matters

**Speaker:** Natalia Fabra
**Affiliation:** Universidad Carlos III de Madrid
**Date:** October 4, 2021
**Format:** Live virtual event (Note: Registration was no longer available for this past event.)

### Seminar Abstract

This seminar assesses how firms' incentives to operate and invest in energy storage depend on the underlying market structure. The analysis characterizes equilibrium market outcomes considering market power in storage and/or production, as well as the effects of vertical integration between storage and production.

Market power is shown to reduce overall efficiency through two primary channels: it induces an inefficient use of storage facilities, and it distorts investment incentives. The worst outcome for consumers and total welfare occurs under vertical integration. The results provide key insights for regulating energy storage, an issue critical for the successful deployment of renewable energy sources.

### Speaker Biography: Natalia Fabra

Natalia Fabra is a Professor of Economics at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. She serves as a Research Fellow at the Centre for Economic Policy Research, an Associate Member of the Toulouse School of Economics, and a Research Fellow at the University of Cambridge Energy Policy Research Group. She is also a member of the Economic Advisory Group on Competition Policy (EAGCP) of the European Commission.

**Expertise:**
Natalia specializes in Industrial Organization, with a strong emphasis on Energy and Environmental Economics, and Regulation and Competition Policy. Her research has been published in leading journals, including the *American Economic Review*, *Management Science*, *The Rand Journal of Economics*, *The Economic Journal*, *Energy Economics*, and *The Journal of Industrial Economics*. She is an Associate Editor for *The Economic Journal* and *The Journal of Industrial Economics*.

**Awards and Fellowships:**

- She has received two distinguished awards as Spanish Best Young Economist (awarded by the Banco Sabadell Foundation and the Madrid regional government).
- In 2017, she was awarded an ERC Consolidator grant titled “Current Tools and Policy Challenges in Electricity Markets” (2018–2023).
- She has held Research Visiting Fellow positions at several institutions, including the University of California Energy Institute (Berkeley), Nuffield College (Oxford), the Toulouse School of Economics, Northwestern University (Chicago), and the Energy Policy Research Group (Cambridge).
