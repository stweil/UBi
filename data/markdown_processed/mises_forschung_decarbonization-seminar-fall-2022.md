# Decarbonization Seminar: Fall 2022 Archive

The Decarbonization Seminar in Fall 2022 brought together leading speakers and participants from academia and industry to discuss the latest advances and challenges associated with the transition towards a decarbonized energy economy.

**Current Seminar Information:**
For the current version of the seminar, please visit: [Decarbonization Seminar Page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar/)

**Organization and Sponsorship:**
The seminar was jointly sponsored by:

- Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)
- Center for European Economic Research (ZEW)

The organizing team included:

- [Gunther Glenk](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/area-accounting-taxation/team/gunther-glenk/) (MISES)
- [Marion Ott](https://www.zew.de/team/mot) (ZEW)
- [Nikolas Wölfing](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/area-accounting-taxation/team/dr-nikolas-woelfing/) (MISES)
