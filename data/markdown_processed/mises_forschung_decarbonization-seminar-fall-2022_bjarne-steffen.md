### Seminar Abstract

Decarbonizing the transport sector, one of the largest carbon emitters globally, is crucial for meeting the Paris Agreement’s climate change goals. While battery electric vehicles are expected to dominate the passenger vehicle market, the efficient decarbonization of the road freight sector remains unclear. Technological options include battery electric, green hydrogen, and synthetic fuel-powered vehicles.

This seminar addresses the lack of analysis regarding the competition between these "green" technologies and, specifically, how public policy can shape this competition to accelerate sector decarbonization. The challenge is compounded by the fact that potential interventions often occur at the local level, yet their innovation effects can spill over to other jurisdictions.

To address this, the presentation introduces a system dynamic model to project technology market shares across regions (North America, China, Europe) and applications (from small urban to large long-haul trucks) over the next two decades. Key findings include:

- Electric vehicles are projected to grow in most application segments.
- Growth can be substantially accelerated through policy intervention.
- Spillovers occur if strong policies are introduced in large regions like China and the European Union.
- While public interventions are essential for niche technology deployment, private interventions show minimal positive spillover effects.

### Speaker Biography

Bjarne Steffen is an Assistant Professor of Climate Finance and Policy and the head of CFP. His research, published in high-impact journals, focuses on policies related to the low-carbon transition in the energy and financial sectors. His work operates at the intersection of environmental/energy economics, financial economics, and innovation studies, covering both developed and developing countries.

- **Developed Countries Focus:** Analyzing the impact of public policy on technology pathways for energy storage, and incorporating technology- and country-specific cost of capital into energy system models.
- **Developing Countries Focus:** Addressing clean energy technology diffusion and the role of multilateral development banks.
