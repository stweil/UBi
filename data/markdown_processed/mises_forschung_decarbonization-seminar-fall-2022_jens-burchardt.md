# Jens Burchardt: Navigating the Road to Net Zero

**Speaker:** [Jens Burchardt](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar-fall-2022/jens-burchardt/)
**Affiliation:** Boston Consulting Group (BCG)
**Event:** Navigating the road to net zero – in an energy crisis
**Date & Time:** October 10, 2022 – 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CEST)
**Format:** Virtual only

## Seminar Abstract

The seminar addresses the critical challenge of decarbonization in Germany. The core questions explored include:

- How can Germany implement historic measures to almost halve emissions by 2030, especially when currently ramping up coal and LNG consumption to compensate for acute gas shortages?
- What steps are necessary to re-establish supply security while simultaneously achieving climate targets this decade?
- How can companies successfully navigate this transformation and create value from the decarbonization process?

## Speaker Biography

Jens Burchardt is BCG’s global expert on climate change. He brings approximately 12 years of international project experience focusing on:

- Climate impact
- The energy transition
- Low-emission technologies (including renewables, hydrogen, power-to-x, and CCUS)

He has served as an advisor to the World Economic Forum (WEF), COP26, and several German government commissions on climate. He is also a TED Speaker and led BCG’s landmark emission reduction studies, “Klimapfade für Deutschland” (“Climate Paths for Germany”), in collaboration with German industry.
