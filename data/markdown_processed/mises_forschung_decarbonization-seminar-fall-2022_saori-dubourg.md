# Saori Dubourg: Energy Transformation for the Chemical Industry

This profile details the presentation by Saori Dubourg, a member of the Executive Board of BASF SE, at the Decarbonization Seminar.

**Speaker:** Saori Dubourg
**Date & Time:** November 23, 2022 – 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CET)
**Location:**

- **In Person:** Room O 131

## Speaker Biography: Saori Dubourg

Saori Dubourg has held a position on the Executive Board of BASF SE since May 2017. Her career spans extensive leadership roles across global business units and strategic advisory positions.

**Current Responsibilities at BASF SE:**

- **Global Leadership:** Since March 2022, she leads the global businesses of Monomers, Performance Materials, Petrochemicals, and Intermediates.
- **Regional Oversight:** She is responsible for the Europe region, the startup trinamiX, and the BASF Corporate Sustainability Board.
- **Previous Roles:** Until February 2022, she managed the global businesses in Agricultural Solutions, Nutrition & Health, and Care Chemicals.

**Career Highlights:**

- **Experience:** Dubourg has been with BASF since 1996, working in various functions across the USA, Japan, Singapore, and Hong Kong. Her expertise covers strategy and operational leadership in areas such as dispersions, monomers, superabsorbents, nutrition, and pharmaceuticals.
- **Executive Leadership:** She previously led BASF's senior executive human resources and served as president for all country operations and functional areas in Asia Pacific.
- **Division Presidency:** From 2013 to May 2017, she was the president of the Nutrition & Health division.
- **Strategic Transactions:** In 2018, she played a key role in the acquisition of businesses and assets from Bayer, strengthening BASF's presence in crop protection, biotechnology, and digital farming. In 2020, she oversaw the divestment of the construction chemicals division to Lone Star.

**Sustainability and Advisory Roles:**

- **EU Policy:** As a member of the EU Commission's Industry Policy Group, she contributed to the vision paper on Europe 2030 (published June 2019).
- **Value Creation:** She champions holistic, future-oriented corporate steering under the theme 'Value to Society'. She chairs the steering committee of the Value-Balancing Alliance, an initiative founded in June 2019 aimed at standardizing the measurement of a company's value contribution across environment, society, and economy.
- **German Advisory:** Since January 2020, she has been a member of the German Council for Sustainable Development, advising the German government on sustainability strategy implementation.
