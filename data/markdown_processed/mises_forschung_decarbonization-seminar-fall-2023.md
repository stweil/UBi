# Decarbonization Seminar Series

The **Decarbonization Seminar** hosts speakers and participants from academia and industry to discuss the latest advances and challenges associated with the transition towards a decarbonized energy economy.

This seminar was jointly sponsored by the [Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar/) and the [Center for European Economic Research (ZEW)](https://www.zew.de/).

**Current Seminar Information:**
You can find the current version of the seminar [here](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar/).

**Organizing Team:**
The organizing team includes members from:
