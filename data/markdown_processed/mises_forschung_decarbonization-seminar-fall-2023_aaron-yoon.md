## Seminar Abstract

This seminar examines the shareholder value implications derived from robust supply chain ESG risk management. The research finds that companies with fewer supply chain ESG incidents demonstrate higher future stock returns and improved accounting performance. Robust risk management creates value by:

1. Attracting pro-social stakeholders.
1. Enhancing supply chain stability.
1. Hedging against regulatory risk.

The presentation further highlights that the lack of supply chain transparency prevents the stock market from immediately and fully capitalizing on the value generated by strong ESG risk management. Overall, the talk emphasizes the net benefits of robust supply chain ESG risk management and advises regulators and investors on the importance of relevant disclosure.

## Speaker Biography: Professor Aaron Yoon

Professor Yoon specializes in how to account for a firm's Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) efforts and integrate this information into portfolio decision-making processes.

**Research Impact:**
His research on ESG was noted by the *Financial Times* as a turning point in how investors view and integrate ESG information. The methodologies suggested in his work have since been widely implemented by asset managers. His work is regularly cited in major outlets such as Bloomberg, Forbes, The New York Times, and The Wall Street Journal.
