# Bjarne Steffen: Seminar Talk Details

**Affiliation:** Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich (ETH Zurich)

## Talk Overview

This presentation is part of the Symposium on „Carbon Emission Pledges and Corporate Decarbonization Initiatives.”

**Admission Note:** Admission for this talk is strictly by invitation only due to limited seating capacity.

## Speaker Biography

Bjarne Steffen is an Assistant Professor of Climate Finance and Policy and heads the CFP department. His research, published in high-impact journals, focuses on analyzing policies related to the low-carbon transition within the energy and financial sectors.

His work operates at the intersection of:

- Environmental/Energy Economics
- Financial Economics
- Innovation Studies

His research scope covers both developed and developing countries.

**Key Research Areas:**

- **Developed Countries:** Analyzing the impact of public policy on technology pathways for energy storage, and assessing the effect of incorporating technology- and country-specific cost of capital into energy system models.
- **Developing Countries:** Focusing on clean energy technology diffusion and the role of multilateral development banks.
