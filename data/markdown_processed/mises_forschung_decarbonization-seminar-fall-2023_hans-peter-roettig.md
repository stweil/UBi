## Seminar: Decarbonization of Automotive Retail from OEM's Perspective

**Speaker:** Hans-Peter Röttig
**Company:** Mercedes-Benz AG
**Date & Time:** October 16, 2023 – 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CEST)

**Participation Details:**

- **In Person:** Room O 129 (Castle, Ostflügel, Level 1, Room 29).
- **Virtual:** Advance registration is required via [this link](https://uni-mannheim.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5wtfuqrrjMpE9VCFTPtBPJzHWAhOCceyIBo).

### Seminar Abstract

With the goal of achieving net carbon-neutrality for the entire life cycle of new vehicles by 2039 (Ambition 2039), Mercedes-Benz' Sales Organization and Retail Network play a crucial role in the value chain. This seminar will provide an introduction to the Decarbonization strategy for the Retail Network, while also discussing open questions and challenges encountered during the strategy design process.

### Speaker Profile: Hans-Peter Röttig

Hans-Peter Röttig completed his Business Economics Studies at the University of Mannheim (1985–1991), earning a Dipl.-Kfm. degree specializing in Marketing, Industrial Management, and Physical Technology.

Since joining Mercedes-Benz AG in 1991, he has held various key roles within the Marketing & Sales domain. His experience includes overseeing Sales and Operations Planning for Commercial Vehicles, implementing Customer-based process costing, managing Customer Relationship Management in Passenger Car Marketing, spearheading the Customer Satisfaction Programme CSI No. 1, and contributing to Brand Management at Retail.

Currently, Hans-Peter Röttig serves as **Manager Retail Strategy**. In this capacity, his primary focus is developing strategies and supporting the implementation of sustainability initiatives across Mercedes-Benz' global sales organizations.
