# Michael Pahle: Climate and Energy Policy Expert

## Event Details

**Event:** Symposium on „Carbon Emission Pledges and Corporate Decarbonization Initiatives”
**Date:** September 27, 2023
**Affiliation:** Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK)
**Note:** Admission for this talk is by invitation only due to limited seating capacity.

## Speaker Biography

Michael Pahle is the head of the working group “Climate and Energy Policy” at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK). He holds a PhD in economics from TU Berlin.

### Research Focus & Expertise

His research centers on critical areas of climate mitigation and policy implementation:

- Emission trading systems.
- Public support for carbon pricing mechanisms.
- Regulatory frameworks necessary to achieve net-zero emissions.

### Professional Contributions

- **BMBF Project:** He serves as the Principle Investigator for “European Climate and Energy Policy” within the BMBF’s [Ariadne Project](https://www.kopernikus-projekte.de/en/projects/ariadne), which is Germany’s largest social science Energiewende research project.
- **Advisory Role:** Based on his research, Michael advises the German government and parliament, the EU Commission, and the EU Parliament on the development, reform, and extension of emission trading schemes.
- **Collaboration:** He actively collaborates with leading EU think tanks, including the European University Institute (Florence) and Bruegel (Brussels).
- **Global Network:** He is a member of the [Global Climate Policy Partnership](https://www.rff.org/gcpp/), a US-based global network connecting research institutions to help major economies and businesses meet ambitious climate goals.

### Media Presence

Michael’s work and views on climate policy have been featured in major national and international media outlets, including *FAZ*, *Spiegel*, *Süddeutsche*, *Zeit*, *Handelsblatt*, *El País*, *Politiken*, *Euractiv*, *Financial Times*, *Bloomberg*, *New York Times*, and *The Wall Street Journal*.
