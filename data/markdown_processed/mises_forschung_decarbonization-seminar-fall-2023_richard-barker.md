# Conceptual Foundations of Sustainability Reporting

This seminar features insights from the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) and the University of Oxford regarding the evolving landscape of sustainability reporting.

**Event Details**

- **Topic:** Conceptual foundations of sustainability reporting
- **Speaker:** Richard Barker
- **Date & Time:** October 23, 2023, 06:15 – 07:15 PM (CEST)
- **Format:** Online only
- **Registration:** Advance registration is required to join virtually via [this link](https://uni-mannheim.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5clcOChqzwoEt2xRjlnAGhtB0MwF0qp7f0m#/).

## Seminar Abstract

Sustainability reporting is entering a new era driven by the launch of European (ESRS) and International (ISSB) standards. This talk will provide a comprehensive overview of how these new standards interact with each other and how they build upon existing frameworks, including GRI, TCFD, SASB, and Integrated Reporting. The focus will be on the underlying concepts, evaluated against evolving institutional practices.

## Speaker Profile: Richard Barker

Richard Barker is a recognized expert in sustainability reporting standards.

- **Affiliations:** Full-time member of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) since July 2022.
- **Academic Role:** Professor of Accounting at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford (currently on leave).
- **Experience:** He has served on EFRAG’s Academic Panel and previously led the establishment of the European Accounting Association’s Stakeholder Reporting Committee.
