# Workshop: Advances in Climate Technologies

The **Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)** is hosting an interdisciplinary workshop titled **Advances in Climate Technologies**.

**Date:** October 28, 2024
**Location:** University of Mannheim (Barock Castle)

### Overview

This workshop aims to convene academic researchers, industry professionals, and policy experts. The primary goal is to assess the readiness and economic viability of key technologies crucial for the transition to a decarbonized economy.

**Topics Covered Include:**

- Hydrogen and E-Fuels
- Energy storage
- Shared autonomous electric vehicles
- Carbon capture and sequestration
- Carbon removals
- Climate engineering

Each subject presentation will be followed by a 20-minute open discussion, culminating in a plenary discussion.
