### 🎤 Speaker Biography

Ivo Welch is a Distinguished Professor of Finance and holds the J. Fred Weston Chair in Finance at UCLA Anderson. His academic career includes time at Yale’s School of Management and Brown University’s economics department.

Professor Welch is recognized as an influential economist, having authored numerous highly cited publications. His accolades include the prestigious Humboldt Research Award and multiple best paper awards in major finance journals. He has also served as the director of the UCLA Anderson Fink Center and the American Finance Association, and is an NBER research associate. His research interests span informational cascades, initial public offerings, capital structure, dividends, and socially responsible investing.

### 📝 Seminar Abstract: Moving the Needle on Climate Change

The seminar addresses the profound challenge of climate change, projecting a potential warming of approximately 2℃ by the end of the century.

**Key Arguments:**

- **Ineffectiveness of Current Activism:** The speaker argues that individual carbon-footprint activism, international negotiations, or mandated treaties are unlikely to achieve the necessary global emission cuts.
- **Focus on Decision-Makers:** The critical factor is not what the world *should* do, but what its *important decision-makers* will actually do.
- **Global Dynamics:** The OECD is no longer the sole determinant of global action, as non-OECD countries will soon house the majority of the world's population and emissions.
- **The Solution:** The most pressing task is the **intelligent phasing out of fossil fuels**. The speaker advocates for subsidizing research across all clean energy aspects, particularly storage, as cheaper clean energy will ultimately displace fossil fuels globally.
