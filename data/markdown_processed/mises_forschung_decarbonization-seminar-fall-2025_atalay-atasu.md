# Seminar: Circular Economy and Sustainable Operations with Atalay Atasu

## Event Details

**Speaker:** [Atalay Atasu](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar-fall-2025/atalay-atasu/) (INSEAD)

**Date & Time:** Monday, October 20, 2025 – 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CEST)

**Format:** This session will be held as a hybrid event.

- **In Person:** Room O 131 (Room 131 on level 1 of the east wing of the castle).
- **Virtual:** Advance registration is required via [this link](https://uni-mannheim.zoom-x.de/meeting/register/jk91ikOHS9-p4bUhRHxaJg).

## Seminar Abstract

This seminar will introduce the broader concept of a circular economy, demonstrating its essential role in the renewable energy transition. The discussion aims to illuminate the fundamental principles of circularity, address common misconceptions, and highlight the need for realism in current research efforts. The presentation will leverage a series of academic papers on the topic. Time permitting, the session will also discuss how academia can influence policy and identify opportunities for impactful research for interested academics.

## Speaker Biography: Atalay Atasu

Atalay Atasu (PhD INSEAD, 2007) is a Professor of Technology and Operations Management at INSEAD. His research focuses on sustainable operations management.

**Publications & Recognition:**
His work has been published in leading journals including *Management Science*, *Manufacturing and Service Operations Management*, *Production and Operations Management*, and *Journal of Industrial Ecology*. He also contributes to practitioner outlets such as *Harvard Business Review*, *Sloan Management Review*, and *California Management Review*.

Atalay’s research has received numerous accolades, including:

- Wickham Skinner Best Paper Award (Winner 2007, Runner up 2014)
- Wickham Skinner Early Career Research Award (2012)
- Paul Kleindorfer Award in Sustainability (2013)
- MSOM Responsible Research Award (2019)
- MSOM Best OM Paper Award (2021)

His research on circularity was recognized by *Harvard Business Review* as a McKinsey Best Article Award finalist in 2022.

**Professional Roles:**
He previously served as president of the Manufacturing and Service Operations Society and as Department Editor for POMS. He currently serves as an Associate/Senior Editor for *Management Science* and *POMS*, and as a department editor at *MSOM*.

**Initiatives:**
Atalay co-founded and co-directs the [INSEAD Business Sustainability Programme](https://www.insead.edu/executive-education/strategy/business-sustainability-programme) and the [INSEAD Sustainable Business Initiative](https://www.insead.edu/sustainable-business-initiative).
