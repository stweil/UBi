# Everett Salas: Advancing a Lower Carbon Future (Chevron)

This seminar session, presented by Everett Salas of Chevron, focuses on practical strategies for corporate emissions reduction and sustainable energy investment.

## Event Details

- **Topic:** Advancing a Lower Carbon Future
- **Date:** Monday, September 22, 2025
- **Time:** 15:00 – 16:30 PM (CEST)
- **Format:** Online only.
- **Registration:** Advance registration is required to join virtually via [this link](https://uni-mannheim.zoom-x.de/meeting/register/Nr8_IiJGRgmsblKxdePAHA).

## Seminar Abstract

The talk will explore Chevron's journey in emissions reduction, demonstrating cost-effective methods for addressing corporate emissions. Key discussion points include:

- Practical optimization of MACCs using portfolio theory.
- The necessity of robust carbon accounting standards to accelerate emissions reduction by providing decision-useful information.

## Speaker Profile: Everett Salas

Everett Salas serves as Principal Strategy Advisor at Chevron New Energies, where he leads Decision and Economic Analysis.

**Expertise:**
With over a decade of experience, his expertise spans:

- Guiding infrastructure investments and decarbonization strategies.
- Financial modeling (NPV, ROI, IRR, payback, OpEx/CapEx forecasting).
- Probabilistic analysis.

His work emphasizes capital allocation, infrastructure finance, and portfolio optimization, translating complex data into actionable insights for energy and climate solutions.
