# Mandatory Energy Efficiency Disclosure in Housing Markets

**Event Details**

- **Speaker:** Steven Puller
- **Affiliation:** Texas A&M University
- **Date:** Monday, June 14, 2021
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Registration was closed)

## Seminar Abstract

Mandatory disclosure policies are implemented broadly despite sparse evidence that they improve market outcomes. This seminar studies the effects of requiring home sellers to provide buyers with certified audits of residential energy efficiency.

**Key Findings:**

- Using similar nearby homes as a comparison group, the research finds that this requirement increases price premiums for energy efficiency and encourages energy-saving investments.
- The study also presents evidence highlighting a market failure—incomplete information by both buyers and sellers—that prevents widespread voluntary disclosure of energy efficiency in housing transactions.
- The findings support that disclosure policies can improve market outcomes in settings with symmetrically incomplete information.

## Speaker Biography

**Steven L. Puller** is a Professor at the Department of Economics at Texas A&M University, and holds several other affiliations, including NBER Research Associate, Research Affiliate at the International Growth Centre, and faculty affiliate at the E2e Project. He is also an adjunct professor at the Bush School of Government and Public Service.

Prof. Puller completed his undergraduate studies in Economics at the University of Chicago and obtained his PhD in Economics at UC Berkeley. His research focuses on industrial economics, regulation, and public policy, with a specific emphasis on energy and transportation. He has published in top general interest and field journals such as the *AER*, *AEJ Economic Policy*, *RAND*, *JPubE*, and the *Review of Economics and Statistics*. His most recent work concerns energy efficiency policies and the design of electricity wholesale and retail markets.
