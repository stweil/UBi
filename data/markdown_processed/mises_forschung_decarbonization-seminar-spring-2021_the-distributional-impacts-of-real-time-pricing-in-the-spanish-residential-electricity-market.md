# The Distributional Impacts of Real-Time Pricing in the Spanish Residential Electricity Market

**Event Details**

- **Speaker:** Mar Reguant (Northwestern University)
- **Date:** Monday, March 8, 2021
- **Time:** 01:30 – 02:45 PM (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Registration was no longer available at the time of the document's context)

## Seminar Abstract

While the benefits of Real-Time Pricing (RTP) of electricity are well known, less is known about their distributional impacts. This paper examines the distributional impacts of RTP by leveraging a country-wide field experiment. Since 2015, RTP has been the default option for most Spanish households. Access to hourly consumption data for over a year, covering more than 4 million households, allows the computation of bill impacts resulting from the switch from flat rates to RTP.

By examining the households' sociodemographic characteristics, the research documents who benefits and who loses from RTP. Furthermore, the authors propose a method to infer consumers' unobserved income by combining highly granular data with information regarding the distribution of income at the zip code level. The results suggest that the distributional impacts of RTP were quite small and, if anything, slightly progressive.
