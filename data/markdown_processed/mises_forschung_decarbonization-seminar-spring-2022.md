# Decarbonization Seminar Spring 2022

The Decarbonization Seminar held in Spring 2022 brought together speakers and participants from academia and industry to discuss the latest advances and challenges associated with transitioning towards a decarbonized energy economy.

**Sponsorship and Organization:**
The seminar was jointly sponsored by the Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES) and the Center for European Economic Research (ZEW).

**Organizing Team:**
The organizing team included:

- [Gunther Glenk](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/area-accounting-taxation/team/gunther-glenk/) (MISES)
- [Marion Ott](https://www.zew.de/team/mot) (ZEW)
- [Nikolas Wölfing](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/area-accounting-taxation/team/dr-nikolas-woelfing/) (MISES)

For the current version of the seminar, please visit: [Decarbonization Seminar Page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar/)
