### Speaker Biography

**Carolyn Deller** is an Assistant Professor of Accounting at the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania. Her research primarily focuses on the design and outcomes of management control systems used by organizations to enhance employees’ motivation, ability, and opportunity to reach their full potential. She holds a DBA (Accounting and Management) from Harvard Business School and a Bachelor of Commerce (Honors) from the University of Melbourne, Australia. She teaches undergraduate managerial accounting.

### Seminar Abstract

Risk oversight has become a critical responsibility for corporate boards. Simultaneously, companies are increasingly recognizing the risks posed by social and environmental issues to investors and stakeholders. This study examines the relationship between board risk oversight and firms’ Environmental and Social (E&S) ratings. Using proprietary data on the board risk oversight practices of firms from 29 countries, the research demonstrates that:

1. Firms with more robust risk oversight exhibit higher E&S ratings.
1. These firms are more likely to integrate E&S issues into their core strategies and executive compensation contracts.
1. Ultimately, these practices lead to better overall E&S outcomes.
