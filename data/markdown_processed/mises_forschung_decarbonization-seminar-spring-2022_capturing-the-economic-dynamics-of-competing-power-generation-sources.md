# Capturing the Economic Dynamics of Competing Power Generation Sources

This seminar presentation, given by Gunther Glenk, examines the changing economics of power generation sources.

## Seminar Details

- **Speaker:** Gunther Glenk
- **Date:** March 7, 2022
- **Time:** 05:00 – 06:15 PM (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Registration required via Zoom)

## Seminar Abstract

Competing power generation sources have experienced considerable shifts in both their revenue potential and their costs in recent years. This presentation introduces the concept of **Levelized Profit Margins (LPM)** to capture the changing unit economics of both intermittent and dispatchable generation technologies.

The framework is applied to the context of the California and Texas wholesale power markets. Key findings include:

- Solar photovoltaic and wind power have substantially improved their competitive position between 2012–2019, primarily due to falling life-cycle costs.
- In California, these gains outweigh an emerging "cannibalization" effect caused by high solar additions making energy less valuable during midday.
- The competitiveness of natural gas power plants has either improved or remained steady. For these plants, declining capacity utilization rates were effectively counterbalanced by a "dispatchability price premium" reflecting the growing market share of intermittent renewables.

## Speaker Biography

**Gunther Glenk** is an Assistant Professor for Accounting at the University of Mannheim. His research focuses on corporate decarbonization, including:

- How companies calculate their carbon footprint.
- Setting appropriate targets for emission reductions.
- Achieving these targets in a cost-efficient manner.

His recent work has concentrated on the competitiveness of clean energy technologies, such as energy storage, zero-emission mobility services, and hydrogen production from intermittent renewable energy sources. His research has been published in leading academic journals in Sustainability Management and has received multiple awards. He holds a BSc, MSc, and Doctorate in Management and Technology from the Technical University of Munich.
