# Corporate Decarbonization: CO2 Footprints, Reduction Targets, and Abatement Costs

This document summarizes details from the seminar titled "Corporate Decarbonization: CO2 footprints, reduction targets, and abatement costs."

- **Speaker:** Jürgen Peterseim (PwC)
- **Date:** May 23, 2022
- **Time:** 05:00 – 06:15 PM (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event
- **Registration:** Attendees must register for the talk via [Zoom](https://uni-mannheim.zoom.us/meeting/register/u50rde2qrT8oHdBEdwZIM5HDUeaKPsQhkYBo).

## Speaker Profile: Jürgen Peterseim

Jürgen Peterseim is a senior professional with over 15 years of experience specializing in:

- Power plant design
- Industrial process analysis and optimization
- Strategy development and product commercialization

His core expertise lies in energy efficiency and developing low-carbon/green energy sources for industry (covering electricity, heating, and cooling). Mr. Peterseim has a proven track record of developing technology solutions for industrial clients, managing the process from market need identification and idea creation/patenting through to product commercialization. Prior to joining PwC in 2019, he worked for an industrial engineering company active in oil & gas, distributed energy generation, industrial energy efficiency, and energy storage. He also serves as an Adjunct Professor, facilitating synergies between research and industry.
