# Identifying and Integrating Climate Risks and Opportunities in a Portfolio Context

This document summarizes details from the seminar: "Identifying and integrating climate related risks and opportunities in a portfolio context – view from BlackRock as an investment manager."

## Event Details

- **Speaker:** Victoria Arnold (BlackRock)
- **Date & Time:** March 14, 2022, 3:00 PM – 6:15 PM (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event (Registration via Zoom required)
- **Topic Focus:** Analyzing and incorporating climate-related risks and opportunities within investment portfolio management, presented from the perspective of BlackRock.

## Speaker Profile: Victoria Arnold

Victoria Arnold is an experienced financial professional with a background in global equities and commodity derivatives.

- **Education:** Master in Management (Diplôme de Grande Ecole) with specialization in financial markets from ESCP Europe.
- **Career Highlights:**
  - **BNP Paribas (2008–2015):** Worked in the Global Equities and Commodity Derivatives division.
  - **DWS (2015–2018):** Responsible for covering institutional clients in Germany and Austria for index solutions.
  - **BlackRock (Since 2018):**
    - Joined BlackRock in July 2018.
    - Currently covers German Corporates & Pensions for ETFs & Index Investing.
    - Appointed local BlackRock ESG-representative for Germany, Austria, and Eastern Europe (since September 2019).
    - Represents BlackRock in the Working Group Sustainable Investing at the BVI (German Asset Management Industry association).
    - Represents BlackRock in the Green and Sustainable Finance Cluster Germany (Industry Think Tank on Sustainable Finance in Hesse and Germany) since September 2021.
