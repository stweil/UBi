# We have all it needs to make it work

**Event Details**

- **Speaker:** Matthias Taft (BayWa r.e. AG)
- **Date & Time:** April 25, 2022, 17:00 – 18:15 (CET)
- **Format:** Live virtual event
- **Registration:** [Register via Zoom](https://uni-mannheim.zoom.us/meeting/register/u5Mrcu6grjkuHd36Ydsr1iWs1eJ0O3mehyND)

## Seminar Abstract

Renewable energy technologies are key to decarbonization and thus achieving the most challenging sustainability goals of our time. The seminar addresses the premise: "We have all it needs to make it work – it’s up to us to make the transition happen."

## Speaker Biography: Matthias Taft

Matthias Taft was born in Munich in 1967. His education includes studies in mechanical engineering at the Technical University of Munich (1988–1993) and industrial engineering at the University of Hagen (1997–2001).

His professional career includes:

- Taking over the management of RENERCO Renewable Energy Concepts AG in 2003.
- Managing BayWa r.e. renewable energy GmbH since 2013.
- Serving as a member of the Board of Management of BayWa r.e. AG for the Renewable and Conventional Energy divisions (2015–2021).
- Serving as CEO of BayWa r.e. AG since 2021.
