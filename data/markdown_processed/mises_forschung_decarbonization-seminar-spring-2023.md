# Decarbonization Seminar

The Decarbonization Seminar hosts speakers and participants from academia and industry to discuss the latest advances and challenges associated with the transition towards a decarbonized energy economy.

**Sponsorship and Organization**
The seminar is jointly sponsored by the [Mannheim Institute for Sustainable Energy Studies (MISES)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar-spring-2023/) and the [Center for European Economic Research (ZEW)](https://www.zew.de/).

The organizing team includes:

- [Gunther Glenk](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/en/area-accounting-taxation/team/gunther-glenk/) (MISES)
- [Marion Ott](https://www.zew.de/team/mot) (ZEW)
- [Nikolas Wölfing](https://www.hwg-lu.de/fachbereiche/fachbereich-management-controlling-healthcare/team/professoren-lehrkraefte/prof-dr-nikolas-woelfing) (MISES)

You can find the current version of the seminar [here](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar-1/).
