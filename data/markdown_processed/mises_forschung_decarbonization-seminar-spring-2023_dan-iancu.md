# Dan Iancu

**Affiliation:** Stanford Graduate School of Business

## Seminar Details

**Topic:** Conditional Incentives for Improving Smallholder Farmer Welfare and Protecting Tropical Forests
**Date:** April 6, 2023
**Time:** 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CEST)
**Format:** Online only (Registration required)

### Seminar Abstract

Smallholder farmers are vital to global agricultural commodity production but often face low incomes and limited productivity. To expand their farmlands and improve their livelihoods, these farmers sometimes resort to tropical deforestation, causing significant environmental damage. In response, major multinational commodity buyers and governmental agencies are increasingly committing to dual goals: halting tropical deforestation while simultaneously enhancing smallholder welfare.

This seminar examines the effectiveness of **conditional incentives**—financial rewards tied to forest protection requirements—in achieving these dual commitments. Using game theoretic models, the research analyzes the decisions made by smallholder communities facing various incentive structures. Key findings suggest that group-level incentives are more effective than individual ones, and the optimal conditions depend heavily on the priorities of the stakeholders and local field conditions. Utilizing data from the Indonesian palm oil supply chain, the study concludes that current price premiums may be insufficient to meet these dual commitments uniformly, suggesting that a modest carbon price could be adequate given the potential CO2 mitigation.

## Speaker Biography

Dan Iancu is an Associate Professor of Operations, Information and Technology at the Stanford Graduate School of Business.

**Education & Experience:**

- **B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science:** Yale University
- **S.M. in Engineering Sciences:** Harvard University
- **Ph.D. in Operations Research:** MIT
- Previously served as a Goldstine Fellow in the Risk Analytics Group at the IBM T.J. Watson Research Center.

**Research Focus:**
Prof. Iancu's research bridges operations, finance, and risk management. He develops tools for dynamic optimization under uncertainty and prescriptive analytics, applying them to operational and contracting problems within complex value chains. A primary area of focus involves designing improved procurement, payment, and financing solutions for global supply chains, addressing the immediate and long-term impacts on both human lives and the environment.

**Publications & Recognition:**
His work has been published in top journals including *Management Science*, *Operations Research*, *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management*, and *Mathematics of Operations Research*. He has received multiple best paper awards (e.g., INFORMS Optimization Society 2009, INFORMS JFIG 2013) and teaching prizes from Harvard and MIT Sloan.
