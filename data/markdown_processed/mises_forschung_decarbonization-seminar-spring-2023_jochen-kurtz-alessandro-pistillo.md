# Product CO2-Sting at Scale at BASF

**Speakers:** Jochen Kurtz and Alessandro Pistillo (BASF)
**Date:** February 27, 2023
**Time:** 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CET)

**Location Details:**

- **In Person:** Room O 129 (Castle, Ostflügel building, Level 1, Room 29).
- **Virtual:** Registration required in advance (Link no longer available).

## Seminar Abstract

The accounting of product-level carbon emission is critically important for companies, not only for disclosure requirements but also to steer and track decarbonization efforts. BASF has established a comprehensive program and a robust tool-set to determine the product carbon footprint of its entire portfolio of 45,000 products. Furthermore, BASF is actively engaging with peers, customers, suppliers, and partners to drive carbon footprint transparency across value chains.

## Speaker Biographies

**Jochen Kurtz**
Vice President for Group Performance Management & Analytics at BASF. As Corporate Controller, he manages both the financial performance and the sustainability reporting & controlling functions within BASF. He brings over 25 years of leadership experience in finance, controlling, and digitalization within BASF across Germany and Belgium. He holds a degree in Business Administration from the University of Mannheim and earned a Ph.D. in Economics from Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, based on a thesis on Circular Economy.

**Alessandro Pistillo**
Director – Digital Strategic Projects in the Corporate Development Department of BASF. His focus lies particularly on customer experience and the intersection between digital technology and sustainability. He is currently leading the establishment of a business ecosystem designed to foster standardization and transparency in greenhouse gas emissions data. Alessandro has over 20 years of experience at BASF Group, covering various leadership roles in product management, marketing, sales, strategy, and engineering across Germany, Switzerland, and China. He is a chemical engineer by education.
