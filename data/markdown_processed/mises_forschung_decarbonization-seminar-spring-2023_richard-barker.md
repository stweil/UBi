# Seminar: Conceptual Foundations of Sustainability Reporting

- **Virtual Attendance:** Requires advance registration (Link was no longer available).

### Seminar Abstract

Sustainability reporting is entering a new era, driven by the launch of European (ESRS) and International (ISSB) standards. This talk will address how these new standards work alongside one another, and how they build upon existing practices utilizing frameworks such as GRI, TCFD, SASB, and Integrated Reporting. The focus will be on the underlying concepts, evaluated in light of evolving institutional practice.

### Speaker Biography

Richard Barker was appointed as a full-time member of the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) in July 2022. He is currently on leave from his role as Professor of Accounting at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford. His professional experience includes serving on EFRAG’s Academic Panel and leading the establishment of the European Accounting Association’s Stakeholder Reporting Committee.
