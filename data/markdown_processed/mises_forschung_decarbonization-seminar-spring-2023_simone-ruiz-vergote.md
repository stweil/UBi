# Managing a financial portfolio’s climate exposure – from the data to the tools

**Speaker:** Simone Ruiz-Vergote
**Affiliation:** MSCI ESG Research
**Date:** March 20, 2023
**Time:** 05:15 – 06:30 PM (CET)

### Seminar Abstract

Historically, corporate disclosures regarding carbon emissions and decarbonization targets were primarily driven by shareholders rather than regulators. While data availability has improved, it remains sparse, particularly concerning Scope 3 (value-chain) emissions. A major challenge for investors is the comparability of this data, which hinders the selection of companies best positioned to outperform in a decarbonizing economy. This landscape is changing as global regulators and standard setters mandate corporate disclosure on climate change exposure and impact. A crucial element of these emerging standards involves forward-looking data, such as climate targets and scenario analysis. MSCI, a leading ESG & Climate data provider, equips the world's largest investors with relevant data to measure their portfolio’s financial carbon footprint and future exposure to climate risks.

### Speaker Biography: Simone Ruiz-Vergote

Simone is the Global Head of ESG & Climate Policy at MSCI ESG Research. She globally steers and coordinates MSCI’s research efforts concerning ESG and climate-related regulatory initiatives.

Her team is responsible for developing research and methodology, enabling MSCI clients to respond to regulations on sustainable finance and climate-related prudential rules. Simone is an active member of the Consultative Working Group of ESMA’s newly created Sustainability Standing Committee.

Simone is based in Frankfurt and previously worked at Allianz, where she spent over a decade building the Group's climate strategy for both investment and insurance sectors. Before that, she spent five years in Brussels, holding roles, among others, with Businesseurope, the largest European business association. She began her career at the European Central Bank.

Academically, Simone recently completed an executive MBA from the Frankfurt School of Finance and Management. She holds degrees in Economics (Lic.Oec.) from HEC Lausanne and European & International Politics (M.Sc.) from Edinburgh University. She is also a certified Financial Risk Manager.
