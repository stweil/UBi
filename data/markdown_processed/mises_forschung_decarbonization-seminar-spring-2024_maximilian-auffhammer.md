**Speaker Biography**
Maximilian Auffhammer is the George Pardee Jr. Professor of International Sustainable Development and Chair of the Berkeley Division of the Academic Senate.

Professor Auffhammer's academic background includes:

- B.S. in environmental science from the University of Massachusetts at Amherst (1996).
- M.S. in environmental and resource economics from the University of Massachusetts at Amherst (1998).
- Ph.D. in economics from UC San Diego (2003).

He joined the faculty at UC Berkeley in 2003. His research expertise lies in environmental and resource economics, energy economics, and applied econometrics. He currently serves as a Research Associate at the National Bureau of Economic Research in the Energy and Environmental Economics group, is a Humboldt Fellow, and has been a lead author for the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).

His research has been published in prestigious journals including *The American Economic Review*, *The Review of Economic Studies*, *The Review of Economics and Statistics*, *The Economic Journal*, *The Proceedings of the National Academies of Sciences*, *The Journal of Environmental Economics and Management*, and *The Energy Journal*.

Professor Auffhammer is a recipient of several awards, including the 2017 and 2021 Cheit Teaching Award in the Haas School of Business, the 2009 Campus Distinguished Teaching Award, the 2007 Cozzarelli Prize (National Academies of Sciences), and the 2007 Sarlo Distinguished Mentoring Award.
