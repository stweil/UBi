### 📝 Seminar Abstract

This presentation reports on the results of a pre-registered field experiment investigating the impact of index provider engagement on corporate climate policy.

In the study, a randomly selected group of 300 out of 1227 international companies received a letter from an index provider. This letter encouraged the company to commit to setting a science-based climate target to maintain inclusion in its climate transition benchmark indices.

**Key Finding:** After one year, the research observed a significant effect: 21.0% of the treated companies committed to targets, compared to 15.7% in the control group. This suggests that engagement from financial institutions can influence corporate policies when a feasible request is combined with a credible threat of exit.

### 👤 Speaker Biography

**Florian Heeb** is an Assistant Professor of Sustainable Finance at the Leibniz Institute for Financial Research SAFE. He also serves as a Research Affiliate at the MIT Sloan School of Management, where he previously worked as a Postdoctoral Associate.

His research focuses on exploring the real-world impact of sustainable investing and how investors integrate impact into their decision-making processes. His work has been published in journals such as the *Review of Financial Studies*, where it was selected as the Editor’s Choice.

Florian holds a PhD from the University of Zurich and an MSc in Environmental Science from ETH Zurich. He brings extensive practical experience in corporate sustainability and climate action from various industry management roles.
