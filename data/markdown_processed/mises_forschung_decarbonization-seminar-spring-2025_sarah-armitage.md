# Sarah Armitage

**Affiliation:** Boston University

## Seminar Details

- **Date:** Monday, March 31, 2025
- **Time:** 3:15 PM – 6:30 PM (CEST)
- **Format:** Online only. Advance registration is required to join virtually. (Registration opens approximately two weeks before the event.)

## Speaker Biography

Sarah Armitage is an Assistant Professor at Boston University's Questrom School of Business. Her academic background includes a Ph.D. from Harvard University, specializing in environmental economics, industrial organization, and public finance.

### Professional Experience

- **Environmental Defense Fund (EDF):** Served as an Economist Fellow during the 2022–2023 academic year.
- **Industrial Economics, Inc.:** Worked as a consultant, supporting state and federal government clients on various environmental projects.
- **MIT:** Served as a research assistant at the Center for Energy and Environmental Policy Research.
- **Prime Coalition:** Served as an Impact Fellow, overseeing Prime's initial impact audit of investments with gigaton-scale emissions reduction potential.

### Education

- **M.Phil. in Economic and Social History:** University of Cambridge (Gates Cambridge Scholar).
- **B.A. in History:** Yale University.
