# Seminar Details: Green Subsidies and Green Industries

**Speaker:** [Florian Egli](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/forschung/decarbonization-seminar/florian-egli/)

**Topic:** Can green subsidies build green industries? Analysing the effects of IPCEIs

**Date & Time:** Monday, March 16, 2026, 15:00 – 16:30 PM (CET)

**Format:** This session will be held as a hybrid event.

- **In-Person Attendance:** Room O 133 (Room 133 on level 1 of the east wing of the castle).
- **Virtual Attendance:** Advance registration is required via [this link](https://uni-mannheim.zoom-x.de/meeting/register/qQxse0gyTG67ydosDW5cNA).

## Speaker Biography

Florian Egli heads the Public Policy for the Green Transition research group at TUM.

**Academic Background:**

- He completed his PhD at the Energy and Technology Policy Group at ETH Zurich, where he worked as a Senior Researcher and remains a Lecturer.
- He is also a Lecturer at the University of St. Gallen.
- Previously, he was an Honorary Research Fellow with Prof. Mariana Mazzucato at the Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose (IIPP) at UCL.

**Other Activities:**

- He is a co-founder and co-president of Expedition Zukunft.
- He is an inaugural member and former speaker of the board of the Swiss Young Academy.
- He is a regular contributor on Monocle M24.
