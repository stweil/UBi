### 📄 Seminar Abstract

This research paper investigates a long-term Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) between a corporation and a new renewable energy generator. The core challenge addressed is how the firm must dynamically satisfy uncertain electricity demand using its existing sources while wholesale electricity prices fluctuate stochastically.

**Key Findings:**

- Upon signing a PPA, the new renewable facility becomes operational, and the firm owns its output for the contract duration.
- The optimal strategy involves the firm dynamically choosing the timing of the PPA initiation and the payment amount to maximize its expected total discounted benefit, following a time-dependent threshold policy.
- **Policy Implications:** Contrary to common assumptions, reducing investment costs for renewable technologies can paradoxically lead to smaller renewable capacity, output, and emissions savings when projects are developed under PPAs. This suggests caution when applying investment tax credits.
- The study also indicates that total renewable energy generation and emissions savings may decrease with higher site productivity, suggesting that restricting development to the most productive sites might be counterproductive under PPA structures.

### 👩‍🏫 Speaker Biography: Dr. Nur Sunar

Dr. Sunar specializes in innovative business models, policies, and technologies, focusing on their impact on inclusion. Her research centers on applying management science to achieve positive outcomes, with a particular focus on:

- Renewable energy technologies (e.g., [rooftop solar panels](https://www.kenan-flagler.unc.edu/research/profit-surge/)).
- Sustainability and smart city technologies (e.g., IoT, smart meters, residential batteries).

**Expertise & Methods:**

- She teaches courses in operations management and has a strong interest in online marketplaces and inclusive health solutions.
- Her research methods are diverse, including machine learning (clustering, deep learning, text mining), data-driven optimization, stochastic analysis, game theory, and econometrics.
- **Publications & Awards:** She has published in top journals such as *Management Science*, *M&SOM*, and *Operations Research*. Her work has received accolades, including the INFORMS Data Mining Best Paper Award and the Amundi-ESSEC ESG Best Paper Award 2024.
- **Education:** She holds a PhD from Stanford Graduate School of Business and a BS in industrial engineering from Bogazici University.
