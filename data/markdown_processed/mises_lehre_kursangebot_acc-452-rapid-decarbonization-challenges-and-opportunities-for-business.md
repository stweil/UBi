# ACC 452 Rapid Decarbonization: Challenges and Opportunities for Business

[Link to Course Page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/lehre/kursangebot/acc-452-rapid-decarbonization-challenges-and-opportunities-for-business/)

This course provides a comprehensive overview of the challenges and opportunities that companies face while transitioning towards a decarbonized energy economy.

## 🎯 Target Audience

The course is designed for bachelor students majoring in Business Administration, Economics, and related disciplines.

## 📚 Course Content and Topics

The curriculum offers both an introduction to the microeconomic theory of corporate decarbonization efforts and a review of recent practical developments. Key topics covered include:

- Carbon Emissions and Carbon Regulations
- Corporate Decarbonization Initiatives
- Competitive Analysis of Emerging Climate Technologies
- Bringing Low-carbon Products and Production Processes to Market
- Climate-tech Entrepreneurship and Project Finance

## 🎓 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, participants will be able to:

- Identify the main challenges and opportunities companies encounter on their path toward a net-zero carbon footprint.
- Understand the theoretical economic concepts central to the energy transition.
- Apply knowledge of recent practical developments and tools.
- Gain experience analyzing real-world case studies.
