# ACC 574: Business Opportunities in the Clean Energy Transition

This course provides an in-depth examination of the business models and emerging opportunities within the clean energy sector, with a specific focus on decarbonized energy systems.

The curriculum analyzes emerging trends by considering the intersection of technological change, core business fundamentals, and the regulatory parameters set by public policy.

## Key Topics Covered

Students will examine several critical areas defining the energy transition, including:

- **Carbon Emissions and the Clean Energy Transition:** Analyzing the role of emissions reduction in the energy shift.
- **Cost Competitiveness Modeling:** Developing models to assess the cost-effectiveness of alternative energy technologies.
- **Renewable Energy Momentum:** Understanding the driving forces and growth trajectory of renewable sources.
- **Integration of Renewable Energy Sources:** Examining the technical and market challenges of integrating variable renewable inputs.
- **Energy Storage:** Reviewing current and future solutions for energy storage solutions.
- **Sustainable Transportation:** Exploring the business and technological aspects of sustainable mobility.
