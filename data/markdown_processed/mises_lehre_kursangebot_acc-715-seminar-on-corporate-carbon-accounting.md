# ACC 715 Seminar on Corporate Carbon Accounting

This seminar provides an in-depth examination of the challenges and opportunities companies face on their journey toward achieving net-zero carbon emissions. The core focus lies on the accounting of corporate carbon emissions and the performance and risk measurement of various abatement strategies.

## Key Topics Covered

The seminar covers several critical areas in corporate sustainability and climate finance:

- Corporate Carbon Accounting and Reporting
- Corporate Decarbonization Pledges and Pathways
- Carbon Pricing and Regulation: Theory and Implementation
- Competitiveness of Clean Energy Technologies
- Climate Risk in Finance and Investment

## Course Methodology and Outcomes

The analysis of these topics requires reviewing the most current academic literature and applying analytical and empirical research methods. Participants are expected to present and discuss key findings, culminating in a formal seminar paper.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Participants will gain a comprehensive understanding of the main challenges and opportunities associated with transitioning to a decarbonized energy economy. The course covers both theoretical economic concepts central to this transition and recent practical developments and tools. Furthermore, participants will improve their ability to write a coherent, state-of-the-art seminar paper.

**Assessment Details:**

- **Form of Assessment:** Written seminar thesis (70%) and in-class presentation (30%).
- **Credits:** 6 ECTS.
