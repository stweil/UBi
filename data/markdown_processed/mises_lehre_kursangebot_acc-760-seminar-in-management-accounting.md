# ACC 760: Seminar in Management Accounting

This seminar provides comprehensive training in the field of management accounting, covering both theoretical concepts and practical applications.

## Course Overview

The primary goal of the seminar is to equip participants with essential managerial skills required in management accounting. Students will develop proficiency in:

- Understanding core management accounting concepts.
- Structuring and writing academic seminar papers.
- Applying fundamental tools of management accounting in real-world scenarios.

## Course Format and Structure

The learning experience is highly interactive and practical, consisting mainly of:

- Independent group work.
- Regular meetings with the course supervisor.
