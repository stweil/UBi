# ACC 922 Decarbonization Seminar

This seminar provides an in-depth look at the challenges and latest advances associated with transitioning towards a decarbonized energy economy.

## Course Details

- **Credits:** 3 ECTS
- **Target Audience:** Doctoral students at GESS.
- **Lecturers:** Amadeus Bach, Gunther Glenk, Stefan Reichelstein

## Course Focus and Topics

The seminar features speakers from both academia and industry to discuss the complexities of sustainability in modern economies. Key topics covered include:

- The economics and management of sustainability activities.
- Clean energy technologies across all economic sectors.
- Specific focus areas:
  - The energy sector.
  - Transportation services.
  - Carbon-free manufacturing processes.

## Participation Requirements

Participants are required to actively engage with the course material by:

1. Attending all seminar talks.
1. Completing corresponding preparation sessions, where students must present a paper and take the role of a discussant.
1. Reviewing supplementary readings, which may include recent theoretical or empirical papers.
