# ACC 923 Corporate Sustainability and Decarbonization

This seminar is designed for doctoral students enrolled in GESS. It provides an in-depth examination of the latest advances and challenges companies face while transitioning towards sustainable business practices and achieving net-zero carbon emissions.

## Course Focus and Topics

The curriculum covers the economics and management aspects of sustainability activities, including comprehensive emission abatement strategies across all economic sectors.

## Learning Objectives

The primary goals of the course are twofold:

1. To introduce students to current research paradigms within the covered sustainability topics.
1. To train students in the skills required to present and critically evaluate academic papers during seminars and conferences, thereby identifying promising avenues for future research.

## Course Structure and Assessment

- **Format:** Participants are required to attend both seminar talks and internal working sessions.
- **Internal Sessions:** Students are expected to present a paper and/or take the role of a discussant. Readings may also include recent theoretical or empirical papers.
- **Assessment:** The final grade is based on:
  - Participation (20%)
  - Paper presentations and discussions (80%)
- **Credits:** 3 ECTS
