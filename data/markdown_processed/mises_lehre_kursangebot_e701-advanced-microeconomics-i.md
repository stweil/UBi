# E701 Advanced Microeconomics I

[Link to Course Page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/mises/lehre/kursangebot/e701-advanced-microeconomics-i/)

This course is designed for doctoral students across all fields of business administration. It provides a graduate-level introduction to microeconomic theory, with a specific focus on aspects highly relevant to business research.

## Course Content and Topics

The curriculum covers several core areas of advanced economic theory, including:

- Theory of consumer choice (under certainty and uncertainty)
- Theory of the firm, production cost, and supply
- Market equilibrium and welfare analysis
- Strategic behavior (under complete and incomplete information)
- Incentives and asymmetric information

## Learning Objectives

The primary goals of the course are to:

1. Enable doctoral students in business to read and discuss advanced economic theory.
1. Equip students with the necessary tools to formulate original research based on theoretically grounded hypotheses.

**Learning Outcomes:** Students will gain the ability to understand and critically evaluate fundamental concepts in microeconomic theory, game theory, and mechanism design, while mastering the relevant tools and underlying assumptions for ongoing economic research.

## Course Structure and Credits

- **Assessment:** The final grade is determined by a combination of Midterm and final exams (80–100%) and Exercises (up to 20%).
- **Credits:** 8 ECTS
