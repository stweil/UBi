# MAN 454: Lecture Series “Science for Future – How Scientific Research can Support Sustainable Development”

This interdisciplinary lecture series aims to raise awareness regarding the potential of scientific research to contribute to sustainable development, as defined by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## Course Overview

The lecture series is designed to showcase cutting-edge research and fundamental knowledge on sustainability-related topics. Researchers from various disciplines at the University of Mannheim will present their work.

**Disciplines represented include (but are not limited to):**

- Economics
- Psychology
- History
- Political Science
- Philosophy
- Information Systems
- Business Management

**Target Audience:**
The lecture series is open to students from all Bachelor programs.

## Format and Structure

The course is structured as a comprehensive series of talks:

- **Total Sessions:** 6 sessions.
- **Talks:** 12 talks in total (meaning two talks are presented on each day).
- **Duration per Talk:** Approximately 60 minutes for the presentation, followed by a 30-minute discussion period.
