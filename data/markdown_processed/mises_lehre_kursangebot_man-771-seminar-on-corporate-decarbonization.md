# MAN 771: Seminar on Corporate Decarbonization

This seminar provides an in-depth examination of the challenges and opportunities facing companies on their journey toward achieving net-zero carbon emissions. The core focus is on the economic analysis required for reducing corporate carbon emissions and formulating effective abatement strategies.

## Key Topics Covered

The seminar covers a broad spectrum of critical sustainability and energy transition topics, including:

- Climate-Tech Innovation
- Corporate Decarbonization Strategies
- Corporate Carbon Accounting and Reporting
- Competitiveness of Clean Energy Technologies
- Emergence of Sustainable Transportation Services
- Pathways for Industrial Decarbonization
- Climate Risk in Finance and Investment

## Academic Approach and Learning Outcomes

The analysis of these topics requires participants to review the most current academic literature and employ rigorous analytical and empirical research methods.

**Learning Outcomes:**
Participants will gain:

1. A deeper understanding of the main challenges and opportunities associated with transitioning towards a decarbonized energy economy.
1. Knowledge of both theoretical economic concepts central to this transition and recent practical developments and tools.
1. Improved skills in writing a coherent, state-of-the-art seminar paper.

**Assessment Details:**

- **Form of Assessment:** Written seminar thesis (50%) and in-class presentation (50%).
- **Credits:** 6 ECTS.
