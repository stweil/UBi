# The Financial Economics of Climate and Sustainability

This course is designed to address the complex intersection of climate change, finance, and economic systems, fostering rigorous, relevant, and collaborative research.

## Course Objectives

The primary goals of the course are twofold:

1. To introduce graduate students to the core questions and advanced methodologies within the rapidly evolving fields of climate and sustainable finance.
1. To connect global researchers interested in this topic, thereby stimulating deeper, collaborative academic work.

## Academic Scope and Approach

Addressing climate change necessitates systemic changes across natural, social, and economic structures, requiring significant global collaboration. Reflecting this, the course is delivered by a diverse team of professors drawn from various schools and universities worldwide.

Each instructor contributes one or more lectures, and the student body represents a wide array of academic backgrounds.
