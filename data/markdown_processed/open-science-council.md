# Open Science Council

The Open Science Council functions as an advisory body that supports the Open Science Office. Its primary role is to provide guidance to researchers on adopting Open Science practices within the University of Mannheim.

## Membership Composition

The Council is composed of:

- The Vice President for Research and Academic Career Paths for the University of Mannheim.
- Three external Open Science Experts.

### Current Members

The council includes representatives from various academic and expert backgrounds, such as:

- Prorektorin für Forschung und wissenschaftliche Karrierewege
- Apl. Professor of Psychological Methodology and Diagnostics (Managing Director of the LMU Open Science Center)
- Professor for Business and Psychology, Wirtschaftsuniversität Wien (Department of Strategy and Innovation, Institute for Cognition and Behavior)

For the most current list of members, please refer to the official [Open Science Council page](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/open-science/open-science-council/).
