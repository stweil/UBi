# 8th Trifels Summer School on Open Science (2022)

The 8th Trifels Summer School, organized by the [University of Mannheim](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/), took place from September 26th to 30th, 2022, at the [Kurhaus Trifels](https://www-jugendstilhotel--trifels-de.translate.goog/?_x_tr_sl=auto&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en-US&_x_tr_pto=wapp) in the Palatinate Forest. The event focused on the topic of Open Science and was designed for early career researchers from all academic disciplines.

The series is hosted by the association [“Begegnungszentrum Kurhaus Trifels”](https://begegnungszentrum--kurhaus--trifels-de.translate.goog/beispiel-seite/?_x_tr_sch=http&_x_tr_sl=auto&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en-US&_x_tr_pto=wapp), which includes several universities: Technical University of Darmstadt, Heidelberg University, Technical University of Kaiserslautern, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, University of Koblenz-Landau, and the University of Mannheim.

**Participation Details:**

- **Target Audience:** PhD students and postdoctoral researchers interested in open science, reproducible research, and science communication.
- **Cost:** Free of charge, including accommodation and meals at the Kurhaus Trifels. Participants only covered their own travel expenses.
- **Outcome:** Participants received a certificate of attendance.

## Program Schedule Overview

The Summer School featured workshops, sessions, and social activities, culminating in a public panel discussion.

### Daily Schedule Highlights

**Monday, September 26th**

- **Afternoon:** Welcome session, followed by introductions on "How is Open Science relevant to my research?" (Dr. David Morgan) and "Trust in Science" (Jana Berkessel).
- **Evening:** Check-in and dinner.

**Tuesday, September 27th**

- **Morning:** Workshops on Reproducible Research (Juli Nagel).
- **Afternoon:** Workshop on "Open Science Methods in Teaching" (Dr. Johanna Gereke & Dr. Anne-Sophie Waag).
- **Late Afternoon:** Guided Tour of Trifels Castle (Social Activity).

**Wednesday, September 28th**

- **Morning:** Workshops on Public History/Citizen Science (Cancelled).
- **Afternoon:** Workshop on "Research data meets copyright" (Peter Brettschneider).
- **Late Afternoon:** Trekking Tour with Lamas (Social Activity).

**Thursday, September 29th**

- **Morning:** Workshops on Pre-Print-Publishing (Cancelled).
- **Afternoon:** Sessions on "Revealing the Open Access potential of my dissertation" (Dr. Philipp Zumstein) and "Open Research Data" (Dr. Rima-Maria Rahal).
- **Evening:** Panel Discussion: "Mit Open Science globalen Krisen begegnen – Offenheit und Transparenz in der Wissenschaft."

**Friday, September 30th**

- **Morning:** Recap and Farewell.

## Key Sessions and Materials

### Panel Discussion: Mit Open Science globalen Krisen begegnen

On Thursday, September 29th, 2022, a public panel discussion took place at the Kurhaus Trifels. The event was open to the public and featured the following participants:

- **Moderator:** [Prof. Dr. Konrad Förstner](https://www.zbmed.de/kontakt/konrad-foerstner/) (ZB Med/TH Köln)
- **Panelists:**
  - [Dr. Rima-Maria Rahal](https://www.coll.mpg.de/rima-maria-rahal) (Max Planck Institute for Research on Collective Goods, Bonn)
  - [Philipp Schmid-zum Berge](https://centire.org/index.php/about-centire/who-is-centire/industry-advisory-board/339-philipp-schmidt-zum-berge) (External R&D Cooperations Manager, Continental)
  - Prof. Dr. Konrad Wolf (Wissenschafts- und Kulturminister a.D., Rheinland-Pfalz)

**Discussion Focus:** The panel debated the implications of Open Science, including whether research results should be considered part of the common knowledge pool, the balance between open data/software and researcher control, and the necessity of science communication beyond academic circles.
