# Collaborative Research Center SFB 884: Political Economy of Reforms

**Funding Period:** German Research Foundation (2010–2021)

## Research Focus and Goals

The SFB 884 addressed the significant economic and social challenges facing modern society, recognizing the widespread consensus that reforms are necessary. The core objective of the interdisciplinary SFB 884 was to provide scientific insights into the success and failure of reform initiatives.

The research specifically analyzed three key dimensions influencing reform outcomes:

- **Competing Interests** (Group A)
- **Contexts** (Group B)
- **The Political Process of Reform-Making** (Group C)

## Methodology and Scope

As a core infrastructure, the center established a data center to collect new data across these three dimensions.

The SFB 884 successfully brought together approximately 150 researchers from diverse fields, including:

- Economics
- Political Science
- Sociology
- Statistics
- Computer Science

The joint academic interest was to advance the theoretical and empirical foundations of quantitative research on societal decision-making in general, with a particular focus on understanding the mechanics of reform-making.
