# SMiP Summer School 2025 Overview

The SMiP Summer School 2025 took place from June 30 to July 4, 2025, at the University of Mannheim, Germany. The event was organized by the DFG-funded Research Training Group on [Statistical Modeling in Psychology](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/) (SMiP) in collaboration with the [Interuniversity Graduate School of Psychometrics and Sociometrics](https://www.iops.nl/) (IOPS).

## Program Content

The summer school provided an in-depth introduction to advanced statistical models used in psychology. Key components of the program included:

- **Workshops:** Parallel workshops covering various topics in quantitative methods and statistical modeling.
- **Keynote Talks:** Plenary keynote presentations from senior researchers.
- **Poster Session:** A dedicated session for participants to present research.
- **Special Topics:** An event addressing the compatibility of work and private life.
- **Social Events:** Various social gatherings for participants.

## Participation Details

**Target Audience:**
The summer school was designed for young researchers in psychology with a specific interest in statistical modeling and quantitative methods. This included doctoral researchers from the SMiP and IOPS programs, as well as early-stage researchers from other institutions.

**Prerequisites:**
Participants were expected to have advanced knowledge of quantitative methods and multivariate statistics, solid data analysis skills, and general experience using R.

**Language:**
The official language of the summer school was English; German language mastery was not a prerequisite.
