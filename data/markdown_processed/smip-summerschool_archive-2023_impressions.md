# Impressions from the SMiP Summer School 2023

*(Pictures by Anke Söllner)*

This page summarizes the events and presentations from the SMiP Summer School held in 2023.

## Programmübersicht

### Welcome Event

- Welcome activities for the participants.

### Workshops

The program featured several specialized workshops:

- **Chris Donkin & Nathan Evans:** An introduction to cognitive modelling.
- **Mirka Henninger, Esther Ulitzsch & Thorsten Meiser:** Modeling heterogeneity of response processes in item response theory.
- **Lesa Hoffman & Jonathan Templin:** Multilevel measurement models.
- **Manuel Voelke & Charles Driver:** Dynamic longitudinal modeling.

### Keynote Talks

- Featured keynote presentations from leading researchers.

### Poster Session

- A dedicated session for presenting research posters.

### Get-together

- A networking event titled “Women, increase your network!”

### Farewell

- Concluding farewell activities.
