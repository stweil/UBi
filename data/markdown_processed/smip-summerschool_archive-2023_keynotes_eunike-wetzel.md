# Keynote: Disentangling the effects of age and time in research on personality development

**Speaker:** Eunike Wetzel
**Date:** 28 June
**Time:** 14:00

This presentation addresses a core challenge in lifespan research: how to accurately separate genuine developmental changes within an individual over time (developmental effects) from general differences associated with chronological age (e.g., older individuals having higher average trait levels).

## Methodological Approaches

The talk outlines two advanced statistical approaches designed to tackle this confounding issue:

1. **Multilevel Models:** Age can be incorporated as a Level-2 predictor within this framework, allowing researchers to statistically disentangle the effects of age from the effects of time.
1. **Local Structural Equation Modeling (LSEM):** This method achieves a similar goal by estimating key parameters—such as those derived from a latent change model—conditionally on the individual's age.

## Empirical Illustration

Both methodologies will be demonstrated using empirical data derived from large samples focusing on the development of the personality trait, narcissism.
