# Keynote: AI Algorithms and How They Interact with Society

**Speaker:** Heiko Paulheim
**Date:** 26 June (Summer School 2023)
**Source:** [AI algorithms and how they interact with Society](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/archive-2023/keynotes/heiko-paulheim/)

This presentation provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI) algorithms, their societal impact, and the differences between AI and traditional computational algorithms.

### Core Topics Covered

- **AI Overview:** A general introduction to AI algorithms, addressing the current public discourse surrounding AI (e.g., ChatGPT) and the mixed public understanding of the technology.
- **Algorithm Comparison:** Detailed explanation of how AI algorithms fundamentally differ from traditional, non-AI algorithms.
- **Research Insights:** Discussion of recent research projects investigating the intersection of AI and society:
  - **ReNewRS Project:** Analysis of polarization effects caused by news recommenders and potential mitigation strategies.
  - **KarekoKI Project:** Investigation into whether algorithmic pricing engines can achieve cooperation and coordination, and whether such practices violate antitrust legislation.
- **Conclusion:** A discussion on why the development of AI algorithms requires a distinct focus and skill set compared to traditional algorithm development.
