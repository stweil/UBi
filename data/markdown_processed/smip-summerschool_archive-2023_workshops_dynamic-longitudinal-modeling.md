# Dynamic Longitudinal Modeling Workshop

This document summarizes the content of the Dynamic Longitudinal Modeling workshop, which took place during the Summer School of 2023.

**Instructors:**

- Manuel Voelkle (Humboldt University Berlin)
- Charles Driver (University of Zurich)

## Workshop Focus and Content

The primary goal of this workshop is to introduce participants to dynamic longitudinal modeling, with a specific emphasis on modeling in continuous time.

**Key Topics Covered:**

1. **Model Comparison:** Distinguishing between static and dynamic models for analyzing change, including a discussion of their respective advantages and disadvantages.
1. **Continuous Time Basics:** Introduction to continuous time modeling, utilizing the R package `ctsem`.
1. **Special Topic Blocks:** The workshop is structured around three specialized blocks:
   - **Block 1:** Focus on heterogeneity, hierarchical models, and input effects.
   - **Block 2:** Focus on time series analysis (N = 1 data), oscillations, and accelerated longitudinal designs.
   - **Block 3:** Advanced topics covering causal inference and machine learning approaches in continuous time modeling, including a discussion of current limitations.

**Target Audience:**
While the emphasis is on applied psychological research, the workshop is designed for an audience primarily interested in quantitative methods and possessing prior experience in multivariate data analysis. Some familiarity with structural equation models (SEM) and longitudinal data analysis is beneficial but not mandatory. The sessions emphasize practical implementation using various datasets and R packages ([www.r-project.org](http://www.r-project.org)).

## Prerequisites and Technical Requirements

**Required Skills:**

- A primary interest in quantitative methods.
- Familiarity with multivariate data analysis.

**Technical Setup:**
Participants are required to bring their own notebooks with a recent version of R and RStudio/Posit installed. While advanced knowledge of R is not required, those unfamiliar with the program should familiarize themselves with basic functionality beforehand.
