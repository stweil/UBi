# Modeling Heterogeneity of Response Processes in Item Response Theory

**Speakers:** Mirka Henninger (University of Zurich), Esther Ulitzsch (IPN Kiel), Thorsten Meiser (University of Mannheim)

This workshop provides a comprehensive overview of basic and advanced models within Item Response Theory (IRT) for cognitive and personality assessment.

## Workshop Content Overview

**Foundational Topics:**
The course begins with an introduction to fundamental IRT models for dichotomous and ordinal items. Participants will cover essential models such as the Rasch model, 2PL and 3PL models, the generalized partial credit model, and the graded response model, including detailed instruction on parameter estimation and model testing. This initial segment ensures all attendees possess the necessary knowledge of core concepts and techniques required for advanced topics.

**Advanced Modeling:**
Building upon the general IRT introduction, the workshop delves into various extensions designed to analyze heterogeneity in latent response processes across individuals and/or items. Advanced topics include:

- Multidimensional and mixture IRT models for analyzing individual response styles in personality assessment.
- Incorporating process data information.
- Accommodating disengaged responding in cognitive measures.
- Analyzing latent processes underlying fast or missing responses.

Furthermore, the workshop covers advanced statistical procedures, including Bayesian estimation and extensions of IRT modeling derived from machine learning. All models are presented with their theoretical and statistical foundations and illustrated using real data. Model specification and estimation will be demonstrated using various R packages through supervised, hands-on exercises.

## Prerequisites and Preparation

**Required Skills:** Participants are expected to have basic experience using the R programming language and should bring their own notebook for practical exercises. (Required R packages will be installed during the workshop.)
