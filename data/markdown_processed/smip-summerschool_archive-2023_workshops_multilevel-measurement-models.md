# Multilevel Measurement Models Workshop (Summer School 2023)

**Instructors:** Lesa Hoffman (University of Iowa) & Jonathan Templin (University of Iowa)

This workshop focuses on the application of latent variable measurement models within multilevel sampling designs. The course structure integrates traditional lectures, guided model-building practice, and dedicated time for individual data analysis.

### Workshop Schedule Overview

The workshop is structured across four days, covering increasingly complex modeling topics:

- **Day 1:** Focuses on latent variable measurement models for normal, binary, and ordinal responses (all presented in slope–intercept form). Introduction to Stan for MCMC estimation.
- **Day 2:** Presents concepts of multilevel models using observed outcomes, transitioning into three-level models for item responses nested within persons nested within clusters.
- **Day 3:** Extends multilevel models to incorporate latent variable measurement models that include level-specific discrimination parameters.
- **Day 4:** Makes connections to advanced models where item parameters are treated as random effects rather than fixed effects (e.g., for predicting sources of item difficulty and discrimination, as in explanatory item response models).

*Note: All instructional sessions are recorded for future participant use.*

### Prerequisites and Skills

Participants should possess the following knowledge and skills to benefit from the workshop:

- Familiarity with R software for data analysis.
- Some prior experience with Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) estimation.
- Prior knowledge of latent variable measurement models (e.g., confirmatory factor analysis for continuous responses; item response theory for binary and ordinal responses).
- Prior knowledge of multilevel models (e.g., hierarchical linear models, mixed-effects models).

**Software Note:** The course utilizes Stan software run through R (using CMDStanR). No prior experience with Stan is assumed, but participants wishing to use their own devices are advised to install Stan beforehand. No readings are required in advance.
