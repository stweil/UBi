## Program Components

### Parallel Workshops

Each participant is assigned to one thematic workshop throughout the summer school. These workshops involve 15–20 participants and cover selected subjects of statistical modeling and quantitative methods. The content ranges from basic introductions to advanced issues and new developments in the field, supplemented by practical exercises. For detailed information, please see the [Workshops](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/workshops/).

### Keynote Talks

Keynote talks provide an overview of current cutting-edge topics of common interest in statistical modeling and quantitative methods. These sessions are presented by invited experts in their respective fields and are held as plenary sessions for all participants and instructors. Details can be found at [Keynotes](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/keynotes/).

### Poster Session

Participants are encouraged to present a poster of their ongoing project during the dedicated poster session. This session offers young researchers a valuable opportunity to discuss their work with peers and senior researchers, including workshop instructors. Furthermore, an active poster presentation may assist participants in securing reimbursement for travel and accommodation costs from their home institutions. The session will also include refreshments and snacks for a pleasant social experience.

### Get-together: “Compatibility of work and private life”

This dedicated get-together provides an opportunity for summer school participants interested in the topic to meet successful researchers. They will share insights into their individual approaches to balancing professional and private life. [More information](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/program/compatibility-of-work-and-private-life/).
