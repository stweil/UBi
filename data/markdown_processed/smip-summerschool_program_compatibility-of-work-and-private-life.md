# Get-together: Compatibility of Work and Private Life

This session is dedicated to the important topic of **"Compatibility of Work and Private Life."**

## Program Details

The event features a panel discussion with experts and role models who specialize in combining family life and academic careers.

**Speakers/Experts:**

- Sophie Scharf (University of Mannheim)
- Marie-Ann Sengewald (University of Bamberg)
- Mischa von Krause (Heidelberg University)
- Andreas Voss (Heidelberg University)

**Format:**

1. **Podium Discussion:** Experts will share their individual views on the topic, followed by a Q&A session with the audience.
1. **Informal Exchange:** The session concludes with drinks and a BBQ in the Rektoratshof, providing an opportunity for further discussion and exchange of views.
