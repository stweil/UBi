# Parallel Workshops for the Summer School

Participants attend one specialized workshop throughout the summer school. The workshop topic is selected at the time of registration.

**Workshop Details:**
Each workshop provides an introduction to the dedicated method, practical applications, and hands-on exercises, alongside an in-depth overview of advanced topics.

**Prerequisite:** Basic familiarity with R is required for all workshops.
**Capacity:** Workshops are limited to a maximum of 15–20 participants.

## Available Workshops

- **Machine Learning Methods for Modeling in Psychology**

  - Instructors: David Goretzko (Utrecht University) & Philipp Sterner (Ruhr University Bochum, German Center for Mental Health DZPG)
  - [Link to Workshop](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/workshops/machine-learning-methods-for-modeling-in-psychology/)

- **Multilevel Measurement Models**

  - Instructors: Lesa Hoffman & Jonathan Templin (University of Iowa)
  - [Link to Workshop](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/workshops/multilevel-measurement-models/)

- **An Introduction to Modeling Experimental Data of Conflict Tasks with Dynamic (Time-Dependent) Diffusion Models**

  - Instructors: Markus Janczyk & Valentin Koob (University of Bremen)
  - [Link to Workshop](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/workshops/an-introduction-to-modeling-experimental-data/)

- **Dynamic Longitudinal Modeling**

  - Instructors: Manuel Voelkle (Humboldt University Berlin) & Charles Driver (University of Zurich)
  - [Link to Workshop](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/workshops/dynamic-longitudinal-modeling/)
