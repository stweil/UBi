# Dynamic Longitudinal Modeling Workshop

This workshop introduces advanced modeling techniques, focusing specifically on dynamic approaches to analyzing change and variability over time.

## Workshop Overview

The primary goal is to equip participants with knowledge of advanced statistical modeling, moving beyond static analyses to understand temporal change. The curriculum covers the fundamentals of dynamic modeling, contrasting it with static models, and exploring practical tools and software implementation.

**Key Topics Covered:**

- Addressing heterogeneity in data.
- Applying hierarchical models.
- Analyzing individual-level data trajectories.
- Exploring innovative study designs.
- Cutting-edge methods for causal inference.
- Integration of machine learning into dynamic modeling, including practical applications and current limitations.

## Target Audience and Prerequisites

The workshop is designed for participants interested in quantitative methods.

**Recommended Background:**

- Prior experience with multivariate analysis is beneficial but not required.
- Familiarity with structural equation modeling (SEM) and longitudinal data analysis is helpful.

**Technical Requirements:**

- **Laptops:** Participants must bring their own laptops.
- **Software:** The latest versions of R and RStudio must be installed.
- *Note:* While advanced knowledge of R is not necessary, participants new to the language are encouraged to familiarize themselves with basic R functionality.
