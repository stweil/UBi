# Multilevel Measurement Models Workshop

**Instructors:** Lesa Hoffman (University of Iowa) & Jonathan Templin (University of Iowa)

This workshop focuses on utilizing latent variable measurement models for research designs involving multiple levels of sampling. Estimation is performed using Bayesian Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods. While examples will feature outcomes from persons nested within clusters, the models are applicable to other multilevel designs (e.g., occasions nested in persons).

Instructional sessions will include traditional lectures and curated examples demonstrating model estimation using R software. All instructional sessions (instructor screen plus audio) will be recorded for future participant use.

## Workshop Content Overview

The course is designed to be accessible, assuming participants do not have extensive prior familiarity with latent variable measurement models, multilevel models, or Bayesian MCMC estimation.

- **Day 1:** Introduction to single-level latent variable measurement models (all in slope–intercept form). This covers confirmatory factor analysis for continuous responses and item response theory models for binary and ordinal responses. MCMC estimation is also introduced.
- **Day 2:** Introduction to multilevel models (i.e., hierarchical linear models, mixed-effects models) for predicting continuous observed outcomes for persons nested in clusters. It also covers three-level models for predicting categorical item responses from persons nested in clusters.
- **Days 3 & 4:** Extension of multilevel models to incorporate latent variable measurement models with level-specific discrimination parameters.

## Prerequisites and Preparation

- **Software:** The course uses Stan software run through R (using `CMDStanR`). No prior experience with Stan is assumed.
- **Optional Live Practice:** Participants wishing to follow along live with Stan examples are requested to install the `cmdstanr` interface by following the [“Getting Started with CmdStanR” vignette](https://mc-stan.org/cmdstanr/articles/cmdstanr.html).
- **Note:** Running Stan live during the workshop is optional, as complete examples of syntax and output will be provided in the course materials.
- **Readings:** No readings are required ahead of time.
