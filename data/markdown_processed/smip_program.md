# PhD Program Overview

PhD Candidates develop their dissertation projects collaboratively with their advisor during the initial months of doctoral studies. These projects generally fall into three core thematic areas. Within each thematic area, two complementary approaches are possible: projects focusing on **model development and statistical refinements** (model development perspective) or projects aiming at **innovative applications of existing models** to new research questions (model application perspective). Candidates benefit from diverse opportunities to discuss their work with fellow PhD Candidates, researchers from the SMiP group, and external experts.

## Thematic Areas for Dissertation Projects

### Modeling Cognition and Social Cognition

This area focuses on cognition, which is defined as the summary term for all processes related to our knowledge about the world.
[Learn more about this topic](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/possible-thesis-topics/modeling-cognition-and-social-cognition/)

### Modeling Motivation and Affect

This research stream addresses affective states—such as feeling happy, relaxed, or fatigued—which fluctuate significantly due to influences like diurnal rhythms, daily events, and self-regulatory behavior.
[Learn more about this topic](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/possible-thesis-topics/modeling-motivation-and-affect/)

### Modeling Individual Differences

At the core of many psychological research domains are the interindividual differences observed in personality attributes and cognitive performance.
[Learn more about this topic](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/possible-thesis-topics/modeling-individual-differences/)

## Qualification Program

To support and accelerate the PhD phase, the SMiP group offers a unique [qualification program](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/qualification-program/).

This comprehensive program spans 6 [semesters](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/semesters/) and includes varied teaching activities. These activities cover core courses in statistical modeling, practical skill trainings (e.g., academic writing and publishing), and workshops on diverse topics presented by SMiP group members or renowned international experts from partner institutions.

In addition to coursework, the program supports active participation in teaching and at conferences. Furthermore, PhD Candidates will have the opportunity to visit external labs in research groups abroad.
