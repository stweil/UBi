# SMiP Qualification Program Overview

The official language for the RTG, and consequently the teaching and communication language, is English. The training program spans six semesters and integrates four core types of activities: core courses, training in key competencies, workshops, and colloquia. To ensure all PhD Candidates can participate fully, the typical format involves one or two-day meetings held at one of the five participating universities.

## Core Courses

These mandatory courses take place only in the first year. They consist of two parts:

- **Foundations of Statistical Modeling 1:** Held in the first semester (6 meeting days).
- **Foundations of Statistical Modeling 2:** Held in the second semester (6 meeting days).

The classes combine lecture series with practical application exercises. Thematically, they cover:

1. Mathematical foundations of advanced statistical methods.
1. Various model frameworks crucial for SMiP research.
1. Statistical techniques forming the basis of SMiP research activities.

## Trainings in Key Competencies

This training is mandatory for all PhD Candidates. The focus areas are designed to prepare candidates for a professional career at the intersection of substantive science and statistical modeling. Topics include:

- Academic writing and publishing.
- Conference presentations.
- Good scientific practice and research ethics.
- Methods of research documentation.

## Workshops

Workshops constitute the most extensive part of the qualification program. They cover diverse topics in two main areas:

- **Substantive Science:** Topics such as recognition memory, judgment and decision making, affective processes, emotion expression, person x situation interaction, social cognition, cognitive aging, and occupational health.
- **Quantitative Methods:** Training in software (e.g., Mplus, advanced R packages, Jags) and advanced techniques (e.g., Bayesian parameter estimation and inference, sequential tests, statistical power analysis, optimal design, model selection, simulation methods, and advanced item response models).

**Prerequisites and Focus:**
While solid statistical knowledge (including univariate and multivariate analysis using standard software) is recommended, introductory software workshops are offered to refresh skills for advanced RTG workshops (R and Mplus). Crucially, no workshop is purely statistical; the overarching goal is to link substantive psychological research with statistical modeling. Therefore, every workshop includes the analysis of psychological data and illustrations of relevant research questions. At least one workshop must focus on advanced test theory and psychometric modeling.

## Colloquia

Two types of colloquia are held:

1. **Local Colloquium:** Organized by each participating university, this is a weekly meeting where students meet with their local supervisor, fellow SMiP members, and associated research groups. A local group member presents ongoing research, and associated challenges are discussed.
1. **Joint Colloquium (Retreat):** Held once per semester, this retreat-like event brings together all SMiP members and PhD Candidates. It typically spans two days and combines expert lectures with an overview of the status of each PhD project, along with future research perspectives.
