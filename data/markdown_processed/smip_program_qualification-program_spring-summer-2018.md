- **Individual Consultations 1:** May 8, 2018 OR May 14, 2018 | **Location:** Mannheim (B6, 30–32, Room 318)
- **Individual Consultations 2:** June 6, 2018 OR June 18, 2018 | **Location:** Mannheim (B6, 30–32, Room 318)
- **Cancelled:** Optional Intermediate Workshop (July 25, 2018)
- **Individual Consultations 3:** July 31, 2018 OR August 1, 2018 | **Location:** Mannheim (B6, 30–32, Room 318)
