# Special Events and Programs of the SMiP Group

The SMiP group organizes various special events and programs in addition to its regular qualification curriculum. These initiatives cover career development, sustainability, and specialized consultation.

## Ongoing and Recurring Programs

### Small Group Meetings

Since 2023, the SMiP group organizes Small Group Meetings approximately once per semester. These sessions focus on specific, deep-dive topics, bringing together SMiP members working in the respective field and featuring one external expert as a guest.
[Learn more about Small Group Meetings](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/special-events/small-group-meetings/)

### Career Options after the Doctorate

This successful event series invites a SMiP alumna or alumnus to report on their individual professional experiences. Participants have the opportunity to ask questions directly to the speaker.
[More information on this coaching measure](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/qualification-program/fall-winter-2025/#c441433)

### Digital Consultation Hours with Lesa Hoffman

The SMiP group's Mercator Fellow, [Lesa Hoffman](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/team/researchers/prof-lesa-hoffman-phd/), offers dedicated digital office hours for SMiP PhD Candidates. During the first half of 2025, Lesa is available every second Wednesday from 3:15 – 4:45 p.m. (The invitation link is sent via e-mail).

### SMiP Summer School

The second SMiP Summer School is scheduled to take place at the University of Mannheim from June 30 to July 4, 2025.
[Details for the SMiP Summer School 2025](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip-summerschool/)

## Thematic Projects and Workshops

### Sustainability in Science

The project "Sustainable Futures" is a key focus area. In May 2025, [Asheley Landrum](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/program/special-events/sustainability-in-science/) will visit the University of Mannheim, where she will offer two workshops and one research talk as part of this project.

### General Special Initiatives

The group has historically organized various special events, including:

- Digital PI Consultation Hours (on demand).
- Informal SMiP gatherings after work.
- The "February of Failures" initiative (Spring 2021).
- A talk series focusing on career options after the doctorate (initiated Fall 2021).
- The voluntary PhD colloquium, organized by SMiP PhD candidates.

## Key Personnel and Resources

- **Lesa Hoffman:** Mercator Fellow providing digital consultation hours for PhD Candidates.
- **Asheley Landrum:** Involved in the "Sustainable Futures" project, scheduled to give workshops and a talk in May 2025.
