# SMiP IOPS Conference 2024 Summary

The SMiP IOPS Conference took place in Mannheim on **July 20th and 21st, 2024**. The event welcomed numerous SMiP and IOPS members.

The program featured four main components:

1. Talks by SMiP and IOPS PhD Candidates.
1. Posters presented by SMiP and IOPS PhD Candidates.
1. A keynote talk by Prof. Dr. Daniel Heck.
1. The presentation of SMiP and IOPS awards (Best Paper, Best Presentation, Best Poster).

## Keynote Presentation

**Speaker:** Prof. Dr. Daniel Heck (University of Marburg)
**Topic:** Cognitive and psychometric modeling of truth judgments

Prof. Heck addressed the challenge of formally modeling the repetition-based truth effect, noting that while the effect is well-replicated, formal statistical models are scarce. He outlined various cognitive and psychometric modeling approaches for truth judgments, emphasizing the benefits of precise mathematical equations over simulation-based models when testing theories about the plausibility of statements. Key discussion points included:

- The importance of model identifiability, auxiliary assumptions, and validation studies.
- How cultural-consensus models provide a psychometric solution for estimating both statement truth status and participant expertise, especially in settings like fact-checking where the actual truth status may be unknown.
