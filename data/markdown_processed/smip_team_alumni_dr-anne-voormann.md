# Dr. Anne Voormann

**Affiliation:** University of Freiburg, Experimental Psychology
**Current Role:** Postdoctoral Researcher
**Contact:** [anne.voormannpsychologie.uni-freiburg.de](mailto:anne.voormannpsychologie.uni-freiburg.de)

## Research Interests

Dr. Voormann's research focuses on human cognition, with specific interests in memory, perception, and visual attention. She investigates sequential effects during task performance and their application in human-machine interaction (HMI).

**Key Research Areas:**

- **Memory:** Researching the processes involved in recognition memory. This includes examining differences between multiple recognition decisions and simple recognition decisions using cognitive modeling. She also investigates the role of guessing and the importance of mis-detection in recognition processes.
- **Sequential Effects:** Focusing on common aspects and modeling techniques for sequential effects observed in various cognitive areas (e.g., visual attention, interference tasks, task-switching).
- **Human-Machine Interaction (HMI):** Applying fundamental knowledge of human cognition to HMI, specifically investigating the impact of vibration on cognitive performance.
