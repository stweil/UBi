# Niels Kukken

Niels Kukken is an alumnus of the SMiP Cohort 2017. He is currently preparing to submit his doctoral dissertation at the University of Tübingen.

## Akademische Betreuung

- **Primary Advisor:** [Prof. Dr. Mandy Hütter](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/team/researchers/prof-dr-mandy-huetter/) (University of Tübingen)
- **Additional Supervisors:** Prof. Dr. Andreas Voss, Prof. Dr. Rolf Ulrich

**Dissertation Proposal:** Dissociating the Effects of Associations and Implications in Evaluative Conditioning

## Forschungsbereiche

- Diffusion Model Analysis
- Interindividual Differences of Intraindividual Variation
- Mathematical Modeling of Cognitive Processes
- Personality

## Veröffentlichungen (Publications)

- Kukken, N., Hütter, M., & Holland, R. W. (2019). Are there two independent evaluative conditioning effects in relational paradigms? Dissociating the effects of CS-US pairings and their meaning. *Cognition & Emotion*. doi: [10.1080/02699931.2019.1617112 ](https://philpapers.org/rec/KUKATT)

## Vorträge (Talks)

- **Kukken, N., Holland, R. W., & Hütter, M.** (2018). Dissociating the effects of implications and pairings in a relational evaluative conditioning paradigm. Talk given at the European Social Cognition Network's Conference (ESCON), Cologne, Germany.
- **Kukken, N.** (2019, June). Are there two independent evaluative conditioning effects in relational paradigms? Dissociating the effects of CS-US pairings and their meaning. Talk given at the 34th IOPS/SMiP Summer Conference, Utrecht, Netherlands.
