# Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS)

The [Graduate School of Economic and Social Sciences (GESS)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/gess/) at the University of Mannheim offers graduate training in empirical and quantitative methods, applying these skills to business, economics, and the social sciences. The 11 full-time, three-to-five-year PhD programs provide access to world-class teaching and a vibrant, international faculty engaged in cutting-edge research. All programs emphasize achieving excellence in research within their specific discipline while encouraging the exchange of ideas, methods, and approaches across the boundaries of the economic and social sciences.
