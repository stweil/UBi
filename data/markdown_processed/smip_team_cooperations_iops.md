## Interuniversity Graduate School of Psychometrics and Sociometrics (IOPS)

We are pleased to cooperate with the [Interuniversity Graduate School of Psychometrics and Sociometrics (IOPS)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/team/cooperations/iops/). IOPS is an institute dedicated to advanced dissertation training in psychometrics and sociometrics, and it also coordinates high-quality research in this field.

**Participating Universities:**

- Leiden University
- University of Amsterdam (UvA)
- University of Groningen
- Twente University
- Tilburg University
- Utrecht University
- KU Leuven

**Participating Research Institutes:**

- CITO (National Institute of Educational Measurement)
- CBS (Statistics Netherlands)

**Program Scope:**
IOPS opens courses for SMiP members to participate in, and SMiP facilitates participation for IOPS members.
