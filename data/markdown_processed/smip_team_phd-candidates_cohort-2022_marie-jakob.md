# Marie Jakob

Marie Jakob is a PhD candidate whose research focuses on signal detection theory applied to discrimination judgments.

## 🧑‍🔬 Forschungsinformationen

- **Dissertationsthema:** Detecting Prototypical and Non-Prototypical Discrimination – A Signal Detection Approach.
- **Primärer Betreuer (Primary Advisor):** [Prof. Dr. Karl Christoph Klauer](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/team/researchers/prof-dr-karl-christoph-klauer/) (University of Freiburg)
- **Zusätzliche Betreuer (Additional Supervisors):** Mandy Hütter, Andreas Voss
