# Steven Bißantz

Steven Bißantz is a PhD candidate focusing on the application of advanced machine learning techniques to psychological assessment and the prediction of empirical findings replicability.

## 🧑‍🎓 Akademische Details

- **Primary Advisor:** [Prof. Dr. Eunike Wetzel](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/smip/team/researchers/prof-dr-eunike-wetzel/) (University of Kaiserslautern-Landau)
- **Additional Supervisors:** Beatrice Kuhlmann, Daniel Heck (external)
- **Dissertation Proposal:** Unraveling the Impact of Ad Hoc and Modified Scales on Direct Replications and Predicting Replicability of Empirical Findings with Advanced Machine Learning Techniques
