# Course Catalog for Exchange Students - Business School

For detailed information on the curriculum, please refer to the official catalog: [Course Catalog (Spring 2021)](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/studium/vom-ausland-nach-mannheim/austauschstudierende/kurse/vorlesungsverzeichnis-fs-2021/betriebswirtschaftslehre-bachelor-alle/)

## Eligibility Guidelines

**For Bachelor's Students:**
You are restricted to choosing courses only from the bachelor's level curriculum. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students, unless you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program when you arrive in Mannheim. For specific questions, please contact the Business School Exchange Team at [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).

**For Master's/MBA Students:**
You are eligible to select Business courses from all master's level courses. If your home university permits you to take selected bachelor's level courses, these are also available to you.

## Specialized Course Areas

### Area Accounting and Taxation

This area covers critical aspects of corporate finance and international tax law:

- Understanding the impact of tax codes on corporate decision-making.
- Analyzing tax regimes through illustrative examples from the Asia Pacific Region and Europe.
- Evaluating the consequences of taxation in cross-border transactions.
- Learning the basic principles of international taxation.
- Identifying key tax issues affecting the tax burden of international investments.
- Analyzing basic tax planning structures related to financing strategies and holding companies.
- Understanding the BEPS (base erosion and profit shifting) debate and how major corporations achieve low tax rates.
- Reviewing current and planned international responses to aggressive tax planning.

### Area Finance

This course is based on the textbook **„Principles of Corporate Finance“ by Richard A. Brealey, Stewart C. Myers and Franklin Allen, 12th edition, published by McGraw-Hill 2016.**

- **Materials:** All lecture slides and exercise materials are available for download on Ilias after registration via the student portal.
- **Self-Study:** The textbook includes practice questions and quizzes. Students may also use previous editions (11th/2013 or 10th/2011), though these are less ideal.

### Area Information Systems

This area focuses on modeling and managing complex business processes using integrated information systems:

- Modeling complex business processes using popular techniques.
- Discussing the requirements, characteristics, and effects of integrated information systems in industrial companies, including complex process interdependencies.
- Completing basic tasks across various functional areas within a wide-spread integrated information system.
- **Functional Areas Covered:**
  - Business Process Modelling
  - Research and Development
  - Marketing and Sales
  - Procurement and Warehousing
  - Production
  - Shipping and Customer Service
  - Finance, Accounting, HR
  - Planning and Control Systems

### Area Management

This area provides comprehensive coverage of Human Resource Management and organizational development:

- Human Resource Environment
- Job Analysis & Job Design
- HR Planning & Recruiting
- Selection
- Training
- Performance Management
- Employee Development
- Separation & Retention
- Compensation and Incentives (including International HRM)
- High-Performance Organizations

## Cross-Disciplinary & Advanced Topics (Skills Development)

The following topics are covered across multiple advanced areas, focusing on research methodology and global challenges:

**Research Methodology:**

- **Literature Reviews:** How to conduct a rigorous and systematic review of the literature.
- **Research Methods:** A discussion of alternate research methods, covering:
  - **Qualitative Research:** Conducting qualitative studies and key readings.
  - **Surveys:** Using surveys in research and key readings.
  - **Experiments:** Using experiments in research and key readings.
- **Analysis & Presentation:** Further resources and examples are provided for applying each method.

**Global & Ethical Case Studies:**

- What is the state of Science regarding Environmental Change?
- What are the effects of Environmental Change on Business?
- Analysis of case studies across three dimensions:
  - Moral case
  - Legal case
  - Business case
