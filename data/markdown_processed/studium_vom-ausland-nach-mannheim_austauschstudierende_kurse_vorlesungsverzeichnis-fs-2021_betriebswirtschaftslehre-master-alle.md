# Course Catalog for Exchange Students (Business School)

## Eligibility Guidelines

**For Bachelor's Students:**
You are restricted to choosing business courses only from the bachelor's level curriculum. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students. Exceptions are only considered if you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program when you arrive in Mannheim. For specific questions, please contact the Business School Exchange Team at [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).

**For Master's/MBA Students:**
You are eligible to select business courses from all master's level offerings. If your home university permits you to take selected bachelor's level courses, these are also available to you.

## Available Course Areas

The Business School offers courses across several specialized areas:

- Area Accounting and Taxation
- Area Finance
- Area Information Systems
- Area Management
- Area Marketing
- Area Operations Management

## Detailed Course Information

For a comprehensive overview of all modules, please consult the detailed course catalog: [Modulkatalog MMM | Universität Mannheim](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/modulkatalog-mmm/is-540-management-of-enterprise-systems/)

### Information Systems (IS) Courses (IS 613 & IS 615)

These courses have limited capacity and require a formal application.

**Application Details:**

- **Application Tool:** Applications must be submitted via the chair's [application tool](https://wifo1-52.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/cvportal/#/upload).
- **Access:** The tool is accessible only within the university network or via [VPN](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/it/services/internet-and-servers/vpn/).
- **Combined Application:** If applying for both IS 613 and IS 615, one application is sufficient; ensure you indicate interest in both courses.
- **Application Period:** 01.08.23 – 01.09.23
- **Required Documents:**
  - A short cover letter (1–2 paragraphs) detailing your motivation and previous experience in software development.
  - Curriculum Vitae and study results (transcript of records).
- **Timeline:**
  - Confirmations will be issued on Monday, 04 September 2023.
  - Cancellations are possible until 10 September 2023.
