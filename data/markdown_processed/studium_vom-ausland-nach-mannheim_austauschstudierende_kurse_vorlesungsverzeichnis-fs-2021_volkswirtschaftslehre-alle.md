# Economics Course Selection for Exchange Students

This guide outlines the rules and procedures for exchange students enrolling in Economics courses at the University of Mannheim.

## Course Selection Guidelines by Academic Level

The rules for course selection differ significantly between Bachelor's and Master's students.

### 🎓 Bachelor's Students

- **Scope:** You must select your economics courses exclusively from the available bachelor's level modules.
- **Restriction:** Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students.
- **Prerequisites:** Exchange students from other departments must confirm they meet all necessary prerequisites.
- **Seminars:** Bachelor's level seminars are typically restricted to enrolled economics students.

### 🎓 Master's Students

- **Scope:** You have greater flexibility and can choose elective modules freely, provided you fulfill the prerequisites for each course.
- **Restriction:** Core modules (E601–603 and E700–703) are generally not open to exchange students.
- **Exception:** You may attend bachelor's level courses if your home university grants explicit permission.

## Example Course Topics

The department offers advanced modules covering various quantitative and computational topics, including:

- **Text Processing:** Regular expressions/regex, text parsing, parts-of-speech tagging, and web-scraping.
- **Text as Data:** Word-embeddings (from bag-of-words to word2vec/GloVe), topic models, sentiment analysis, and text-similarity measures.
- **Machine Learning with Text (in Python):** ML fundamentals, BERT implementation, and text-classification.

*Note: Course content is illustrated using recent applications from economics and management science literature.*
