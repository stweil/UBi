# Course Catalog for Exchange Students (Business School)

This catalog outlines available courses for exchange students. For specific questions regarding course selection, please contact the Business School Exchange Team at [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).

## Eligibility Guidelines

**For Bachelor's Students:**
You are restricted to choosing courses only from the bachelor's level curriculum. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students, unless you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program at the time of arrival in Mannheim.

**For Master's/MBA Students:**
You can select Business courses from all available master's level courses. If your home university permits, you are also welcome to take selected bachelor's level courses.

### 💰 Area Finance

- **Research Methodology:**
  - **Literature Reviews:** How to conduct a rigorous and systematic review of the literature.
  - **Research Methods:** Discussion of alternate research methods, covering what the method entails, how it is used, exemplars of use, and key issues for the researcher. This includes:
    - Qualitative research (conducting studies, key readings).
    - Surveys (using surveys, key readings).
    - Experiments (using experiments, key readings).
  - **Analysis & Presentation of Results:** Further resources and examples are provided for applying each method.
  - **Thematic Focus:** State of Science regarding Environmental Change, and the effects of Environmental Change on Business.
  - **Case Studies:** Moral case, legal case, and business case analysis.

### 💻 Area Information Systems

This area focuses on modeling complex business processes and understanding integrated information systems within industrial companies.

- **Core Skills:**
  - Modeling complex business processes using popular techniques.
  - Discussing the requirements, characteristics, and effects of integrated information systems, including complex process interdependencies.
  - Completing basic tasks across various functional areas within a widespread integrated information system.
- **Functional Modules Covered:**
  - Business Process Modelling
  - Application Systems in Research and Development
  - Marketing and Sales
  - Procurement and Warehousing
  - Production
  - Shipping and Customer Service
  - Finance, Accounting, HR
  - Planning and Control Systems

### 💼 Area Management

This area emphasizes sustainability, corporate responsibility, and organizational development.

- **Key Topics & Readings:**
  - **Sustainability & Ethics:** Modules covering the ecological crisis, the role of business in an eco-friendlier future, and co-creation of a better world.
  - **Organizational Focus:** Human Resource Environment, Job Analysis & Job Design, HR Planning & Recruiting, Selection, Training, Performance Management, Employee Development, Separation & Retention, Compensation, Incentives + International HRM, and High-Performance Organizations.
  - **Academic References:** Includes seminal works on corporate value and sustainability (e.g., Porter & Kramer, Hart & Milstein, Orsato).
  - **Standards:** The SBTi Corporate Net Zero Standard (03–2024).

### 📣 Area Marketing

This area focuses on developing and managing superior marketing strategies.

- **Core Learning Objectives:**
  - Understanding the fundamental principles of developing and managing marketing strategies.
  - Explaining the critical importance of marketing strategy for superior business performance.
  - Critically evaluating marketing strategy arguments (e.g., strategy follows structure vs. vice versa).
  - Solving complex marketing strategy problems, such as determining the most valuable customer segments.
  - Evaluating empirical and theoretical marketing strategy research from various sources.
- **Key Texts:** Includes foundational texts on brand management (e.g., Keller, Aaker, Kapferer).
