# Course Catalog Guidance for Exchange Students (Business School)

This guide outlines which courses are suitable for exchange students based on their current academic level.

## Eligibility by Student Level

**For Bachelor's Students:**
You are restricted to choosing courses only from the bachelor's level curriculum. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students. Exceptions are only considered if you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program when you arrive in Mannheim.
*For specific questions, please contact the Business School Exchange Team at [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).*

**For Master's/MBA Students:**
You are eligible to select Business courses from all master's level courses. If your home university permits you to take selected bachelor's level courses, these are also available to you.

## Available Course Areas

The Business School offers courses across several specialized areas:

- Area Accounting and Taxation
- Area Finance
- Area Information Systems
- Area Management
- Area Marketing
- Area Operations Management

### Detailed Course Information

**Information Systems (IS)**
For a detailed course description, please consult the [Modulkatalog MMM | Universität Mannheim](https://www.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/modulkatalog-mmm/is-540-management-of-enterprise-systems/).

- **Note:** This course has limited capacity. Applications must be submitted via the chair's [application tool](https://wi1vm-web.bwl.uni-mannheim.de/cvportal/#/upload). This tool is accessible only within the university network or via [VPN](https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/it/services/internet-and-servers/vpn/).
- If applying for IS 613 and IS 615, a single application is sufficient; however, you must indicate that you are applying for both courses. Remember to also register via Portal2.

**Marketing**
All lectures for this module will be uploaded as recordings to [ILIAS](https://ilias.uni-mannheim.de/ilias.php?baseClass=ilrepositorygui&ref_id=1491552).
For scheduling information regarding lectures and exercise sessions, please visit the ILIAS group, select "Folders" and "Dateien," then "Lecture Slides and Recordings," followed by "Lecture 00 Organizational Details," and finally the PDF file "Lecture 0."
