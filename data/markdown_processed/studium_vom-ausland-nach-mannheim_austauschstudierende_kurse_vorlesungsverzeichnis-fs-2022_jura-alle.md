# Academic Course Catalog: Law Studies (Spring 2022)

This document consolidates the curriculum offerings for law students, covering comparative law, German private law, EU law, and emerging digital legal fields.

## Comparative Law

This course provides an accessible overview of Comparative Law, focusing specifically on **European Legal Traditions**. It examines the similarities and differences between legal systems, which drives European harmonization.

**Key Components:**

- **Comparative Law Methodology:** Aims, tools, and methods of comparative legal research.
- **Legal Concepts:** Basic concepts of contract law (party autonomy, formation, performance) and business law (division of work, liability, tort law, insurance).
- **Legal Systems:** Analysis of how common concepts are governed by different legal families, including the study of legal hybrids and transplants.

## German Private Law (Zivilrecht)

This course offers a comprehensive survey of German private law, covering general rules, commercial law, and civil procedure. The German Civil Code (BGB) is central to understanding the system.

**Core Areas Covered:**

- **Structure:** Definition and historical development of private law; the structure of the BGB.
- **Key Legal Fields:**
  - Law of Obligations (Contracts, Torts, and Unjust Enrichment).
  - Property Law.
  - Law of Succession and Company Law (including partnerships and corporations).
- **Context:** Understanding the influence of European Union (EU) law and the general principles of interpretation.

## European Union Law & Market Freedoms

This module explores the impact of EU law on private and corporate entities operating across member states.

**Key Topics:**

- **Market Freedoms:** A systematic survey of the free movement of goods, persons, services, and capital, analyzed through the jurisprudence of the European Court of Justice.
- **EU Law Impact:** Understanding how EU law grants rights and imposes obligations across national borders.
- **Taxation:** Examination of EU Tax Law, covering the harmonization of indirect and direct taxes, with a focus on fundamental freedoms.
- **Competition Law:** Core elements of EU competition law, including horizontal and vertical restraints, market definitions, and merger control.

## Digital Law and Platform Regulation

This section addresses the legal challenges posed by the internet and digital platforms.

**Key Themes:**

- **E-Commerce & Private Law:** Legal issues in online commerce, including contract formation, standard terms, consumer protection, and online auctions.
- **Intellectual Property:** Copyright restrictions for content presentation (file sharing, streaming) and domain name system implications for trademark law.
- **Platform Governance:** Analyzing regulatory approaches, including risk-based regulation, content moderation, and the balance between free expression and platform responsibility.
- **Specific Issues:** Algorithmic bias, systemic risks of AI, and regulatory pitfalls for intermediaries.

## International Law and Dispute Resolution

This area covers cross-border legal disputes, both in state courts and through arbitration.

**Topics Covered:**

- **International Criminal Law:** Survey of foundational texts and principles (e.g., works by Cassese, Cryer, Schabas).
- **Cross-Border Litigation:** Procedures for litigation before state courts, including jurisdiction (Brussels I Regulation), provisional measures, and evidence access.
- **Arbitration Law:** Overview of arbitration as a dispute resolution mechanism, including drafting agreements, common rules (ICC, LCIA), and enforcement via the New York Convention.
