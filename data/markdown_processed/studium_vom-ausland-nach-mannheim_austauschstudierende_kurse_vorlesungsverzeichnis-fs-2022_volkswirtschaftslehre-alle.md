# Economics Courses for Exchange Students

This guide outlines important information regarding the selection of economics courses for exchange students at the University of Mannheim, differentiating requirements for Bachelor's and Master's students.

## Course Selection Guidelines

### For Bachelor's Students

- **Scope:** You must select courses exclusively from the Bachelor's level curriculum. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students.
- **Prerequisites:** Exchange students from departments other than Economics may only attend lectures if they meet the necessary prerequisites.
- **Seminars:** Bachelor's level seminars are typically restricted to enrolled Economics students.

### For Master's Students

- **Scope:** You have the freedom to choose elective modules available within the department, provided you fulfill the prerequisites for each course.
- **Restrictions:** Core modules (E601–603 and E700–703) are generally not open to exchange students.
- **Exception:** You may attend Bachelor's level courses if your home university grants explicit permission.

## Example Course Topics

The department covers a range of advanced topics, illustrated by examples such as:

- **Text Processing:** Regular expressions/regex, text parsing, parts-of-speech tagging, web-scraping, and dictionaries.
- **Text as Data:** Word-embeddings (from bag-of-words to word2vec/GloVe), topic models, sentiment analysis, and text-similarity.
- **Machine Learning with Text (in Python):** ML fundamentals, BERT, and text-classification.

*Note: Course content is illustrated using recent applications from economics and management science literature.*
