# Course Catalog for Exchange Students (Business School)

This guide outlines the rules for selecting suitable courses for exchange students at the Business School.

## Course Selection Guidelines

The appropriate course selection depends on your current academic level:

### 🎓 For Bachelor's Students

- You are restricted to choosing courses only from the **bachelor's level** catalog.
- Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students.
- **Exception:** An exception may be made if you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program when you arrive in Mannheim.
- **Inquiries:** Please contact the Business School Exchange Team via [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).

### 🎓 For Master's / MBA Students

- You are eligible to choose Business courses from **all master's level courses**.
- If your home university permits, you may also select specific bachelor's level courses.

## Available Subject Areas

The Business School offers courses across the following specialized areas:

- Accounting and Taxation
- Finance
- Information Systems
- Management
- Marketing
- Operations Management
