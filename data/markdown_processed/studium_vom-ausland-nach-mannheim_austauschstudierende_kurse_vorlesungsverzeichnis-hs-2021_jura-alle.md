# Academic Course Catalog Overview

This document consolidates descriptions for various advanced law courses, covering comparative, national, international, and specialized legal fields.

## ⚖️ Comparative Law II: Common vs. Civil Law Systems

This course compares legal systems, focusing on the divide between Common Law (e.g., Australia/US) and Civil Law (e.g., Germany). It details methodological differences and treats core business subjects like contract formation, agency, and interpretation in depth.

**Key Topics Covered:**

- Basic features, tools, and methods of comparative law.
- Development and current status of Common Law and Civil Law as legal families.
- The role and education of lawyers, judges, and juries.
- Legal reasoning, statutory construction, and judicial precedents.
- Comparative assessment of key legal institutions.

## 🇩🇪 German Private Law (BGB Focus)

An introduction to German private law, essential for foreign law students. The course begins with the legal sources of German law, emphasizing the Civil Code (BGB) and its history. The methodology is clarified through systematic descriptions and case solutions in core areas.

**Structure:**

- Definition and historical development of private law.
- Structure of the BGB.
- Tort Law (Deliktsrecht).
- Law of Obligations (Schuldrecht).
- Property Law (Sachenrecht).
- Overview of Inheritance, Family, Company, and Civil Procedure Law.

## 🌐 International Law and Global Governance

This course examines the role of international organizations (e.g., WTO, UN, OECD) as global governance actors. It analyzes how these bodies regulate trade and business at regional and universal levels, moving beyond mere negotiation forums.

**Key Learning Outcomes:**

- Economic relevance and structure of international organizations.
- International organizations as law-makers and standard-setters.
- Monitoring and dispute settlement functions.
- Interaction between international, EU, and national legal norms.

## 🏛️ German Constitutional Law (Grundgesetz)

A general introduction to German Constitutional Law, focusing on the *Grundgesetz* (Basic Law). The course covers the structure, fundamental rights (Art. 1–19), and core principles of the Federal Republic.

**Contents:**

- The German legal system and Constitutional Law.
- The *Grundgesetz* and its relationship with the European Union.
- Fundamental Rights and Constitutional Principles.
- The Federal State structure, constitutional organs, and legislative procedure.

## 💰 Law and Economics

This course applies economic concepts and methods to legal problems, providing a behavioral theory to predict how people respond to laws.

**Core Areas of Application:**

- **Foundations:** Utility maximization and efficiency (Pareto/Kaldor-Hicks).
- **Property Law:** Necessity of property rights (Coase Theorem, Tragedy of the Commons).
- **Tort Law:** Comparing negligence vs. strict liability.
- **Contract Law:** Minimizing transaction costs and avoiding market failures (e.g., Market for Lemons).

## 🛡️ Intellectual Property Law

This course provides an overview of intellectual property (IP) as a tool for innovation and global competitiveness. Students examine the multi-level IP protection system (TRIPS, WIPO, EU, National).

**Key Topics:**

- Legal concepts: Patent protection, utility models, trademarks, design, and copyright.
- The impact of competition law on IP protection.
- Contractual exploitation and enforcement strategies for IPRs.
- Co-existence of national and Community IP-rights.

## 💼 International Business Transactions Law

Focuses on the legal and commercial background of cross-border transactions. The course provides an initial understanding of practical legal advice in international trade.

**Key Areas:**

- Pitfalls and legal frameworks of cross-border transactions.
- The role of contracts and international conventions (e.g., CISG).
- Financing, dispute resolution, and enforcement of international awards.

## 🇪🇺 Regulatory Law and EU Law

This course examines regulatory law, which aims to create competition in formerly monopolistic infrastructure markets (e.g., energy, telecommunications). It strongly integrates EU law principles.

**Focus Areas:**

- Basics of regulatory law, EU competences, and legal limits.
- Specific regulation of energy and telecommunications law, including digital networks.
- Analysis of EU legislation and case law from the European Court of Justice (ECJ).

## 🗣️ Legal Advocacy and Communication Skills

This advanced course focuses on the art and science of communication for lawyers. It develops skills necessary for courtroom performance, professional speaking, and persuasive writing.

**Skills Developed:**

- Structuring persuasive oral and written presentations.
- Negotiation psychology (competitive and cooperative).
- Active listening and interrogating witnesses.
- Crisis communication strategies.
