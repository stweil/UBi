# Economics Course Selection for Exchange Students

This guide outlines the rules for selecting economics courses for exchange students at the University of Mannheim, differentiating between Bachelor's and Master's levels.

## 🎓 Course Selection Guidelines

### For Bachelor's Students

- **Scope:** You must select courses exclusively from the Bachelor's level curriculum.
- **Restriction:** Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students.
- **Prerequisites:** Exchange students from other departments may only attend economics lectures if they meet the required prerequisites.
- **Seminars:** Bachelor's level seminars are typically restricted to enrolled economics students.

### For Master's Students

- **Scope:** You have the freedom to choose elective modules available within the department, provided you fulfill the prerequisites for each course.
- **Restrictions:** Core modules (E601–603 and E700–703) are generally not open to exchange students.
- **Exception:** You may attend Bachelor's level courses if your home university grants explicit permission.
