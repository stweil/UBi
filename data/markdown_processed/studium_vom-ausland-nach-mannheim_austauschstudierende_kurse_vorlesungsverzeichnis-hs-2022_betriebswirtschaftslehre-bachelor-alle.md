# Course Catalog for Exchange Students - Business School Mannheim

## Course Selection Guidelines

**For Bachelor's Students:**
You should select your business courses exclusively from the bachelor's level courses. Master's level courses are generally not open to undergraduate students. Exceptions are only considered if you are in your 4th year of an undergraduate program when you arrive in Mannheim.
*For questions, please contact the Business School Exchange Team via [incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de](mailto:incoming.business@uni-mannheim.de).*

**For Master's/MBA Students:**
You are eligible to choose Business courses from all master's level courses. If your home university permits you to take selected bachelor's level courses, these are also available to you.

## Course Areas

### Area Finance

- **Proficiency in Financial Products:** Ability to describe the functioning of financial markets and identify the roles and interactions of market players. Ability to use financial market data to implement financial strategies.
- **Investment Management:** Successfully building and managing a portfolio of financial products. Ability to identify and measure financial risk and make investment decisions.
  - Session 1: Introduction to Digital Assets as an Alternate Asset Class
  - Session 2: Behavioural Biases and IPS
  - Session 3: Prepare Investment Policy Statement (IPS) – case study (e-learning)
  - Session 4: Valuation of Digital Assets
  - Session 5: Fundamental of Programming using Python (e-learning)
  - Session 6: Risk and Return of Portfolio with Digital Assets: Theory
  - Session 7: Risk and Return of Portfolio with Digital Assets: Practice using Python
  - Session 8: Technical Indicators and Trading Strategies: Theory
  - Session 9: Technical Analysis: Practice using Python
  - Session 10: Capstone Project
