# University Wide Electives & Academic Programs

This section details advanced elective courses available to exchange students, covering specialized topics in law, technology, and political science.

## 🏛️ Digital Assets Regulation (BA/MA Level)

This course addresses the rapidly growing legal landscape surrounding "digital assets." It aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of digital assets, methods for protecting their rights under current legislation, and the concept of digital ownership.

**Key Topics Covered:**

- **Digital Assets:** Notion, types, and the current legal landscape.
- **Digital Ownership:** Examination of European and American perspectives on ownership.
- **Data Protection:** Basics of privacy law, covering Big Data, Internet of Things (IoT), and the link between data and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- **Data Ownership:** Legal regulation of personal and machine-generated data, and the correlation between data and digital ownership.
- **Crypto-Assets:** Legal regulation of cryptocurrencies and NFTs, including transfer and connection to intellectual property rights.
- **Online Accounts & Inheritance:** Discussion of social media and online gaming accounts from a digital ownership perspective, and instruments for defining the post-mortal fate of digital assets (digital inheritance).

**Learning Outcomes:** Students gain a deep understanding of the digital assets concept and advanced approaches to protecting digital rights.
**Examination:** Essay

**Lecturer:** Prof. Dr. Kateryna Nekit
*Expertise:* Digital property and the role of data in the digital age.

## 🇩🇪 German Constitutional Law (BA/MA Level)

This course offers a general introduction to German Constitutional Law, covering the constitution, its background, contents, and methods for interpreting constitutional legal texts.

**Core Focus:** The German *Grundgesetz* (Basic Law), which serves as the formal constitutional document of the Federal Republic of Germany. The course places particular emphasis on the fundamental rights provisions (Art. 1–19).

**Contents:**

- The German legal system and Constitutional Law.
- The *Grundgesetz* and its relationship with the European Union.
- Fundamental Rights and Fundamental Constitutional Principles.
- The Federal State structure, constitutional organs, and the legislative procedure.
- Public administration and the judicial system.

**Learning Target:** Students will be able to understand the basic principles of the *Grundgesetz*, solve simple cases regarding fundamental rights, and compare German legal structures with other legal systems.
**Examination:** Oral Exam
**Lecturer:** Katharina Longin

## 🌐 International Law and Litigation (BA/MA Level)

This course provides an overview of international law, focusing on procedural aspects and comparative legal frameworks.

**Key Topics Covered:**

- Concept and practical relevance of international litigation.
- Sources of international procedural law.
- International jurisdiction and coordination between different jurisdictions.
- Conduct of proceedings, including the recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments.

**Recommended Literature:**

- Fentiman, Richard: *International Commercial Litigation* (2nd edition, Oxford 2015).
- Hartley, Trevor C.: *International Commercial Litigation* (3rd edition, Cambridge 2020).
- Junker, Abbo: *Internationales Zivilprozessrecht* (6th edition, München 2023).
