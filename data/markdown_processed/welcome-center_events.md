# Events at the Welcome Center

Discover upcoming workshops, tours, and social gatherings designed for the international community at the University of Mannheim.

**Working Across Cultures: An Intercultural Workshop for International Researchers**
Join this valuable intercultural workshop scheduled for April 24th to gain insights for a successful research experience in Germany.

**Guided City Tour**
Explore Mannheim with a unique guided tour that brings history, innovation, and culture to life.

**Summer BBQ**
Save the Date! Join us for the Summer BBQ event for international researchers.

Learn about Germany’s famous beer production during a behind-the-scenes brewery tour. Discover brewing traditions, local flavors, and the cultural role of beer in Germany.

**Winter Celebration**
Looking for community and warmth? Join us for our Winter Celebration event.
